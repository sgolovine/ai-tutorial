**What you'll get from this page:** a repeatable, ethical outreach process for CONAFROEDU 2026. You'll **find** relevant institutions and people with AI, **verify** them, **personalize** a short message, **send it yourself**, and **follow up once**. It includes target lists with real starting points, prompts, **ready-to-adapt templates in Brazilian Portuguese** (email and WhatsApp), a follow-up calendar tied to the real deadlines, and the privacy and anti-spam rules that keep the committee's reputation intact.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The goal is **10–20 excellent, relevant conversations**, not 1,000 cold messages. One NEABI coordinator who forwards your call to their research group is worth more than a mass email nobody reads. And mass email can get your Gmail flagged as spam a week before the deadline.

## Timing: two outreach windows, two different asks

CONAFROEDU has two funnels with different deadlines, so your outreach must change on **5 November**.

| Window | Main ask | Who to prioritize | Real deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Now → 4 Nov** (2 weeks) | "Please share our **call for papers** (chamada de trabalhos) with researchers and teachers who document their practice" | NEABs/NEABIs, education PPGs, ANPEd GT21 route, ABPN/CONNEABS, research groups | Submissions close **4 Nov 2026** (time: confirm with the committee) |
| **5 Nov → 4 Dec** | "Please share an invitation to **participate** (attend) in an online congress on anti-racist education" | Teacher unions and networks, school managers, secretarias' ERER coordinators, licenciatura courses, Black education collectives, media | Price tier changes **16 Nov**; **20 Nov** Consciência Negra; programme by **25 Nov**; event **5–6 Dec** |

> [!WARNING]
> After 4 November, don't keep asking partners to circulate the call for papers. That only changes if the committee **officially** extends the deadline and Even3 is updated. Asking people to share outdated information damages the relationship.

**Media lead time:** if you want coverage around **20 November**, pitch by **6–10 November**. Editors plan ahead, and many educators are busy with their own Consciência Negra activities that week.

> Start with **warm** contacts: the institutions of confirmed speakers and of the scientific committee, and NEABs where someone on the team already knows a person. A message that begins "A professora X, que vai falar no congresso, sugeriu…" (only if true!) gets read.

## The process, step by step

### Step 1: Set a small weekly target

Example for the week of 21–27 October: **10 NEABs/NEABIs + 5 PPGs**, all contacted by 27 October. One volunteer, about 3 hours total, split into research (1 h), verification (1 h) and drafting/sending (1 h).

### Step 2: Find candidates with AI

Use a search-first assistant with citations. **Perplexity Free** works well, as do Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude with web search. Start from directories that actually exist, such as CONNEABS, rather than asking AI to "list all NEABs" from memory.

```prompt
Find up to 10 NEABs/NEABIs or anti-racist education research groups at Brazilian universities or federal institutes, using the ABPN/CONNEABS directory (https://abpn.org.br/conneabs-2/) and the institutions' own official websites.
For each: institution, campus/state, exact unit name, one relevant ERER or teacher-education activity, exact source URL, date of the source, and the PUBLISHED institutional contact (email or contact form).
Open each source; do not invent emails. Separate confirmed current contacts from old directory entries or inaccessible pages.
Do not collect personal contacts of members or students.
Purpose: evaluate an institutional invitation to share CONAFROEDU 2026 (online, 5–6/12/2026). Do not assume any partnership. Answer in Portuguese, as a table.
```

> [!NOTE]
> **Lattes (CNPq)** is a CV platform. It is useful for *discovering* who researches ERER and Africanidades, but it is **not a mailing list**. Don't scrape CVs or copy personal emails from them. Contact people through institutional routes. ([CNPq platform manuals](https://www.gov.br/cnpq/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/manuais-de-uso-das-plataformas-do-cnpq/manuais-de-uso-das-plataformas-do-cnpq))

### Step 3: Verify every lead (human, 3–5 minutes each)

1. **Open the source.** Does the page exist? Is it the institution's official site?
2. **Is it current?** Look for 2025–2026 activity. Old coordinators change.
3. **Is the contact institutional?** Department or NEAB email, or a contact form. Avoid personal emails found on third-party sites.
4. **Is there a real fit?** Write one sentence: why would *this* group care about CONAFROEDU?
5. **Do they accept external dissemination?** Some lists or pages say so. If unclear, your message asks politely.

For the opposite problem (too much material about a speaker or partner), **Gemini Notebook** (formerly NotebookLM, free) can answer questions only from the sources you upload, with citations ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16206563?hl=pt-BR)).

### Step 4: Record it in the outreach tracker

A shared sheet with one row per **institution** (not per person):

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| Institution / unit | NEAB – [Universidade], campus [X] |
| State | SP |
| Why relevant (1 sentence) | Teacher-education course on Lei 10.639 in 2026 |
| Source URL + date checked | link · 22/10 |
| Institutional contact + route | neab@… · email |
| Contact verified? | yes / no |
| Ask | share call for papers / share attendance invitation |
| Sent on / by | 23/10 · Ana |
| Follow-up on | 30/10 |
| Response | shared / declined / no answer |
| Opt-out / do not contact | ☐ |
| Tracking link used | utm_source=parceiro_neabi_01 |

### Step 5: Draft with AI, one message at a time

Give the assistant **only verified context** and the approved fact sheet. Ask for **one** message per institution.

```prompt
Write an email draft in Brazilian Portuguese to [INSTITUTION / UNIT], max 120 words.
Verified context: [ACTIVITY], source [URL], checked on [DATE].
Real request: [share the call for papers with your list / share an attendance invitation / talk about a possible collaboration].
Approved event facts: [FACT SHEET].
Make a specific and honest connection between the institution's work and the congress. Do not invent prior relationships, support, discounts, sponsorship or the recipient's interest. Be transparent that registration is paid and that publication has a separate fee. End with one simple question and offer not to contact them again. Do not send; give me the draft for review.
```

### Step 6: Human review checklist (before every send)

- [ ] Correct name of the institution, unit and campus (NEAB ≠ NEABI)
- [ ] The "why you" sentence is true and specific
- [ ] No invented partnership, endorsement, discount or "a pedido de…"
- [ ] Facts match the fact sheet: 5–6 Dec 2026, 100% online, Even3 link, deadline 4 Nov (if before), registration is paid, publication **R$ 16,98 per work**
- [ ] Certificate described carefully ("certificado de 40 horas, conforme critérios da organização"), with no promise of credit or progression
- [ ] No "Qualis", "MEC recognition" or "últimas vagas" claims
- [ ] Opt-out sentence present
- [ ] One link only (with your tracking parameters, tested)

### Step 7: Send it yourself, the right way

- Send from the **committee's email** (`conapsuporte@gmail.com`) or an institutional address, **one recipient per email**. No big CC lists, and no BCC blasts.
- Use a clear subject: `Chamada de trabalhos | CONAFROEDU 2026 | submissões até 04/11`.
- On WhatsApp Business, contact only people who initiated the conversation or expressly authorized this kind of message from the committee. A public number, saved contact or acquaintance is not permission. Otherwise use the appropriate institutional email/form to ask about sharing.
- Log it in the tracker immediately.

### Step 8: Follow up once, then close the loop

See the cadence below. If they say yes, thank them, send the kit and report back later (for example, "your network brought X registrations", if you can measure it). If they say no or ask not to be contacted, mark **do not contact** and respect it permanently.

## Follow-up cadence

| Day | Action | Example dates for the call for papers |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 0** | First personalized message | 22–27 Oct |
| **Day 5–7** | **One** short, polite follow-up, only if no reply | 29 Oct–2 Nov |
| **After that** | Stop. Mark "no answer". Don't add them to any list. | — |
| **If they reply yes** | Thank them the same day; send the kit; agree a date | — |
| **5 Nov** | If they shared the call: thank them, and ask (once) whether they'd also share the attendance invitation | 5–9 Nov |
| **After the event** | Thank partners, share the real results and proceedings link when it exists (planned by 15 Dec) | 7–18 Dec |

A single follow-up after about a week is good manners, not a legal safe harbour. Some contexts (a busy coordinator, a formal association) call for **no** follow-up at all.

**WhatsApp condition for every template and follow-up:** use only in a recipient-initiated conversation or after recorded permission from another channel. In particular, the group-admin permission request is not an exemption for cold WhatsApp contact. A single follow-up still requires that permission.

## LGPD and anti-spam: the ethics of outreach

Brazil's **LGPD** (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, [Lei 13.709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)) applies to the contact data you collect, including the public email of a coordinator. The following is practical guidance, not legal advice:

- **Purpose and minimum data.** Collect only what you need to make a relevant institutional contact: name of unit, institutional email, source. No CPFs, no personal phone numbers scraped from the web.
- **A legal basis, honestly assessed.** Consent is one basis. *Legitimate interest* is another, but ANPD (the data protection authority) says it requires a balancing test and is **not blanket permission for unlimited outreach** ([ANPD guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia_orientativo_hipoteses_legais_tratamento_de_dados_pessoais_legitimo_interesse)). A single, relevant institutional message is very different from a marketing sequence.
- **Published ≠ free to reuse.** An email identifying a person is personal data; a generic unit mailbox is not automatically personal data. Use it for the purpose for which it was published (contacting the unit), not to add to newsletters.
- **Opt-out, always.** Every message offers it, and you honour it permanently. Keep a small "do not contact" list.
- **Don't share lists.** Never give your tracker or registrant data to partners or sponsors.
- **AI tools and data.** Don't paste contact lists or registrant exports into AI assistants. Draft messages with the institution's public information only.
- **Retention.** Delete prospecting data no longer needed. Keep only a restricted minimum suppression identifier needed to honor do-not-contact requests, with retention period/legal basis set by the responsible organizer. Do not keep whole conversations indefinitely.

**Platform rules:**

- **WhatsApp:** businesses must have the person's **opt-in** before messaging them, and must honour opt-outs. Unofficial bulk senders and purchased lists can get your number banned ([WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)). Cold-messaging people you found in a group is not acceptable.
- **Facebook/Instagram:** no mass DMs, no scraping, no fake accounts ([Meta on scraping](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/04/how-we-combat-scraping/)). See [Computer-Use & Browser Agents](computer-use-agents.md#what-not-to-automate).
- **Email:** individual, relevant, identified sender, easy opt-out. Gmail and Outlook filter mass identical emails, and recipients can mark you as spam.

> [!CAUTION]
> Representation matters in outreach too. Don't ask Black colleagues for unpaid labour "for the cause", don't treat quilombola or religious communities as audiences to be harvested, and don't name anyone as a supporter without their written agreement. See [Representing Black & African Cultures Respectfully with AI](representation-bias.md) and [Ethics, Policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

## Measuring which partnerships work

- **Tracking links per partner** (UTM parameters). They tell Even3 or Google Analytics where a visit came from. Example (test that it keeps the registration path working before you share it):
  `https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/?utm_source=parceiro_neabi_01&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=conafroedu_2026_submissoes&utm_content=release`
  Use institution codes, never personal names or emails, in links.
- **Coupons:** Even3 supports discount coupons by amount or percentage, with limits and expiry ([Even3 help](https://ajuda.even3.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/212182786-Criar-cupons-de-desconto)). A coupon like `NEABPARCEIRO` is only an idea. Offer one **only if the committee approves** the discount and its cost.
- **Tracker results:** count who shared, which shares brought clicks, and what people said. Review weekly. See [Measuring Results for Free](measuring-results.md).
