## AI workflow

1. **Paste the fact sheet first, every time.** An AI assistant has never heard of CONAFROEDU, so without facts it invents a venue, a deadline or "certificado reconhecido pelo MEC". Start every chat with the [CONAFROEDU context block](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block) and the [brand voice paragraph](prompting-basics.md#your-brand-voice-in-one-paragraph).
2. **Save the context once, but update every copy.** A Claude Project, a Gemini Gem/skill or a Gemini Notebook saves you pasting, but none of them updates itself. Keep a list of where copies live and update them all on 5 November and 16 November ([Save your context](prompting-basics.md#save-your-context-so-you-dont-paste-it-every-time)).
3. **Treat `[CONFIRMAR]` as a hole, not a guess.** Tell the AI to write `[CONFIRMAR]` wherever a fact is missing, then press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> and search for it before anything is scheduled. Nothing with `[CONFIRMAR]` left in it gets published ([Prompt library](prompt-library.md#the-confirmar-convention)).
4. **One deliverable per message.** Ask for the Facebook post first, pick the best of three openings, and only then ask for the Instagram and WhatsApp versions. Asking for everything at once makes facts drift between versions ([Iterate](prompting-basics.md#iterate-the-second-prompt-is-where-quality-happens)).
5. **Make the AI list the facts it used.** End prompts with "list every fact a person must check". The reviewer then runs the [60-second fact check](prompting-basics.md#the-60-second-fact-check) against a short list instead of rereading everything.
6. **Write instructions in English if you like, but ask for Brazilian Portuguese output.** Then read it aloud. If it sounds dubbed, ask: *"Reescreva como uma professora brasileira falaria com colegas no grupo da escola. Mantenha os fatos exatamente iguais."* ([Natural pt-BR](prompting-basics.md#how-to-write-in-natural-brazilian-portuguese-with-ai)).
7. **Start a fresh chat when quality drops.** Long conversations make assistants "forget" early instructions. Open a new chat and paste the fact sheet, the voice paragraph and your best draft.
8. **Use the right free tool for the job.** Gemini Notebook grounds answers in supplied sources and provides citations; verify those passages before using FAQs or bios. Perplexity finds sources for outreach, but open every link yourself ([Choosing AI tools](choosing-ai-tools.md#best-for-table-conafroedu-tasks)).

## Facebook

9. **One Page, one Event, one registration link.** Before creating anything, ask the committee whether a CONAFROEDU Page or Event already exists. Two competing Events split RSVPs and questions ([Facebook Events & Groups](facebook-events-groups.md#before-you-click-create-five-decisions)).
10. **Keep a pinned post that matches the current phase.** Pin the essentials: what, when, for whom, how to register, how to submit while open, contact. Rewrite it on **5 Nov** (submissions closed), **16 Nov** (new prices) and **25 Nov** (program) ([Optimize the Page](facebook-playbook.md#step-3--optimize-the-page-new-or-existing)).
11. **Put every fact in the caption text, not only in the image.** Many people never read text inside images, and screen readers can't. Leave the Page address empty, because a city makes people think there is a venue. Say clearly that "Vou/Tenho interesse" on the Event is **not** registration.
12. **Test Live eligibility now, not on 4 December.** StreamYard's help center describes Meta's requirements as a Facebook account at least 60 days old and a Page with at least 100 followers. If you don't meet them, plan the YouTube fallback, and download every Live recording the same day, because Facebook deletes replays after 30 days ([Facebook Live](facebook-playbook.md#facebook-live-check-eligibility-now-not-on-4-december)).
13. **Test three time slots, then judge after 48 hours and 7 days.** Our working hypotheses (Brasília time) are Tue/Thu 07h–09h, Tue/Wed 12h–14h and Wed/Thu 18h30–20h30. No study has looked at Brazilian teachers specifically, so rotate and compare ([When to post](facebook-playbook.md#when-to-post-test-dont-guess)).
14. **Post in Groups by hand, with permission.** Read the rules, ask the moderator, adapt the text to that group and post once. Start with 3–5 strong groups. No tool can legitimately auto-post into other people's Groups ([Groups](facebook-events-groups.md#groups-participate-first-promote-second)).

## WhatsApp & Instagram

15. **Quick replies are your best "bot", and a person still sends them.** In the free WhatsApp Business app, save shortcuts such as `/prazo` and `/comosubmeter`, `/certificado` and `/humano` with approved facts only. A person picks the shortcut, adjusts the first line ("Oi, Ana!") and sends ([FAQ answer bank](replies-inbox.md#the-conafroedu-2026-faq-answer-bank)).
16. **Prefer a Channel to groups and broadcast lists.** WhatsApp Channel followers choose to follow, can leave at any time, and their numbers aren't shown to admins or other followers. Ordinary broadcast lists only reach people who saved *your* number, up to 256 per list ([Create a Channel](instagram-whatsapp.md#create-a-whatsapp-channel)).
17. **Reach private groups through people who belong there.** Send a coordinator a short forwardable text and one image through an appropriate institutional channel, and let them decide. WhatsApp requires a recipient-initiated conversation or recorded permission first. Never ask to be added to a group to promote, and never add people yourself ([Partner groups](instagram-whatsapp.md#partner-groups-and-forwarded-messages)).
18. **Ask for one personal Status a week.** A WhatsApp Status from a known colleague may reach people a new Page does not; compare actual visits instead of assuming it performs better. Give organizers and ambassadors a short text, an image and their own tracked link ([Status](instagram-whatsapp.md#status-personal-invitations)).
19. **Run an Instagram Collab with a confirmed speaker.** One co-authored Reel or carousel can appear on both profiles once the speaker accepts the invitation. Pick one genuinely useful topic, e.g. "Como trabalhar a Lei 10.639 em ciências e matemática?" ([Collabs](instagram-whatsapp.md#step-3--collabs-with-speakers-the-best-free-reach-on-instagram)).
20. **Remember that Instagram caption links aren't clickable.** Write "link na bio", keep one tagged Even3 link in the bio, and use up to five relevant hashtags. Update the Highlights ("Inscrição", "Submissão", "Acesso", "Certificado") after 4 and 16 November.

## Design & images

21. **Generate images without text, then type the text in Canva.** AI still breaks `Educação`, `Inscrições` and `Consciência`. Ask for "no words, letters, numbers or logos", leave empty space, and add every fact as editable text ([Portuguese text in images](image-generation.md#portuguese-text-in-images-generate-without-text-add-it-in-canva)).
22. **Build one master template and always duplicate it.** A 1080 × 1350 Canva design named "MESTRE — Feed — NÃO EDITAR" keeps four volunteers looking like one congress. Reuse one approved AI background across a whole series (for example, every countdown) and change only the number ([Canva templates](brand-kit.md#part-3--canva-templates)).
23. **Test every font with a Portuguese line.** Type *Educação, Participação, Conceição, ações, à, ê, õ — 5 e 6 de dezembro* before adopting a font. If any letter looks wrong or falls back to another font, pick a different one ([Typography](brand-kit.md#typography)).
24. **Real, consented photos come first.** A speaker card uses the speaker's own approved photo, cropped and nothing more. No AI "enhance", which can lighten skin or change hair ([Order of preference](representation-bias.md#order-of-preference-real-people-and-black-brazilian-artists-first)).
25. **Review the whole campaign grid, not one image.** Look at all your posts together: who teaches, who leads, who uses technology, which skin tones appear in which roles? When no one can explain why a symbol is there, remove it. Typography and simple shapes are always a safe choice ([Review checklist](representation-bias.md#pre-publication-review-checklist)).
26. **Let accepted authors make their own card.** If your plan offers a copy-generating Canva template link, test it with another account before sharing a "Trabalho aprovado" card. Never share master edit access; otherwise send a blank PNG. Authors may choose to fill it in and share ([Accepted-author kit](speaker-partner-kits.md#kit-1-accepted-authors-meu-trabalho-foi-aprovado-no-conafroedu)).

## Video & motion

27. **A real phone video beats any AI video.** Ask a confirmed speaker for 15–30 seconds, vertical, facing a window, in a quiet room, and to send it as a WhatsApp *document* to keep the quality ([Speaker invitation videos](video-repurposing.md#speaker-invitation-videos-recorded-on-a-phone)).
28. **Turn one talk into six Reels.** Name a "clip spotter" for each session who notes timestamps of strong moments. After the event, correct the transcript first, then let AI *propose* (never invent) clips ([1 talk → 6 Reels](video-repurposing.md#workflow-1-talk--6-reels-step-by-step)).
29. **Proofread every auto-caption.** Check names, accents, ERER, NEABI, "Lei 10.639/2003" and dates line by line. Automatic captions are a draft, and they matter for Deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers ([Auto-captions](video-repurposing.md#auto-captions-in-portuguese-accessibility)).
30. **Make one countdown MP4 per date, and mind the grammar.** A posted countdown never updates itself. The 7-day card goes out on **Saturday 28 November**, and the last one says "**Falta** 1 dia", not "Faltam 1 dias" ([Which number on which day](motion-graphics-claude.md#which-number-to-post-on-which-day)).
31. **If you generate video, make small, text-free inserts.** Keep to 5-second B-roll with no people and no text, set an attempt limit before you start, and add every word in the editor. Ignore any tutorial that recommends Sora, which was discontinued in 2026 ([Video generation](video-generation.md#the-short-version)).
32. **Use no music, or the Meta Sound Collection.** Trending songs and CapCut's "commercial" sounds aren't cleared for promoting an event on Facebook and Instagram. A clear voice with good captions is enough ([Music](video-repurposing.md#music-what-you-can-legally-use)).

## Community & partners

33. **Start with warm contacts.** Begin with the institutions of confirmed speakers and committee members, and NEABs/NEABIs where someone on the team already knows a person. "A professora X, que vai falar no congresso, sugeriu…" gets read, but only write it if it's true ([Outreach](outreach.md#who-to-approach-research-leads-not-confirmed-partners)).
34. **Aim for 10–20 excellent conversations, not 1,000 cold messages.** Use appropriate institutional email/form for first contact; WhatsApp requires recorded permission. Send each message yourself, one recipient at a time, follow up **once** after about a week, then stop. Respect every "não, obrigada" permanently ([Follow-up cadence](outreach.md#follow-up-cadence)).
35. **Send kits, not requests.** A kit is one or two images, two or three suggested captions, a tracked link and the facts, plus the line "Adapte ao seu jeito de falar". Sharing is always optional for the person receiving it ([Promo kits](speaker-partner-kits.md#the-four-kits-at-a-glance)).
36. **Prepare 20 November with a Black educator, starting now.** Agree on the topic, credit, approval and, if possible, payment during the week of 2 November; it can't be improvised the week before. If you want press coverage around 20 November, pitch education media by **6–10 November** ([Sprint, week 2](conafroedu-sprint.md#week-2--mon-2-to-sun-8-nov-close-submissions-switch-to-attendance)).
37. **Agree on a moderation plan before you need it.** Hide or delete racist comments (after a screenshot), support the person targeted and don't debate trolls in public. Never blanket-block words like "negro", "racismo" or "antirracista", because legitimate discussion uses them every day ([Moderation](replies-inbox.md#moderating-an-anti-racist-education-page)).

## The academic audience

38. **Keep *inscrição* and *submissão* separate in every post.** Registering and sending a paper are different steps with different deadlines (4 Nov for submissions, 6 Dec for registrations). After 4 November, every message is about attending; "you don't need to present to take part" is a useful post ([Two clocks, two funnels](campaign-timeline.md#two-clocks-two-funnels)).
39. **Describe the certificate carefully.** Write "certificado de 40 horas, conforme os critérios divulgados pela organização", and add that using it for *horas complementares* or career progression depends on each institution. Don't explain eligibility until the committee confirms it ([Words we use](brand-kit.md#words-we-use-and-words-we-avoid)).
40. **Don't confuse ISBN and DOI with Qualis.** ISBN and DOI identify a publication and make it citable; they don't create a Qualis rating. Use the safe ready-made answer for "os anais são Qualis?" ([FAQ answers](replies-inbox.md#the-answers-ready-to-paste-in-portuguese)).
41. **Show the full cost up front.** Registration was R$ 55,00–70,00 by category until 15 November, then R$ 60,00–75,00 from 16 November, as displayed on 21 October. Publication in the proceedings costs a separate R$ 16,98 per work. Check the Even3 checkout before any price post; academics trust events that don't hide fees.
42. **Make first-time authors feel capable.** Tell teachers that a classroom practice they already did can become a *práticas exitosas* report. A committee member can record a 60–90-second screen video of a test submission on Even3, which answers "how do I submit?" better than any bot ([`/comosubmeter`](replies-inbox.md#the-answers-ready-to-paste-in-portuguese)).

## Saving time

43. **Batch once a week.** A 90-minute Monday session with two people (draft with AI, check facts, schedule in Meta Business Suite) replaces daily scrambling ([Weekly batch session](scheduling-tools.md#the-weekly-batch-session-90-minutes-once-a-week)).
44. **Run everything from one shared sheet with IDs.** One row per post, one ID through every step, statuses from `idea` to `checked`, and one named queue owner. This prevents the classic mistake of the same post scheduled twice by two people ([One sheet, one scheduler, one owner](scheduling-tools.md#the-system-one-sheet-one-scheduler-one-owner)).
45. **Audit the queue whenever a fact changes.** Check scheduled posts on the evening of 4 November and the morning of 16 November for stale submission calls and old prices. Never pre-schedule event-day posts for 5–6 December ([Queue audit](scheduling-tools.md#the-queue-audit-stop-stale-posts-before-they-go-out)).
46. **Measure registrations, not likes, in 30 minutes a week.** Take every link from a tagged "Links" tab, count confirmed registrations on Even3, and drop any format that eats hours and brings nobody. If the team falls behind, follow the [minimum plan](conafroedu-sprint.md#if-you-fall-behind-the-minimum-plan) ([Weekly review](measuring-results.md#step-4-the-weekly-review-30-minutes)).

## Ethics & safety

47. **Keep personal data out of AI tools.** The only narrow approved-bio exception is documented in [ethics](ethics-legal.md); publication permission alone is insufficient. Before asking AI for help with a message, replace names, numbers and schools with `[PESSOA]` and `[ESCOLA]`. Analyze Even3 data only as counts by date, category and source ([LGPD](ethics-legal.md#4-lgpd-emails-whatsapp-numbers-and-registrant-data)).
48. **Never fake evidence.** There is no previous edition, so there are no "photos from last year". The rules: no AI crowds, no invented quotes or testimonials, no "últimas vagas" for an online event with no announced cap, and no institution named as a partner without written agreement ([The six golden rules](ethics-legal.md#the-six-golden-rules)).
49. **Label realistic AI media.** Turn on Meta's AI label for realistic synthetic video, AI dubbing and avatars. For illustrations, add a line such as *"Ilustração criada com IA; não retrata participantes do evento."* A label doesn't make a stereotyped image acceptable ([Disclosing AI](ethics-legal.md#1-disclosing-ai-on-facebook-and-instagram)).
50. **Automate drafts, never contact.** Mass DMs, auto-joining or auto-posting in groups, scraping member lists and WhatsApp "bulk senders" break platform rules and can get the support number or Page restricted mid-campaign. Do not use external tools/agents for Group mass posting or cold DMs. Admins may use native scheduling in groups they manage; other groups use manual moderator-approved posting ([What NOT to automate](computer-use-agents.md#what-not-to-automate)).

## Printable checklist: the tips people forget

- [ ] Fact sheet updated and every saved AI project updated with it (tip 2).
- [ ] No `[CONFIRMAR]` left in anything scheduled (tip 3).
- [ ] Pinned post, Event and Highlights match the current phase (tips 10 and 20).
- [ ] Live eligibility tested or YouTube fallback chosen; Live recordings downloaded the same day (tip 12).
- [ ] Group posts made by hand, only with permission (tip 14).
- [ ] All text in images added in Canva and proofread at phone size (tips 21 and 23).
- [ ] Auto-captions proofread line by line (tip 29).
- [ ] Countdown number matches the posting date (tip 30).
- [ ] Every submission call removed on 5 Nov; every old price removed on 16 Nov (tips 38 and 45).
- [ ] Certificate and Qualis wording checked (tips 39 and 40).
- [ ] Only aggregated data pasted into AI tools (tip 47).
- [ ] Realistic AI media labeled (tip 49).
