This site is a practical handbook for the volunteer team promoting **CONAFROEDU 2026 — Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação** ("Desenvolvimento, Participação e Educação Antirracista"), a national, 100% online academic congress on **5–6 December 2026**. It shows you how to use free AI tools and organic (unpaid) Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp to bring in authors and participants — honestly, respectfully and without an ad budget.

## Who this is for

- **Educators and researchers on the organizing committee** who are not marketing or tech specialists.
- **Volunteers** who post, design, answer messages or contact partners — even a few hours a week.
- Anyone organizing a **future edition** or a similar academic event in Brazil: the methods are reusable, even though our examples use CONAFROEDU's real 2026 dates.

You don't need to know anything about AI. Every guide explains terms the first time they appear, gives numbered steps and includes ready-to-use prompts with a **Copy** button.

## Our principles

These run through every guide on the site.

1. **Organic and low-cost first.** No paid ads. Every tool is shown with its free option and real cost (in R$ and US$ where published). We always start with the cheapest path that works.
2. **Honest communication.** Real dates, real prices, real conditions. No invented deadlines, no fake "últimas vagas", no promises about MEC recognition, Qualis or career progression. When the committee hasn't decided something, we say so.
3. **Respectful representation.** CONAFROEDU is about Africanidades and anti-racist education. Real photos of real people (with consent) and work by Black Brazilian artists come before AI images; any AI imagery is reviewed for stereotypes and disclosed. See [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully](representation-bias.md).
4. **A human in the loop, always.** AI drafts; people decide. Every AI-made post is checked by a second person before publishing, and AI never sends messages, joins groups or answers sensitive questions on its own.
5. **Privacy and consent.** No registrant lists, CPFs or unpublished papers in public AI tools. No scraping, no mass unsolicited messages, no fake accounts. Details in [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

> [!NOTE]
> **About accuracy.** AI products change every few weeks. Researched on **21 October 2026**. Verified facts link to their sources; account-specific features, blocked sources and untested workflows are identified where relevant. If something looks different in your account, trust what you see on the official page — and tell the team so the guide can be updated.
