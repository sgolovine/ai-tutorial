**What you'll get from this page:** the rules and good practices that keep the CONAFROEDU 2026 campaign honest, legal and respectful. You'll learn when to label AI content on Facebook and Instagram, how to get consent for photos and voices (including minors), what you can and can't do with AI-generated images and music, how LGPD affects the emails and WhatsApp numbers you collect, which spam rules apply to Groups and WhatsApp, how to make posts accessible, and why responsible AI use matters even more on an anti-racist education theme.

> [!IMPORTANT] Not legal advice
> This page is practical guidance written for a volunteer team, based on public sources checked on 21 October 2026. It is **not legal advice**. For decisions with legal consequences (privacy notices, image-rights contracts, contests, contracts with artists), consult a qualified professional, e.g. a lawyer or the legal office of a partner university.

## The six golden rules

1. **Never fake evidence.** No invented testimonials, participants, speakers, previous editions, endorsements, scarcity or deadlines.
2. **Ask before using anyone's image, voice or words**, and ask *separately* for AI uses (dubbing, avatars, edits).
3. **Label realistic AI media** and explain illustrations in plain language.
4. **Only message people who asked for it**, and stop immediately when they ask you to.
5. **Keep personal data out of AI tools** and public folders, except the narrow approved-bio workflow documented below.
6. **Make every post usable by everyone:** text in the caption, alt text, captions on video.

## 1. Disclosing AI on Facebook and Instagram

### What Meta asks for

Meta applies an **"AI info"** label (in the Portuguese interface it is called "Informações de IA"; controls and placement vary) to content it detects as AI-generated, using industry signals and self-disclosure ([Meta's labeling approach](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)). Meta's 2026 policy communication reiterates that users **must use the disclosure tool** for organic posts with **photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio** that was digitally created or altered, and that penalties may apply if they don't ([Meta, Feb 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/02/meta-prepares-for-2026-us-midterms/)).

### What that means in practice

| What you're publishing | Use Meta's AI disclosure control? | Add a plain-language note in the caption? |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker video with **AI dubbing** into Portuguese | **Yes**: realistic altered audio | Yes: "Dublagem em português criada com IA e aprovada pelo palestrante." |
| **Avatar / digital twin** of a speaker or organizer | **Yes** | Yes: "Apresentador virtual. Texto revisado pela organização." |
| Realistic AI-generated video of a classroom or people | **Yes** | Yes: "Cena gerada com IA; não retrata participantes do evento." |
| AI-generated illustration (still image) | Meta may label it automatically; use the control if offered | Yes, if anyone could mistake it for a real photo: "Ilustração gerada com IA; não retrata participantes do evento." |
| Motion graphics made with Claude/code (text, shapes) | Generally no realistic people or audio, so no | Optional |
| Caption text drafted with AI and edited by a person | No | No |
| Real photo, cropped and color-corrected | No | No |

**Also keep in mind:**

- **Don't remove provenance metadata.** Canva's AI terms specifically prohibit removing provenance/metadata tags from its AI outputs ([Canva AI Product Terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/ai-product-terms/)).
- **Labels may not appear identically** on every cross-posted surface. Check the final publishing screen.
- **There's no evidence that a label reduces reach.** Don't avoid labeling for that reason.
- **A label doesn't make anything acceptable.** It doesn't authorize deception, impersonation, infringement or using someone's likeness without consent. And research has found that stereotyped AI faces still influence viewers *even when labeled* ([Scientific Reports, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-99623-3)), so disclosure doesn't fix a biased image.

## 2. Consent: image, voice and likeness

### Speakers and authors

Ask for explicit permission for **each** use. A general "you can use my photo" doesn't cover everything:

| Use | Separate permission? |
|---|---|
| Publishing their photo in posts and cards | Yes |
| Recording their session | Yes |
| Cutting clips from the recording for Reels | Yes |
| Editing the photo (crop, light) | Covered by the photo permission if stated |
| Uploading their photo/voice to an **external AI service** | **Yes, separately** |
| Translating their words into subtitles | Yes (and they approve the translation) |
| **AI dubbing** with a synthetic voice | **Yes, separately**, after approving the translated script |
| **Voice cloning** or a **digital avatar** | **Yes, separately**. Some tools (e.g. HeyGen) require a consent recording from the person themselves ([HeyGen help](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/12092609-recording-your-consent-video)), but that doesn't replace your own agreement |

Keep every quote traceable to what the person actually said or wrote, and let them approve sensitive edits.

### A simple authorization form (template)

Use this as a starting point and have it reviewed by someone with legal knowledge before using it. It's in Portuguese because that's the language your speakers will sign it in:

```text
TERMO DE AUTORIZAÇÃO DE USO DE IMAGEM, VOZ E NOME – CONAFROEDU 2026

Eu, [nome completo], autorizo [nome/razão social do organizador e contato] a utilizar meu nome, imagem e voz exclusivamente para [finalidades específicas, incluindo divulgação das inscrições pagas do I CONAFROEDU 2026], nos usos, canais e prazo assinalados abaixo. Remuneração/contrapartida acordada: [descrever ou informar que não há].
Responsável pelo tratamento (controlador) e contato de privacidade: [identificar].
Aviso de privacidade, finalidades e condições de tratamento: [link/documento].

[ ] Fotografia enviada por mim, em publicações de divulgação do congresso
[ ] Gravação da minha participação nos dias 5 e 6/12/2026
[ ] Trechos editados da gravação (cortes/Reels) para divulgação
[ ] Legendas e traduções do que eu disser, com minha aprovação prévia
[ ] Quero avaliar um anexo OPCIONAL e separado para uso de IA (não autoriza uso antes de preenchido e aprovado):
Serviço/provedor: [nome]. Transformação e peças exatas: [descrever dublagem/avatar/outro].
Material enviado, compartilhamento/transferências aplicáveis e condições de tratamento: [descrever/link].
Prazo de armazenamento e exclusão: [definir]. Aprovação prévia da pessoa para roteiro, transformação e resultado final: [procedimento].
Não autoriza reconstrução generativa de retratos para os cards desta campanha.

Canais: Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, página do evento na Even3 e [outros].
Prazo: até [data] para novas publicações. Posso revogar esta autorização para usos futuros a qualquer momento, pelo contato [e-mail/WhatsApp].
Crédito preferido: [como quer ser identificada/o].

Local e data: ____________  Assinatura: ____________
```

### Attendees during the online congress

- **Announce recording** at the start of each session and in the access instructions.
- **Screenshots of video-call galleries** show other people's faces and names. Crop to the speaker, or ask everyone visible before posting.
- **Chat messages** are not public quotes. Ask before reposting someone's comment.
- For UGC (participants' own posts), see the [UGC section of the kits guide](speaker-partner-kits.md#user-generated-content-ugc-ask-credit-never-fabricate).

### Minors and school photos

Many educators submit **práticas exitosas** (classroom experience reports) with photos of students. These are a sensitive area:

- **Don't reuse student photos from papers in marketing.** Authorization for a scientific report is not authorization for promotion.
- **Brazilian law treats children's and adolescents' data with special care.** ANPD (Brazil’s national data-protection regulator) states that their data must be handled in their **best interest** ([ANPD Enunciado 1/2023](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-divulga-enunciado-sobre-o-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-de-criancas-e-adolescentes/Enunciado1ANPD.pdf)).
- **Default for this campaign:** no identifiable minors in promotional posts. Show materials, hands, the teacher, or illustrations instead. If a school-based image is truly essential, involve the school and the families and get proper review first.
- **Never upload children's faces or voices to AI generators** or editing services.
- **Don't invite identifiable stories of children experiencing racism** in public comments. Discuss institutional responses in general terms with a qualified educator.

## 3. Copyright and licenses

### The four questions to ask about any asset

1. May **this account / plan** be used to promote the congress?
2. Does the **specific asset or model license** allow this use?
3. Do we have rights to **every input** (photos, slides, logos, music, voices)?
4. Is the result **original enough**, and does it avoid copying someone else's work?

> [!WARNING] "Organic" is still promotion
> A free post, an unpaid campaign and an educational theme do **not** make your use "noncommercial". Promoting a paid congress is promotional use. Choose tools and assets that allow it.

### AI-generated images, text and music

- **Who owns AI output is unsettled.** Brazil's copyright law (Lei 9.610/1998) describes an author as a natural person ([Lei 9.610](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9610.htm)). Whether a purely AI-generated image is protected in Brazil isn't settled, and a vendor saying "you own it" doesn't decide it. Keep the human-made parts (layout, text, real logo, edits) in your editable source files.
- **Free plans often exclude commercial use.** Examples at the time of writing: Suno's Free plan has **no commercial rights** ([Suno pricing](https://suno.com/pricing)); ElevenLabs says its Free plan has **no commercial license** ([ElevenLabs help](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/pt/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform)). Check the current plan page before using any output.
- **Canva Education isn't for conference promotion.** Education Pro content is for educational, noncommercial use. A teacher's free Education account doesn't cover marketing a paid congress. Use a regular account and commercially licensed elements.
- **Don't prompt for imitation.** No "in the style of [living artist]", no famous singers' voices, no recognizable melodies.

### Music

| Option | Can CONAFROEDU use it? |
|---|---|
| **Meta Sound Collection** | Meta describes it as cleared for commercial use, unlike the ordinary music library, which is for personal/noncommercial use ([Meta music help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/402084904469945)). Best first choice for Facebook/Instagram only. Not automatically licensed for WhatsApp, YouTube or your site |
| **Ordinary Instagram/Facebook music library** | Personal/noncommercial scope; avoid for promoting the congress |
| **CapCut "Commercial Sounds"** | Its international agreement limits them to CapCut/TikTok uses unless separately cleared, so **not** cleared for Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp |
| **AI music (e.g. Suno paid plan)** | Only from a paid plan that grants commercial use, **created while subscribed** to a plan granting commercial use; keep creation/subscription records. Download date is not the criterion; rights can survive cancellation ([Suno help](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2416769)) |
| **Original or commissioned music** | Good choice with a written permission covering promotion, edits, channels and duration |
| **No music** | Often best: a clear real voice with accurate captions |

### Photos and illustrations

| Source | What the license allows (check the specific file) |
|---|---|
| **Real, consented photos of speakers/participants** | The best choice; record the permission |
| **Nappy** | Free, CC0-style use incl. commercial and edits; no implied endorsement, no degrading use ([license](https://nappy.co/license)). International library: don't call a model "Brazilian" or a participant |
| **Pexels** | Free promotional use and edits; no implied endorsement ([license](https://www.pexels.com/license/)) |
| **Wikimedia Commons** | Per-file licenses; usually requires **attribution, license link and noting changes** ([reuse guide](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)) |
| **Brasil com S** | Free images are **editorial only**: no promotional use, no edits ([terms](https://www.brasilcoms.com.br/termosdeuso)). Don't use them in registration cards |
| **Commissioned work by a Black Brazilian artist/designer** | Agree in writing on fee, deliverables, channels, period, credit and whether AI may be used on the work. Communities like [PretUX](https://www.pretux.com.br/quero-ajudar/) list Black designers for hire. Visibility is not payment |

**Credit format examples:** `Ilustração: [nome] (@perfil), encomenda para o CONAFROEDU 2026; uso autorizado.` / `Foto: [autor] / [acervo], [licença e link]; [alteração, se houver].` Credit doesn't replace a license.

### Institutional names and logos

The scientific committee's members are affiliated with several universities, but **personal affiliations are not institutional endorsement**. Don't use any university, NEAB/NEABI, ministry or association logo, or say "apoio" or "parceria", without written authorization. Don't imply MEC, CAPES or PNEERQ recognition.

For speaker bios, the only limited exception to the no-personal-data AI rule is **minimal approved professional source text**, using a committee-approved service/privacy setting, documented purpose/legal basis, notice and the speaker’s agreement to external AI processing. Public availability or permission to publish alone is insufficient. Exclude full CVs, contacts, sensitive details and unrelated information. Otherwise write the bio manually. This exception does not authorize AI portrait reconstruction.

## 4. LGPD: emails, WhatsApp numbers and registrant data

**LGPD** (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, [Lei 13.709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)) is Brazil's data protection law. Any information that identifies a person, such as name, email, phone number or photo, is personal data.

### The essentials

- **You need a legal basis and a specific purpose** for each use of personal data. **Consent** is one basis, but not the only one. Where you rely on consent, it must be specific, demonstrable and easy to withdraw.
- **Legitimate interest is not a blanket marketing permission.** ANPD's guide requires a case-by-case assessment and balancing ([ANPD guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia_orientativo_hipoteses_legais_tratamento_de_dados_pessoais_legitimo_interesse)), and it can't be used as the basis for sensitive data.
- **Sensitive data needs extra care.** LGPD treats information about **racial or ethnic origin, religious belief and health** as sensitive (art. 5, II). For an event about Africanidades, this matters: **don't ask for racial self-declaration or religion** in marketing forms, never infer them from names or photos, and handle accessibility requests (which can reveal health information) only among the people arranging them.
- **Collect only what you need.** Don't ask for a CPF, school or students' details just to send a schedule.

### A workable setup for CONAFROEDU

1. **Separate service messages from marketing.** Messages about a registration someone made (access link, schedule, certificate) are part of the service. Newsletters, future editions and promotions are **optional** and need their own choice.
2. **Use unchecked, specific opt-in boxes**, one per channel (email, WhatsApp), with an honest description of frequency and period.
3. **Identify who is responsible.** A privacy notice must say who the organizer (data controller) is and how to contact them. The public Even3 page currently shows only "Secretaria do Evento", so **the committee must define and publish this**.
4. **Record each opt-in:** wording shown, purpose, channel, date/time and where it came from.
5. **Honor opt-outs quickly**, whatever words the person uses ("SAIR", "pare", "não quero mais"). Keep only the minimum record needed to not contact them again.
6. **Define retention and access:** who can see exports, where they're stored (never a public folder) and when they're deleted.
7. **Don't share lists** with partners or sponsors for their own marketing.
8. **Don't upload registrant exports to AI tools**, not even to "find better audiences". Work with aggregates (see [Measuring Results](measuring-results.md)).
9. **Don't import external lists** (other events, bought lists, scraped contacts) into Even3 notifications or email tools.

Suggested optional checkbox (needs organizer review):

```text
[ ] Quero receber pelo WhatsApp informações e lembretes sobre o I CONAFROEDU até 6/12/2026, em média [1 a 2] mensagens por semana. Posso cancelar a qualquer momento respondendo SAIR ou pedindo por qualquer canal. Aviso de privacidade: [link].
```

Use a **separate** checkbox for future editions or a broader newsletter; that's a different purpose. Declining marketing must never block registration.

### Cookies and analytics

If you add Google Analytics 4 or other trackers to Even3 or a website, ANPD's cookie guide recommends clear choices, an easily visible option to reject non-necessary cookies, and consent-based cookies off by default ([ANPD cookie guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia_orientativo_cookies_e_protecao_de_dados_pessoais)). For this campaign, Even3's own reports and UTM links are usually enough. Don't install advertising pixels you don't need.

**Benefit-for-post arrangements:** complimentary admission, gifts or payment in exchange for promotion can be branded content. Check eligibility and use Meta’s **paid partnership label**, plus a clear caption disclosure; Collab is not a substitute. Resolve label availability before agreeing to the arrangement ([Meta’s explanation](https://about.fb.com/fr/news/2023/02/comment-meta-accompagne-les-createurs-de-contenus-et-les-acteurs-de-linfluence-en-ligne/)). Independent unpaid sharing is a different case.

## 5. Anti-spam rules: Facebook, Groups, Messenger and WhatsApp

### Facebook and Groups

- **Spammy behavior loses distribution.** Meta's 2025 crackdown targets excessive or unrelated hashtags, unrelated captions and fake engagement ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/cracking-down-spammy-content-facebook/)). **Engagement bait** ("comente SIM", "marque 5 amigos") has long been demoted, including in Portuguese ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/)).
- **No unauthorized bots or integrations for Group posting.** Removal of the external Groups API did not ban [Facebook’s native scheduled/recurring posts in groups you manage](https://www.facebook.com/help/124331564818285/). In other people’s groups, follow our manual, moderator-approved process.
- **No scraping.** Meta says automated collection of data without permission violates its terms ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/04/how-we-combat-scraping/)). Don't extract group members or Event respondents to message them.
- **Respect each group's rules.** Read them, ask moderators, post once where allowed, and stop if they say no. Details in [Facebook Events & Groups](facebook-events-groups.md).
- **No fake accounts or borrowed identities** to get around a refusal.

### WhatsApp

- **Opt-in and opt-out are required.** WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy (updated September 2026) requires permission to contact someone and respecting requests to stop ([policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)). A public phone number, group membership or a Facebook Event response is **not** permission.
- **Broadcast lists only reach people who saved your number** in their own contacts, and each list holds up to 256 contacts ([WhatsApp Help](https://faq.whatsapp.com/653415899610349/?cms_platform=android&locale=en_US)). Saving your number is a delivery condition, not marketing consent.
- **Never add people to groups without asking.** And remember a group invite link exposes members' phone numbers to each other. **Channels** don't show followers' numbers ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/09/whatsapp-channels-heres-everything-you-need-to-know/)), so they're the privacy-friendlier option for public updates.
- **No unofficial bulk senders**, purchased lists or browser extensions promising unlimited sends. WhatsApp can restrict accounts that violate its policies.
- **The WhatsApp Business Platform (API) costs money.** Marketing templates are charged per message. See [Automating Replies](replies-inbox.md) before considering it.

### Messenger and email

- **Messenger:** a standard 24-hour window applies after a person messages the Page; a Page comment allows one private reply **within seven days**. Wait for the person’s reply before continuing under the applicable window; it does not authorize a campaign. "They liked the post" is not consent.
- **Email:** free tools (Brevo, MailerLite) are for **opt-in** lists. A free quota is not permission to upload a bought or scraped list.

### Not OK → do this instead

| Not OK | Do this instead |
|---|---|
| "Marque 5 professores para concorrer" | A useful resource with a direct link; any promotion reviewed separately (contests may have specific legal rules) |
| The same link dropped in 30 groups | 3–5 relevant groups, moderator permission, a tailored contribution |
| Messaging everyone who clicked "Interested" | A pinned Event post and a voluntary WhatsApp Channel |
| Adding teachers' numbers to a group | Sharing a Channel link people can choose to follow |
| "Últimas vagas!" for an online event with no cap | A true date/price deadline ("o valor muda em 16/11") |
| Auto-reply pretending a person read the message | "Resposta automática; nossa equipe responde em [horário]" |

## 6. Accessibility

Accessibility is part of an anti-racist and inclusive event, not an extra. The W3C (the organization that sets web standards) explains that images need text alternatives conveying their information, and that captions should include speech **and relevant non-speech sounds** ([W3C images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/), [W3C captions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/)).

### Publishing checklist

- [ ] Essential video visuals are narrated: say aloud screen steps, slides and animated text. For silent media, publish an equivalent description/transcript and audio description where needed. Test understanding without looking ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/av-content/)).
- [ ] Date, time zone (horário de Brasília), price and registration instructions are in the **caption text**, not only inside the image.
- [ ] **Alt text** added where the platform allows (and checked after cross-posting). If there's no alt-text field, describe the image in the caption.
- [ ] **Video captions** proofread: names, acronyms (ERER, NEABI), accents. Auto-captions always need checking.
- [ ] Speakers identified in captions when more than one person talks.
- [ ] **Contrast:** text clearly readable. As a reference, WCAG asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; free online contrast checkers can test your colors.
- [ ] Information never conveyed **by color alone** (e.g. tracks or deadlines).
- [ ] Text checked at phone size; nothing important near the edges in Stories/Reels.
- [ ] **No decorative Unicode "fonts"** (𝓯𝓪𝓷𝓬𝔂 text): screen readers can't read them.
- [ ] Few emojis, at the end of sentences, not replacing words.
- [ ] Hashtags with capitals for each word (#EducaçãoAntirracista), which is easier for screen readers.
- [ ] No flashing effects in videos.
- [ ] **Libras** (Brazilian Sign Language) interpretation or other services are **only advertised if confirmed** by the committee. It's worth discussing whether the opening or key sessions could have Libras interpretation. If so, announce it clearly; if not, don't imply it.
- [ ] A real contact for accessibility requests, handled privately.

**Alt text example** (describe only what's actually in your image): "Card com fundo laranja e texto: CONAFROEDU 2026, Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação, 5 e 6 de dezembro, 100% on-line. Inscrições pela Even3." Don't guess people's race, disability or emotions in alt text.

### Prompt: alt text and caption review

```prompt
Prepare an accessibility review for this social media asset. Answer in Brazilian Portuguese.
Factual description of the image/video and all visible text: [PASTE]. Corrected transcript, if video: [PASTE].
1) Write a short alt text (max. 250 characters) conveying the important information, without assuming anyone's identity, race, disability or emotions.
2) List the information that appears only in the image and must be repeated in the caption (date, time zone, price, link).
3) For video: list speech, speaker identification and sounds needing captions, plus essential visual information that must be narrated or described.
4) List what a person must check visually before publishing (contrast, small text, edges).
```

## 7. Responsible AI on an anti-racist education theme

CONAFROEDU is about Africanidades, Afro-Brazilian history and culture and anti-racist education. Careless AI use here doesn't just look bad; it can reproduce the very racism the congress exists to confront. Research has documented AI image generators lightening skin, homogenizing Black faces, defaulting to poverty or "generic Africa", and placing white people in positions of authority. Read [Representing Black & African Cultures Respectfully with AI](representation-bias.md) before making any image or video with people.

**Principles for the team:**

1. **Real people first.** Consented photos of the real speakers and educators, and work by Black Brazilian photographers, designers and illustrators (paid and credited), come before any generated image.
2. **No synthetic "participants".** Never use AI people as attendees, speakers, testimonials or "photos from the event", and never invent a previous edition.
3. **Never "fix" real people.** No AI retouching that changes skin tone, features or hair texture.
4. **No decorative sacred or cultural symbols.** Orixás, Candomblé/Umbanda imagery, Adinkra and Kente are not generic "African" decoration. Use them only with a real reason and review by knowledgeable people.
5. **Review by people with authority to say no.** Ideally a Black educator or designer, with agreed time, reviews representation before publishing.
6. **Don't infer race or religion** of real people from photos or names, not with AI and not by hand.
7. **Check AI's facts about laws and history.** AI often gets details wrong: Lei 10.639 is from **9 January 2003**, Lei 11.645 from **10 March 2008** (it added Indigenous history and culture), and Lei 14.759/2023 made **20 November** a national holiday. Don't let AI invent quotes from historical figures.
8. **Don't overpromise academic benefits.** No "vale pontos no Qualis", "garante progressão", "reconhecido pelo MEC" or "curso oficial do PNEERQ". Describe the 40-hour certificate "conforme os critérios divulgados pela organização" and note that recognition depends on each institution.
9. **Don't turn pain into sales.** On 20 November and in posts about racism in schools, offer substance and respect; don't use trauma as a hook.

## Pre-publication ethics checklist

- [ ] Facts match the current Even3 page; nothing unconfirmed is presented as fact.
- [ ] No invented people, quotes, testimonials, partners, endorsements, scarcity or editions.
- [ ] Permission recorded for every real person's image, voice and words, including separate AI permissions.
- [ ] No identifiable minors.
- [ ] Every asset's license allows promotional use; credits included.
- [ ] Realistic AI media labeled with Meta's control; illustrations explained in the caption.
- [ ] Representation reviewed (see [representation-bias](representation-bias.md)).
- [ ] Messages only to people who opted in; opt-out honored.
- [ ] No personal data in AI, except the documented minimal approved-bio workflow above.
- [ ] Paid-partnership control and caption disclosure checked for benefit-for-post arrangements.
- [ ] Accessibility checklist done.

## Decisions the committee needs to make

- [ ] Who is the legal organizer/data controller, and where is the privacy notice?
- [ ] Retention: when will marketing lists and exports be deleted?
- [ ] Will sessions be recorded? Where will recordings be stored, and for how long? (Facebook Live replays are kept for only **30 days**; [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/updating-our-facebook-live-video-storage-policy/).)
- [ ] Which accessibility services will actually be offered (captions, Libras)?
- [ ] Who has authority to approve or reject images for representation?
- [ ] Is there budget for commissioning at least one piece from a Black Brazilian artist?
