This page is the site's dictionary and address book. If a guide uses a word you don't know (Collab, UTM, Qualis, *anais*, LGPD…), look it up here. If you need the official page behind a claim, such as a pricing page, a Meta help article or a law, the resource list has it.

**What you'll get from this page:**

- A **glossary** in five groups: AI, marketing and social media, Meta features (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp), Brazilian academic and cultural context (written for English-speaking readers), and legal, privacy and accessibility terms.
- A **curated resource list**: only tools, help centers and official sources that the guides actually cite, grouped by category, with free-tier notes.

> [!NOTE]
> **Last verified: 21 October 2026.** Prices and free tiers are snapshots taken from the official pages on that date, and they change often. USD and BRL prices are separate vendor listings, not conversions. Check the live plan page before relying on a limit or paying for anything.

## Glossary

Each term links to the guide where it matters most.

### AI terms

| Term | What it means | Covered in |
|---|---|---|
| **AI assistant** | The app or website you chat with: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, Copilot, Grok, Perplexity. It wraps one or more models with a chat window, file uploads, search and a price plan. | [Choosing AI tools](choosing-ai-tools.md#assistants-vs-models-the-one-concept-you-need) |
| **Model** | The "engine" inside an assistant (for example Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Gemini 4 Argon). Better models are often reserved for paid plans. | [Choosing AI tools](choosing-ai-tools.md) |
| **Prompt** | The message you type to an AI assistant. A good one gives the role, the facts, the task, the audience, the format and what not to do. | [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#the-anatomy-of-a-good-prompt) |
| **Fact sheet / context block** (*ficha oficial*) | The team's short, approved list of facts (dates, prices, links, open questions) pasted at the start of every chat, so the AI stops guessing. | [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block) |
| **`[CONFIRMAR]`** | "To confirm": the team's marker for a fact only the committee can decide. Nothing that still contains it gets published. | [Prompt library](prompt-library.md#the-confirmar-convention) |
| **Hallucination** | A confident, fluent statement from an AI that is false, such as an invented deadline, price, quote or "MEC recognition". | [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#verify-every-output-dates-prices-promises) |
| **Project, skill, custom GPT** | Saved context/instruction mechanisms, not models. Claude Projects: Free up to five; Claude Skills: Free with code execution, installed through Customize → Skills. Legacy custom GPTs have migration rules; personal ChatGPT accounts cannot create new GPTs. Use current Projects/Plugins instead of retired setup tutorials. | [Setup](setup-tools.md) |
| **Gemini Notebook** (formerly NotebookLM) | Google’s source-grounded research assistant with citations. Check each cited passage; do not treat a notebook answer as independently verified. Source types and connected Gemini behavior vary. | [Setup](setup-tools.md) · [Learning](learning-with-ai.md) |
| **Artifact / Design** | In Claude, an editable document, page or animation shown beside the chat, which you can export. Used for motion graphics. | [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md) |
| **Free tier / usage limit** | What a plan gives you at no cost. Limits often reset over time and are rarely a fixed number of messages. | [Choosing AI tools](choosing-ai-tools.md#free-vs-paid-what-it-costs) |
| **API / API key** | The technical door through which other software (Zapier, Make, n8n) uses an AI model; the key is a password-like code. Billed separately from a chat subscription. | [No-code automations](no-code-automation.md#key-words-in-plain-language) |
| **Agent (browser or computer-use)** | An AI that clicks, types and fills in forms for you, in a browser or on your computer. Useful for bounded research; never for posting or messaging at scale. | [Computer-use agents](computer-use-agents.md) |
| **Prompt injection** | Hidden instructions on a web page or in a message that try to trick an AI agent ("ignore your instructions and…"). | [Computer-use agents](computer-use-agents.md#golden-rules-for-supervising-an-agent) |
| **Leaderboard** | A public ranking of models by people's preferences or tests. It doesn't measure Portuguese quality, licensing or respectful representation. | [Choosing AI tools](choosing-ai-tools.md#leaderboards-interesting-not-decisive) |
| **Open model** | A model you can download and run yourself (e.g. Qwen, Mistral Small). "Open" doesn't mean free to run. | [Choosing AI tools](choosing-ai-tools.md) |
| **Reference image** | An image you give an image model to control style, colors or layout. It helps consistency but doesn't guarantee it. | [Image generation](image-generation.md#keeping-a-consistent-look-across-the-campaign) |
| **Outpainting / extend** | Asking an image tool to continue a picture beyond its edges, for example from 4:5 to 9:16. | [Image prompt cookbook](image-prompts.md#fix-it-prompts-iteration) |
| **Text-to-video / image-to-video** | Making a clip from a description alone, or animating a still image you upload. The second gives more control. | [Video generation](video-generation.md#key-terms-in-plain-language) |
| **Native audio** | Sound created by the video model together with the picture. Portuguese speech is a weak spot. | [Video generation](video-generation.md#audio-native-sound-voices-and-music) |
| **Credits** | The "currency" many AI tools use. A credit is not a second of video; each model spends credits at its own rate. | [Video generation](video-generation.md#costs-and-free-tiers) |
| **B-roll** | Supporting footage that plays under a voice or text, such as a 5-second abstract insert. | [Video generation](video-generation.md) |
| **Upscaling** | Enlarging a low-resolution image or clip with software. It is not the same as generating at high resolution. | [Video generation](video-generation.md) |
| **AI dubbing** | Replacing a speaker's voice with a synthetic voice in another language. Needs separate, explicit consent and an AI label. | [Reels, clips & captions](video-repurposing.md#ai-dubbing-and-translation-use-with-care) |
| **Avatar / digital twin / voice cloning** | A synthetic presenter, or a copy of a real person's face or voice. Rarely the right choice for an academic congress. | [Reels, clips & captions](video-repurposing.md#avatars-rarely-the-right-choice-here) |
| **Motion graphics** | Short animations of text, shapes and photos, such as a countdown or an animated speaker card. | [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md) |
| **Kinetic typography** | Words that appear or move in rhythm, good for a short, verified quote. | [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md#kinetic-typography-for-a-quote) |
| **Stop motion** | Movement in visible "jumps" between poses, made from photos of real objects, generated frames or code. | [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md#a-real-stop-motion-workflow-with-image-frames) |
| **Provenance metadata** | Hidden information in a file that records it was AI-generated. Canva's terms forbid removing it. | [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md#1-disclosing-ai-on-facebook-and-instagram) |
| **Connector / permission** | A connection allowing an assistant to use another service. Review scopes; a browser profile does not isolate connected account data. | [Setup](setup-tools.md) |
| **ChatGPT Work / legacy agent / Atlas** | Work is the current multi-step task experience. Legacy ChatGPT agent is unavailable; Atlas is retired. Old /agent or Atlas installation instructions are not current guidance. | [Official retirement](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001371-chatgpt-atlas-retirement) · [Work](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001275-chatgpt-work-and-codex) |
| **Practice task / teach-back** | Try a small real task, then explain the steps in your own words and check against the official source. | [Learning with AI](learning-with-ai.md) |

### Marketing and social media terms

| Term | What it means | Covered in |
|---|---|---|
| **Organic** | Unpaid reach: posts, shares, groups and partners, with no ad budget. Everything on this site is organic. | [Facebook playbook](facebook-playbook.md) |
| **Funnel** | The path from first hearing about the congress to doing the thing you want. CONAFROEDU has two: authors (submissions until 4 Nov) and participants (registrations until 6 Dec). | [Campaign timeline](campaign-timeline.md#two-clocks-two-funnels) |
| **CTA (call to action)** | The one thing a post asks people to do: submit, register, read the program. One per post. | [Copywriting](copywriting.md#the-caption-formula) |
| **Hook** | The first line or first seconds of a post or Reel, which decide whether people keep reading. | [Copywriting](copywriting.md#hook-bank-first-lines) |
| **Caption** | Two meanings: the text of a post, and the subtitles on a video. Portuguese uses *legenda* for both. | [Copywriting](copywriting.md), [Auto-captions](video-repurposing.md#auto-captions-in-portuguese-accessibility) |
| **Alt text** (*texto alternativo*) | A short description of an image that screen readers read aloud to blind and low-vision people. | [Copywriting](copywriting.md#alt-text-describe-whats-actually-there) |
| **Reach, views, interactions** | Reach estimates unique people; views count every play or display (replays included); interactions are reactions, comments, shares and saves. | [Measuring results](measuring-results.md#step-2-read-metas-free-insights) |
| **Engagement rate** | Interactions divided by reach, as a percentage. Only compare rates calculated the same way. | [Measuring results](measuring-results.md) |
| **Engagement bait** | Posts that coerce reactions ("comente SIM", "marque 5 amigos"). Meta demotes them. | [Facebook playbook](facebook-playbook.md#engagement-do-and-dont) |
| **UTM (tagged link)** | Labels added to the end of a link (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) so analytics can show which post or partner sent a visit. | [Measuring results](measuring-results.md#step-1-set-up-your-utm-links) |
| **Link in bio / link page** | On Instagram, caption links aren't clickable, so posts point to the one link in the profile, or to a free page with several links. | [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md#step-2--links-on-instagram) |
| **UGC (user-generated content)** | Content participants make themselves, such as an author's post. Ask before reposting and never fabricate it. | [Speaker & partner kits](speaker-partner-kits.md#user-generated-content-ugc-ask-credit-never-fabricate) |
| **Promo kit** | A small folder (images, suggested captions, a tracked link, the facts) that lets someone share the congress in their own voice. | [Speaker & partner kits](speaker-partner-kits.md) |
| **Ambassador** | A volunteer who agrees to share the event with their own networks, with a personal tracked link. | [Speaker & partner kits](speaker-partner-kits.md#kit-4-ambassadors-and-referral-links) |
| **Social proof** | Evidence that real people value something, such as real acceptances and real testimonials. Never invented. | [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md#the-six-golden-rules) |
| **Batching** | Doing one kind of task many times in a row, such as writing and scheduling a week of posts in one 90-minute session. | [Scheduling tools](scheduling-tools.md#the-weekly-batch-session-90-minutes-once-a-week) |
| **Content calendar / queue** | The plan of what goes out when, and the list of posts already scheduled. One person owns the queue. | [Campaign timeline](campaign-timeline.md#build-your-content-calendar-free-spreadsheet) |
| **Pinned (featured) post** | The post fixed at the top of a Page with the essentials. Update it when the phase changes. | [Facebook playbook](facebook-playbook.md#step-3--optimize-the-page-new-or-existing) |
| **Cross-posting** | Publishing the same piece on several channels. Adapt it rather than copying. | [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md#step-5--cross-post-without-losing-quality) |
| **Aspect ratio (4:5, 1:1, 9:16)** | The shape of an image or video: portrait feed post (1080 × 1350), square (1080 × 1080), full-screen vertical for Reels and Stories (1080 × 1920). | [Image generation](image-generation.md#sizes-for-facebook-and-instagram) |
| **Safe area** | The part of a vertical video that app buttons and captions don't cover. Keep text inside it. | [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md#troubleshooting) |
| **Release** | A short, factual text (100–150 words) that partners or media can reuse. | [Speaker & partner kits](speaker-partner-kits.md#kit-3-partners-neabisneabs-graduate-programs-associations-collectives) |
| **Opt-in / opt-out** | A person's clear choice to receive messages, and their request to stop. Both must be respected. | [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md#consent-and-lgpd-in-practice) |
| **Price tier / early bird** (*lote*, *virada de lote*) | A price that is valid until a date. For CONAFROEDU, the current tier ends on 15 November and new prices start on 16 November. | [Campaign timeline](campaign-timeline.md#key-day-playbooks) |

| **Professional account** | An Instagram business or creator account, which is what usually lets Business Suite schedule posts and show Insights. Switching is free. | [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md#step-1--confirm-the-account-and-connect-it) |
| **AI info label** (*Informações de IA*, check your app) | Meta's label for AI-generated content. Required for realistic AI video and audio. | [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md#1-disclosing-ai-on-facebook-and-instagram) |

| **Community, Status, broadcast list** | Grouped WhatsApp groups; 24-hour personal updates; a message sent individually to up to 256 people who saved your number. | [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md#whatsapp-pick-the-right-tool) |

### Brazilian academic and cultural context (for English-speaking readers)

| Term | What it means | Covered in |
|---|---|---|
| **CONAFROEDU** | *Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação*, a national, 100% online academic congress; the 1st edition takes place on 5–6 December 2026. | [Home](index.md) |
| **Africanidades** | African and Afro-diasporic knowledge, histories and cultures, especially as studied and taught in Brazilian education. | [Representation & bias](representation-bias.md) |
| **ERER** | *Educação para as Relações Étnico-Raciais*, education for ethnic-racial relations. Explain it on first mention. | [Brand kit](brand-kit.md#words-we-use-and-words-we-avoid) |
| **Lei 10.639/2003 and Lei 11.645/2008** | The laws that made Afro-Brazilian history and culture (2003), and then Afro-Brazilian **and Indigenous** history and culture (2008), mandatory in Brazilian schools, through article 26-A of the **LDB** (Lei 9.394/1996, the national education law). | [Representation & bias](representation-bias.md#why-this-matters-especially-for-conafroedu) |
| **20 November** | *Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra*, a national holiday since Lei 14.759/2023. In 2026 it is a Friday. A moment to contribute, not to sell. | [Sprint plan](conafroedu-sprint.md#week-4--mon-16-to-sun-22-nov-novembro-negro-and-20-november) |
| **Novembro Negro** | "Black November": the month of events around 20 November. | [Campaign timeline](campaign-timeline.md) |
| **Dia de Finados** | 2 November, a national holiday (Day of the Dead) that falls two days before the submission deadline in 2026. | [Campaign timeline](campaign-timeline.md#key-day-playbooks) |
| **Educação básica** | Basic education: early childhood, primary and secondary school. *Professoras e professores da educação básica* are school teachers. | [Brand kit](brand-kit.md#who-were-talking-to) |
| **Graduação, licenciatura, pós-graduação** | Undergraduate studies; undergraduate teacher-training degrees; graduate studies (specialization, master's, doctorate). | [Brand kit](brand-kit.md#who-were-talking-to) |
| **PPG / PPGE** | *Programa de Pós-Graduação (em Educação)*: a graduate program in a university, with its own secretariat and student mailing channels. | [Outreach](outreach.md) |
| **NEAB / NEABI** | *Núcleo de Estudos Afro-Brasileiros (e Indígenas)*: Afro-Brazilian (and Indigenous) studies centers at universities and federal institutes. Each one is independent. | [Outreach](outreach.md#who-to-approach-research-leads-not-confirmed-partners) |
| **ABPN / CONNEABS** | The Brazilian Association of Black Researchers, and its national consortium of NEABs, which publishes a directory. | [Outreach](outreach.md#who-to-approach-research-leads-not-confirmed-partners) |
| **ANPEd GT21** | The working group on education and ethnic-racial relations of Brazil's national association for education research. | [Outreach](outreach.md) |
| **Chamada de trabalhos** | Call for papers. Not an *edital*, which is a formal document. | [Copywriting](copywriting.md#3-get-the-vocabulary-right) |
| **Inscrição vs. submissão** | Registering to take part vs. sending a paper for review. Different steps, different deadlines (6 Dec vs. 4 Nov). | [Copywriting](copywriting.md#3-get-the-vocabulary-right) |
| **Modalidades** | Submission formats: *artigo completo* (full paper), *resumo expandido* (extended abstract), *resumo simples* (short abstract; to be confirmed for CONAFROEDU) and *práticas exitosas / relato de experiência* (classroom-practice report). | [Replies & inbox](replies-inbox.md#the-answers-ready-to-paste-in-portuguese) |
| **Parecer, carta de aceite** | The review and the acceptance letter. | [Copywriting](copywriting.md#3-get-the-vocabulary-right) |
| **Eixos temáticos** | Thematic tracks. Copy the event's real track names. | [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#how-to-write-in-natural-brazilian-portuguese-with-ai) |
| **Ouvinte** | A participant who attends without presenting a paper. | [Replies & inbox](replies-inbox.md#the-answers-ready-to-paste-in-portuguese) |
| **Prorrogação** | An extension of a deadline. Only the committee can decide one, and Even3 is updated first. | [Sprint plan](conafroedu-sprint.md#if-the-committee-extends-the-deadline) |
| **ABNT** | Brazil's standards body; its rules govern citation and reference formatting in papers. | [Replies & inbox](replies-inbox.md#the-answers-ready-to-paste-in-portuguese) |
| **Anais** | Conference proceedings: the collection of the event's papers. Not automatically a journal or a Qualis-rated publication. | [Brand kit](brand-kit.md#words-we-use-and-words-we-avoid) |
| **ISBN / DOI** | ISBN (*International Standard Book Number*) identifies a monographic title/edition/format, such as a proceedings volume. DOI (*Digital Object Identifier*) persistently identifies an object, such as a paper, dataset or proceedings. CONAFROEDU separately advertises an individual DOI per work; neither identifier is a quality rating ([DOI Foundation](https://www.doi.org/the-identifier/what-is-a-doi/)). Proceedings target: 15 Dec; fee R$16.98/work. | [FAQ](replies-inbox.md) |
| **Qualis / CAPES** | CAPES is the federal agency that evaluates Brazilian graduate programs; Qualis is its system for classifying scholarly output in that evaluation. CAPES announced in 2025 that the 2025–2028 evaluation shifts toward classifying articles ([CAPES](https://www.gov.br/capes/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/avaliacao-da-producao-intelectual-e-ampliada)). Never promise "Qualis" or "points". | [Brand kit](brand-kit.md#words-we-use-and-words-we-avoid) |
| **MEC** | The Ministry of Education. Never claim "MEC recognition" for the congress or its certificate. | [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#verify-every-output-dates-prices-promises) |
| **Certificado de 40 horas / carga horária** | The advertised 40-hour certificate and its documented workload. The criteria still need committee confirmation. | [Replies & inbox](replies-inbox.md#the-answers-ready-to-paste-in-portuguese) |
| **Horas (atividades) complementares** | Extra-curricular hours that many undergraduate courses require. Whether a certificate counts depends on each course's rules. | [Brand kit](brand-kit.md#who-were-talking-to) |
| **Progressão** | Career advancement for teachers in public school networks. Rules differ by network; never promise it. | [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md#7-responsible-ai-on-an-anti-racist-education-theme) |
| **Lattes (CNPq)** | Brazil's national researcher-CV platform. Useful to discover who researches a topic; not a mailing list. | [Outreach](outreach.md#step-2-find-candidates-with-ai) |
| **PNEERQ** | *Política Nacional de Equidade, Educação para as Relações Étnico-Raciais e Educação Escolar Quilombola*, a federal policy instituted by a 2024 MEC ordinance ([overview](https://www.gov.br/igualdaderacial/pt-br/assuntos/programas-e-projetos/observatorio-de-monitoramento-e-avaliacao/politicas-intergovernamentais/educacao/perguntas-e-respostas/politica-nacional-de-equidade-educacao-para-as-relacoes-etnico-raciais-e-educacao-escolar-quilombola-pneerq)). Attending CONAFROEDU is not a PNEERQ course or credential. | [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md#7-responsible-ai-on-an-anti-racist-education-theme) |
| **Quilombola; Black, African, Afro-Brazilian, Indigenous** | Quilombola communities have self-defined identity, a distinct history and specific territorial relations linked to resistance to historical oppression ([Decreto 4.887/2003, art. 2](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/decreto/2003/d4887.htm)). These terms are not interchangeable; people can appear together, but do not merge their identities or infer them from appearance. | [Representation](representation-bias.md) |
| **Terreiro; Candomblé, Umbanda; orixás** | Places of worship and distinct African-matrix religious traditions, and their deities. Never decoration; consult practitioners. | [Representation & bias](representation-bias.md#religious-and-cultural-symbols-handle-with-care) |
