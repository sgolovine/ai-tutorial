AI image generators can give a volunteer committee something that used to need a designer: backgrounds, illustrations and visual ideas in minutes. They also make confident mistakes, such as misspelled Portuguese, invented logos, wrong dates, and people who look "plausible" but stereotyped. This guide shows you how to get the benefits and avoid the mistakes.

**What you'll get from this page:**

- A plain-language comparison of the current image models (October 2026) and what each one is best for.
- Free tiers, prices and how to pay from Brazil (BRL, Pix).
- Whether you are allowed to use the images to promote the congress.
- The most important habit: **generate the image without text, then add the text in Canva**.
- Verified sizes for Facebook and Instagram.
- Three step-by-step workflows that take under 30 minutes, plus guidance on editing real photos and keeping a consistent look.

> [!NOTE]
> Last verified: 21 October 2026. AI tools change monthly. Prices are snapshots from official pages; your account may see different quotas, taxes or offers. Always check the plan page before paying.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> CONAFROEDU is a congress about Africanidades and anti-racist education. Every image that shows people or cultural symbols must go through the review on [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully](representation-bias.md). Please read that page before you publish your first AI image.

## Why old tutorials may mislead you

Many YouTube videos and blog posts from 2024–2025 are already out of date:

- **DALL·E 2 and DALL·E 3 were retired on 12 May 2026.** OpenAI's current family is **GPT Image 2.5** (variants called *Sunburst* and *Flare*); inside ChatGPT it is simply called **Images** ([OpenAI help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11084440-images-in-chatgpt)).
- **Google's Imagen is shut down in the Gemini API.** Google's current image models are the **Nano Banana** family (Nano Banana 2.1, 2 and Pro), used inside the Gemini app ([Google image guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation)).
- **Midjourney is now on V8.2**, with a new Edit Model. Older "character reference" tutorials describe a different workflow ([Midjourney versions](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32199405667853-Version)).
- New versions also exist for FLUX (FLUX 3 Image), Ideogram (4.5) and Recraft (V4.1).

If a tutorial names a model not listed below, check that it still exists before following it.

## Model comparison: what each one is best for

"Best for" means *the best candidate to try for this campaign*, not a universal winner. No model is guaranteed to spell Portuguese correctly, keep a person's identity or produce the same result twice.

| Model / tool | Best for | Watch out for | Free access? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT Images** (GPT Image 2.5) | Complex illustrations, edits described in ordinary language, poster concepts, transparent backgrounds | Can still misplace text and lose consistency across a series | Yes, on all ChatGPT plans, with limits |
| **Gemini app** (Nano Banana 2.1 / 2 / Pro) | Conversational editing, combining reference images, adapting a background to several crops; the app documents [up to 10 files per prompt](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14903178?hl=en), subject to availability | Reference images do not guarantee perfect likeness | Nano Banana 2 has free access; **2.1 requires a Google AI plan**, per the [image product page](https://gemini.google/overview/image-generation/) |
| **Canva AI** (Magic Media, Dream Lab) | Finishing everything: templates, text, crops, consistent layouts | Underlying models change; free AI uses are limited | Free plan with limited AI uses |
| **Adobe Firefly / Express** (Firefly Image 5) | Generative Fill, extending or fixing a background, photo compositing | Free credits are limited; beta features have separate terms | Express Free plan; limited Firefly credits |
| **Ideogram 4.5** | Typography and lettering concepts, posters, precise edits | Small text and accents still need checking | Variable weekly free credits for eligible accounts |
| **Midjourney V8.2** | Moodboards, expressive art direction, a distinctive campaign look | Weak for exact names and dates; no free trial; images public by default | No |
| **Recraft V4.1** | Vector (SVG) icons and graphic systems | Free-plan images are public, owned by Recraft and **not licensed for commercial use** | Yes, 30 daily credits, prototyping only |
| **FLUX 3 Image / Klein** | Technical teammates building repeatable assets via API | Licenses differ by model; "FLUX is free" is wrong | No dependable free tier |
| **Meta AI** (Imagine) | Quick ideas inside WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook | Commercial terms and the exact model available in Brazil are not confirmed | Meta AI is available free in Brazil |
| **Seedream 5.0, Qwen-Image, Grok Imagine, MAI-Image, Stable Diffusion** | Alternatives for advanced users or a specific look | Rights and Portuguese quality vary by service; some downloadable models are research-only | Varies |

> [!NOTE]
> Leaderboards (for example [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/image/leaderboard/text-to-image)) currently rank OpenAI's GPT Image 2.5 first and Google's Nano Banana 2.1 close behind. These boards measure general human preference. They do **not** test Portuguese accents, commercial rights or respectful representation of Black Brazilians. No model has been shown to be the "best" or bias-free for Afro-Brazilian subjects.

**Our recommendation for this team:** use the free ChatGPT or Gemini app for backgrounds and illustrations, and **Canva Free** to finish every piece. Only consider paying once you know what is slowing you down.

### Can we get Canva for free through education or a nonprofit?

- **Canva for Education** is free only for verified **K–12 (educação básica) teachers** and eligible schools. University lecturers, students and a congress committee do not qualify just by working in education. Even when a teacher qualifies, Education content may only be used for **educational, noncommercial purposes**, so it is not a licence to promote a paid congress ([eligibility](https://www.canva.com/education/eligibility-guidelines/), [content license](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/)).
- **Canva for Nonprofits** offers premium access for up to 50 people to approved charitable organizations with official registration documents ([rules](https://www.canva.com/nonprofits/eligibility-guidelines/)). Universities and professional societies are excluded. Whether CONAFROEDU's legal host qualifies is something to **confirm with the committee**. Keep working with the Free plan while any application is reviewed.

## Commercial use: may we use these images to promote the congress?

CONAFROEDU charges registration fees, so treat every promotional post as **commercial use**, even if you are not paying for ads. "Organic", "free post" and "educational theme" do not create an exemption.

Ask four questions about every image:

1. Does **this plan** of the tool allow commercial/promotional use?
2. Does the **specific model** allow it (some downloadable models are research-only)?
3. Do we have rights to **every input**: photos, logos, fonts, music, people's faces?
4. Does the result avoid copying someone else's work, brand or likeness?

| Tool | Commercial-use position (summary) |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT / OpenAI | OpenAI assigns output rights to you, to the extent the law permits; not necessarily exclusive ([terms](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/)) |
| Gemini API | Google does not claim ownership of generated content; the consumer app has its own terms |
| Midjourney | Paid plans allow commercial use. Images are public unless you use Stealth (Pro/Mega only) |
| Ideogram | Hosted (website/app) outputs: commercial use allowed per its pricing FAQ |
| Recraft | **Free plan: not licensed for commercial use.** Paid plans: yes, and the rights continue after you cancel |
| Adobe Firefly | Non-beta outputs may be used commercially; beta features have separate notices |
| Canva | AI terms and stock/template licences are separate. **Do not remove Canva's provenance metadata** from AI outputs ([AI terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/ai-product-terms/)) |
| Meta AI | Commercial terms for its image features in Brazil are not confirmed; use it only for ideas until checked |

> [!WARNING]
> A "you own it" statement from a vendor does not settle Brazilian copyright. Brazil's copyright law (Lei 9.610/1998) defines an author as a natural person, so the legal status of a purely AI-generated image is unclear. Keep your **human-made layout, text and the real logo** as the core of each design, and never ask an AI to create the CONAFROEDU logo or "a logo like X". More in [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

## Portuguese text in images: generate without text, add it in Canva

This is the single most useful rule on this page.

**What the evidence says:** the August 2026 [LingT2I preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11002v1) examines multilingual generation and text rendering, including Portuguese. Its composite metrics are **not a current pt-BR accent pass rate** and do not identify a reliable winner for this campaign. The practical rule remains: **proofread every letter and keep final text editable**.

Words that commonly break: `Educação`, `Inscrições abertas`, `Participação`, `Consciência Negra`, `História e cultura afro-brasileira`, `5 e 6 de dezembro`, and speakers' full names.

### The recommended pattern

1. **Generate** the illustration or background with the instruction "no words, numbers, letters or logos" and an empty area for the title.
2. **Download** the best version (PNG if possible).
3. **Open Canva** and place the image on your template.
4. **Type** all facts as live text: CONAFROEDU 2026, dates, "100% online", the call to action, names.
5. **Add the real logo** from your brand files, never a generated one.
6. **Proofread** at phone size with a second person, letter by letter.

```prompt
Create an editorial illustration for an online education congress about Africanidades and anti-racist education in Brazil. Vertical 4:5 format. Keep the bottom half for the scene and leave the top half calm and simple for a title that will be added later. Do not include any words, letters, numbers, logos or signatures. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photograph of a real event.
```

> [!TIP]
> On the Portuguese site this prompt is in Portuguese. The models understand both. If you do want to test lettering, put the exact words in quotation marks, ask for very little text, and **if the same line fails twice, stop and type it in Canva instead.** It is faster.

## Sizes for Facebook and Instagram

These sizes were checked against Meta's help pages on 21 October 2026. Some are official rules; others are sensible working sizes the team chooses. The table tells you which is which.

| Asset | Size to design | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post (portrait) / speaker card | **1080 × 1350 px (4:5)** | Recommended working size for both Facebook and Instagram. Instagram also accepts up to 3:4 (1080 × 1440) ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1631821640426723)) |
| Square post / carousel pages | **1080 × 1080 px (1:1)** | Working size; use the same size for every page in a carousel |
| Story / Reel | **1080 × 1920 px (9:16)** | Working size. Instagram Reels accept ratios from 1.91:1 to 9:16, at least 30 FPS and at least 720 px resolution ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1038071743007909)) |
| Reel cover | Keep a high-resolution 9:16 cover | Meta recommends 420 × 654 px but crops it differently in feed and profile; check both |
| Facebook Page cover | **851 × 315 px** (or 1702 × 630 for sharpness) | Official: minimum 400 × 150; fastest loading with sRGB JPG, 851 × 315, under 100 KB. Facebook crops it differently on computer and phone, and the profile photo overlaps on mobile ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/help/125379114252045)) |
| Facebook Event cover | **1920 × 1005 px (≈1.91:1)** | Team choice: Meta publishes **no official pixel size**. Once added, the cover can be repositioned but not resized ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/help/1910675759253872)) |
| Live thumbnail / presentation slide | **1920 × 1080 px (16:9)** | Working size |

> [!TIP]
> Keep the important content (title, date, logo) in the **center** of covers and leave decorative edges that can be cropped. Export cards with text as **PNG** for sharp letters; export photos as **JPG** when file size matters. Do not upscale a small image and call it high resolution.

## How to write an image prompt

Describe the image in this order. You will not need every part every time.

| # | Part | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Purpose** | "Background for a speaker card" |
| 2 | **Subject** | "Adult Black Brazilian educators discussing a lesson plan" |
| 3 | **Composition** | "People in the lower third, empty space at the top" |
| 4 | **Style** | "Paper-cut illustration, simple shapes, subtle texture" |
| 5 | **Colour and light** | Your brand colours (hex codes) and "soft, warm light" |
| 6 | **Shape** | "Vertical 4:5" |
| 7 | **Constraints** | "No words, numbers, logos; no religious symbols" |
| 8 | **References** | "Image A sets the colours; image B sets the layout" |

For photographic scenes, describe **framing and light** ("waist-up, eye level, soft window light") instead of magic words like "ultra-realistic 8K". For illustrations, describe **materials and shapes** ("flat geometric shapes, paper grain, two accent colours"). You can write in Portuguese; it works well. See [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md) for the general method and the [Image prompt cookbook](image-prompts.md) for ready-made prompts.

```prompt
Create an editorial illustration for CONAFROEDU 2026, an online congress on Africanidades and anti-racist education in Brazil. Show adult Black Brazilian educators with different skin tones, ages and hair (coily, curly, braided), in research, teaching and leadership roles. A contemporary scene of knowledge exchange, with books that have no readable text and an abstract online meeting. Do not depict real participants or speakers. Do not add religious symbols, generic "African" patterns or costumes. Style: simple paper-cut shapes, subtle texture, warm and professional. Palette: [your brand colours, e.g. navy #17324D, green #3F8F79, cream #F6F2E9]. Vertical 4:5 composition. Keep the scene in the lower half and the upper half simple for a title. No words, numbers, logos or brands. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photograph of a real event.
```

## Step-by-step: three workflows under 30 minutes

Before you start: be signed in to a free account, and have the approved text and any approved photo ready. If a "Pro" badge or payment screen appears, use the manual alternative; do not start a trial by accident.

### Workflow 1: announcement card, Canva only (20–25 min, R$0)

1. **(0–3 min)** Canva → *Create a design* → *Custom size* → **1080 × 1350 px**. Start blank or with a free social-post template.
2. **(3–7 min)** Upload the approved logo and one real, consented photo of an educator or speaker. A simple rectangular crop is fine; background removal may be a paid feature. No approved photo? Use solid colour and simple shapes.
3. **(7–13 min)** Add live text: `CONAFROEDU 2026`, `Desenvolvimento, Participação e Educação Antirracista`, `5 e 6 de dezembro de 2026`, `100% online`, `Inscreva-se pelo Even3`. Use two text sizes, strong contrast and generous margins. **Do not add a price or deadline you haven't confirmed.**
4. **(13–17 min)** Put the Even3 link (`https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/`) in the post caption and check it opens the right page. Repeat the key information in the caption for accessibility.
5. **(17–22 min)** A second person checks `Participação`, `Educação`, names and dates at phone size. *Share → Download → PNG*. If a premium element blocks the download, replace it with a free one.
6. **(22–25 min)** Duplicate the design and keep it as your **master template**.

### Workflow 2: AI background + Canva finish (25–30 min, R$0)

1. **(0–8 min)** Follow [tool setup](setup-tools.md), then open ChatGPT or the Gemini app. Paste the illustration prompt above, changing the colours to your brand kit. **Maximum two attempts.**
2. **(8–12 min)** Review the image with the [representation checklist](representation-bias.md#pre-publication-review-checklist): who is shown, in what roles, skin tones, hair, any stray symbols or fake letters. If it fails, use a plain background instead.
3. **(12–14 min)** Download the image and upload it to Canva on a 1080 × 1350 design.
4. **(14–22 min)** Add the live text and real logo exactly as in Workflow 1, steps 3–4.
5. **(22–27 min)** Proofread with a second person at phone size. Download PNG.
6. **(27–30 min)** In the caption, add a short label such as "Ilustração criada com IA" ("Illustration created with AI"), and save the prompt and image in your shared folder.

### Workflow 3: one design, every format (15–20 min, R$0)

1. Open your approved 4:5 master in Canva.
2. Create a **new** 1080 × 1920 design for Stories (one-click *Resize* may be a Pro feature, so copy and paste the elements manually).
3. Move the title and date to the middle area, away from the top and bottom where the app's buttons appear.
4. If the background is too short for 9:16, **export only the separate illustration/background**, without text, logos or portraits, and upload only that file to the AI. Extend it with the prompt below, then put it back under the original Canva text, logo and photo layers. If no separate background exists, fill the edges with solid color in Canva instead. A promise to “preserve everything” is not protection for a finished speaker card:

```prompt
Extend this image to a vertical 9:16 format. Continue only the background colours, shapes and texture at the top and bottom. Do not add people, objects, words, numbers or logos. Keep everything already in the image unchanged.
```

5. Repeat for a 1920 × 1005 Event cover with the title centered.
6. Preview every version on a phone before publishing.

## Editing real photos (speakers, lives and recaps)

CONAFROEDU 2026 is the **first edition**, so there are no past-CONAFROEDU photos. Never generate images that look like a previous edition or a crowded audience. The real photos you will have are speakers' portraits, screenshots from online sessions (after 5–6 December), and, only with explicit permission, material from related events.

**Order of preference for photo edits:**

1. **Ordinary edits first:** crop, straighten, exposure, white balance, light noise reduction. Canva and Adobe Express Free do this without AI.
2. **Redact** (blur or cover) names, chat messages, emails and faces of people who did not consent in session screenshots.
3. **Masked AI fixes only for non-factual background defects**, such as a stain on a wall, and only when justified.

```prompt
Photo 1 is an authorized photograph. Edit ONLY the empty area of the wall marked in the mask to fix an uneven light spot. Keep all pixels outside the marked area unchanged where the tool allows. Do not change people, faces, skin tone, hair, clothing, the number of people, signs, logos or objects. Keep the framing and the documentary look of the photograph.
```

> [!WARNING]
> That instruction is a request, not a guarantee. Compare the original and edited versions side by side at full size. If the tool changed a face, skin tone, hair texture or the number of people, discard the edit and use ordinary retouching. Never "improve" a photo by adding people, diversity or attendance; that turns a document into a fabrication.

Also remember: permission to publish a portrait does **not** automatically include permission to upload it to an AI service. Ask the speaker. Do not upload children's faces or private attendee lists to any generator (see [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md)).

## Keeping a consistent look across the campaign

A coherent series looks professional even when the AI model changes. Consistency comes from your **templates**, not from the AI.

1. **Make a one-page style sheet** (see [Build your brand kit](brand-kit.md)): two main colours plus one accent (with hex codes), two fonts that support Portuguese accents, logo files, margins, photo treatment and three approved card templates.
2. **Save one approved illustration as a style reference** and reuse it.
3. **Keep the same layout** for the whole speaker series; only the photo, name and topic change.
4. **Reuse one background across several posts and crops** instead of generating a new one each time.
5. **Return to the approved master.** Repeated edits on an edit slowly degrade the image.

```prompt
Use image A as a style reference: same palette, paper texture, shape thickness and lighting. Create a new scene about continuing teacher education. Do not copy the characters or the exact composition. Keep the empty space at the top and do not include any text.
```

> [!NOTE]
> For real speakers, "consistency" means **preserving the real person**, never generating a "similar-looking" replacement.

## Iterating: how to fix a result without starting over

1. **Write the facts first.** Keep an approved text block (name, dates, online format, Even3 link). See the context block in [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md).
2. **Generate two or three cheap concepts** before asking for maximum quality.
3. **Fix one problem per message.** "Move the shapes to the right" works better than "make it better".
4. **Give references clear roles.** "Image A controls the colours; image B the layout."
5. **Add text and logo in the editor**, kept editable until final export.
6. **Make each format deliberately** and check that extended edges did not invent details.
7. **Proof at phone size and full size**: smallest text, eyes, hands, stray letters, the registration link.

More fix-it prompts are in the [Image prompt cookbook](image-prompts.md#fix-it-prompts-iteration).

## Disclosure: telling people when AI was used

Meta applies an "AI info" label to content it detects as AI-generated, and **requires you to use its disclosure tool** for organic posts with photorealistic video or realistic audio that was digitally created or altered ([Meta policy](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)). For still illustrations, add a short caption line when someone might mistake the image for a real event or real people:

- *"Ilustração criada com IA; não retrata participantes do evento."*
- *"Arte: [nome do/da artista]. Fundo gerado com IA."*

There is no evidence that a label reduces reach, and a label does not make a misleading or stereotyped image acceptable.

## Checklist before you publish an image

- [ ] All facts (dates, "100% online", prices, names) match the approved fact sheet; nothing invented.
- [ ] Every Portuguese word and accent was checked by a person at phone size.
- [ ] All text and the logo are live layers added in Canva/Express, not generated pixels.
- [ ] The image passed the [representation checklist](representation-bias.md#pre-publication-review-checklist).
- [ ] No fake audience, fake past edition, fake testimonial or invented partner logo.
- [ ] Real people's photos have permission for this use (and for AI upload, if any).
- [ ] The tool and plan allow commercial use (not Recraft Free, not Canva Education content).
- [ ] The crop was previewed in the real Facebook/Instagram publishing screen.
- [ ] AI use is disclosed where needed; artists and photographers are credited.
- [ ] The editable design, the prompt and the source/licence information are saved in the shared folder.
