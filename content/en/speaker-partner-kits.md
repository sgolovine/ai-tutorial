**What you'll get from this page:** four ready-to-build promo kits for CONAFROEDU 2026 (accepted authors, speakers, partner institutions and volunteer ambassadors). You'll also get step-by-step instructions to make them in Canva Free, copy-paste AI prompts, the Even3 features that help (and the ones that aren't confirmed yet), and a plan for collecting user-generated content (UGC) honestly.

A **promo kit** is a small folder with everything someone needs to share the congress in their own voice: an image or two, a short video if possible, two or three suggested captions, the right link, and the facts. You prepare it, they decide whether and how to use it.

> [!TIP]
> The cheapest audience you can reach is the audience other people already have. One teacher sharing "my paper was accepted" with their school's WhatsApp group can reach people your Page never will, and their colleagues will trust it more than an ad.

## Why kits matter so much for CONAFROEDU

CONAFROEDU is a first edition; the committee must check its actual Facebook audience. Its real distribution network is the people already involved:

- **Accepted authors.** Each work can have up to six authors (according to the published rules). Every acceptance is a real, positive piece of news that authors *want* to share with their colleagues, graduate program (PPG), school and family.
- **Speakers.** They bring authority and their own academic and teaching networks.
- **Partner institutions.** NEABIs/NEABs, education graduate programs, associations, unions and collectives reach exactly the right audience through channels they control.
- **Ambassadors.** A handful of motivated teachers and students can carry the invitation into school and campus groups that the committee can't (and shouldn't) enter uninvited.

None of this is a guaranteed result. It's a sensible bet for a no-budget campaign: make sharing easy, accurate and optional, then [measure what actually brings registrations](measuring-results.md).

- Dates: **5–6 December 2026**, **100% online**.
- Registration and rules: [Even3 event page](https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/).
- Submissions (and presenter video) close **4 November 2026**. Current ticket tier ends **15 Nov**; prices rise **16 Nov**. Program by **25 Nov**. Certificates from **6 Dec**. Proceedings target **by 15 Dec**.

## Kit 1: Accepted authors ("Meu trabalho foi aprovado no CONAFROEDU!")

### Why this kit comes first

Timing. Submissions close on 4 November and the event promises a review result within five business days of each submission. That means most acceptances will land between now and mid-November, which is exactly when the campaign shifts from *recruiting papers* to *filling the audience*. Each author's post is genuine social proof from a real person: no invented testimonial, no stock crowd.

### What goes in the kit

1. **Feed card** (1080 × 1350 px, portrait 4:5) with editable fields:
   - "Trabalho aprovado" (headline)
   - Author name(s), exactly as registered
   - Title of the work (optional; the author decides)
   - Modality: artigo completo, resumo expandido, resumo simples or práticas exitosas
   - "I CONAFROEDU — Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação"
   - "5 e 6 de dezembro de 2026 · 100% on-line"
2. **Story version** (1080 × 1920 px) with the same text, rearranged, and space at the top and bottom free of text (app buttons cover those areas).
3. **Two caption options**: one for use *before* 4 Nov (can invite colleagues to submit) and one for *after* 4 Nov (invites them to attend).
4. **A tracked link** (see [tracking links](#tracking-links-for-every-kit) below).
5. **Three lines of instructions**: how to fill in the card, where to post, and that sharing is entirely optional.

### Wording rules: be precise, not inflated

| Write | Don't write (yet) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Meu trabalho foi aprovado no I CONAFROEDU" | "Publicado nos anais com DOI" | Proceedings are a target for 15 Dec; acceptance does not prove publication. Announce publication only once it exists, and use only a DOI confirmed by the publisher ([Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/research-nexus/pending-publications/)) |
| "Vou participar do congresso em 5 e 6/12" | "Vou apresentar às 14h no dia 5" | The official program comes by 25 Nov; current entries are provisional |
| "Trabalho aprovado na modalidade resumo expandido" | "Qualis A", "vale pontos" | ISBN/DOI do not establish a Qualis rating; recognition depends on each institution |
| Names of co-authors who agreed | Names of co-authors who weren't asked | Each person named should agree before the post goes out |

### How to deliver it: the cheapest path first

**Option A: copy-generating template, if available.** Never share edit access to the master. If your plan offers a template link that gives each person a separate copy, test it with another account first. If unavailable, send the blank PNG in option B. A normal edit link changes the shared master ([Canva sharing help](https://www.canva.com/help/share-via-link-or-email/)).

**Option B: blank image.** Export a PNG with an empty band where the author's name would go. Authors add their name with the text tool in Instagram, WhatsApp Status or any editor.

**Option C: personalized by the committee.** Only on request and only for a few people. It doesn't scale for a volunteer team.

**How to send it:** Even3 lets organizers send notifications to groups of people, including authors ([Help: notify people](https://ajuda.even3.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/115003452963-Notificar-pessoas-pela-plataforma)), and its submission module has acceptance letters and email templates ([Help: configure submissions](https://ajuda.even3.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/115003706743-Configurar-as-submiss%C3%B5es-do-meu-evento)). Add one short line with the kit link to the acceptance message. Keep it to an optional invitation, not a demand.

### Step by step in Canva Free (about 25 minutes, R$ 0)

1. Open Canva → **Criar um design** → **Tamanho personalizado** → **1080 × 1350 px**.
2. Apply the event's colors and fonts from your [brand kit](brand-kit.md). Add the real CONAFROEDU logo, not an AI-generated one.
3. Add the fixed text: "I CONAFROEDU", the theme, "5 e 6 de dezembro de 2026 · 100% on-line".
4. Add the editable fields as clearly marked placeholders: `[SEU NOME]`, `[TÍTULO DO TRABALHO]`, `[MODALIDADE]`.
5. Use high contrast (dark text on light background or the reverse) and at least two text sizes. Check it at phone size.
6. Ask a second volunteer to check every accent: *Participação*, *Educação*, *Antirracista*, *Africanidades*.
7. **Duplicate** the page and rearrange the elements on a new **1080 × 1920** design for Stories (one-click resize may be a paid feature).
8. Share the **tested copy-generating template link**, or export and send the blank PNG. Do not send the master edit link.

> [!NOTE]
> If you'd like a decorative background, you can generate a text-free one with an AI image tool, but a solid color with simple shapes is safer and faster. If you use AI imagery, read [Representing Black & African Cultures Respectfully](representation-bias.md) first; avoid generic "African" patterns and sacred symbols as decoration.

### Prompt: personalize captions for an author

```prompt
You are helping an author write a short, personal social post in Brazilian Portuguese.
Facts (do not add anything else): the author's work "[TITLE]" ([MODALITY]) was accepted at the I CONAFROEDU – Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação, 5–6 December 2026, 100% online. Registration link: [TRACKED LINK]. Today is [DATE]; paper submissions close on 4 November 2026 (only mention submissions if today is on or before that date).
Write 3 options of 50–90 words in natural, warm pt-BR, first person, for Facebook/Instagram and WhatsApp Status:
1) proud and personal; 2) focused on why the topic matters for schools; 3) a short invitation to colleagues.
Do not claim publication, DOI, Qualis, presentation time or certificate hours. Do not ask for likes or shares. Use at most 2 hashtags (#CONAFROEDU #EducaçãoAntirracista).
```

> [!NOTE]
> The prompt is in English so you can read it, but the output is in Portuguese because that's what the authors will post. The pt-BR version of this page has the prompt fully in Portuguese.

## Kit 2: Speakers

### Who gets it

Only **confirmed** speakers. The program on Even3 currently lists named speakers for 5 December, but the official program is due by 25 November, and generic entries still overlap some slots. Confirm each person's participation, name spelling, title, bio and photo permission before building their kit.

### What goes in the kit

- [ ] Event name, 5–6 December 2026, online, confirmed session time in **Brasília time** (only once confirmed), one-sentence purpose.
- [ ] Short bio (60 words) and long bio (120 words), approved by the speaker.
- [ ] Approved headshot, with permission for this use.
- [ ] Portrait card (1080 × 1350) and Story (1080 × 1920).
- [ ] A 15–30-second vertical video invitation recorded by the speaker on their phone (see below).
- [ ] Alt-text suggestion for each image and corrected captions for the video.
- [ ] Three optional captions in natural pt-BR: a personal invitation, a practical teaching problem, a factual deadline.
- [ ] Their tracked link.
- [ ] Instructions for an Instagram **Collab** post, if they want one.
- [ ] What's not public yet (embargoes) and a contact for corrections.
- [ ] An explicit line: "Adapte ao seu jeito de falar." (Adapt it to your own voice.)

### Step by step

1. **Ask first.** Send a short message through the contact the speaker already uses with the committee. Ask what they're comfortable sharing and how much time they have. A single useful post is better than five identical posters.
2. **Write manually by default.** AI may use only a minimal professional bio approved for processing by the chosen committee-approved service, with the privacy conditions in [ethics](ethics-legal.md). Permission to publish alone does not authorize external AI processing. Never paste a full CV, contacts or sensitive details; do not let AI fill gaps from memory.
3. **Make the portrait card without changing the person.** Use the approved photo with a normal crop. Don't use AI "enhance", background replacement or beauty filters. These can lighten skin, smooth features or alter hair. Compare any edit side by side with the original.
4. **Ask for a phone video.** Suggested script: "Olá, eu sou [nome], da [instituição]. No CONAFROEDU, nos dias 5 e 6 de dezembro, vou falar sobre [tema]. Uma pergunta que vamos discutir é: [pergunta]. As inscrições estão no Even3." Window light, quiet room, phone vertical. Speak any essential slide/screen text or visual demonstration aloud.
5. **Add accurate captions** to the video (see [Reels, Clips, Captions & Dubbing](video-repurposing.md)) and check every name.
6. **Send the kit** in one message with a folder link (Google Drive or similar), not ten separate files.
7. **Suggest a light schedule:** reveal post, one useful answer (the video), a final invitation in the last week.
8. **Offer a Collab.** On Instagram, a Collab post appears on both profiles once the invited account accepts ([Meta: Collabs](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/music-and-collabs-on-instagram/)). Check the current composer for limits. A Collab is co-authorship; ask, don't assume.

### Prompt: speaker bio from approved sources

```prompt
Use ONLY the source text below. Write two bios of [FULL NAME, exactly as written] for the CONAFROEDU 2026 program: 60 words and 120 words, third person, in natural Brazilian Portuguese.
Then give me a table "fact → exact sentence in the source".
Mark anything unclear as [CONFIRMAR]. Do not add degrees, positions, awards, institutions, racial self-identification or publications that are not in the source. Do not correct the spelling of names.
Source: [PASTE ONLY MINIMAL PROFESSIONAL BIO APPROVED FOR THIS EXTERNAL AI PROCESSING; OTHERWISE STOP AND WRITE MANUALLY]
```

### Prompt: the full speaker kit drafts

```prompt
Prepare draft materials for a promo kit for confirmed speaker [NAME].
Approved topic: [TOPIC]. Event facts: [PASTE FACT SHEET]. What we really know about their audience: [INFO or "unknown"]. Agreed benefits for the speaker: [DESCRIBE or "none"].
Deliver in Brazilian Portuguese: (1) a short personal invitation caption, (2) a caption built around one useful idea from their topic, (3) a factual caption with date and link, (4) a 30-second vertical video script in their voice, (5) a short message they could forward to a colleague or institution.
For a benefit-for-post arrangement, check eligibility and use Meta’s paid partnership label plus clear caption disclosure; Collab does not replace it. Do not claim support from the institution where they work. Do not invent credentials or quotes. Do not write as if they already approved. Mark where the tracked link goes. Everything will be reviewed by the team and by the speaker.
```

## Kit 3: Partners (NEABIs/NEABs, graduate programs, associations, collectives)

Partner institutions are your highest-relevance channel: a NEABI sharing the call with its teacher-training list reaches people already working on ERER (education for ethnic-racial relations). Use [AI-Assisted Outreach & Partnerships](outreach.md) to find and approach them. This kit is what you send *after* someone says yes.

### What goes in the packet

1. **A 100–150-word release in pt-BR**, factual and complete: what, when, format, audience, deadlines, costs, link, contact.
2. **A short WhatsApp text** that works when forwarded (one paragraph, one link).
3. **One square card (1080 × 1080)** and **one vertical card (1080 × 1920)**, readable without the caption.
4. **Links:** the rules/registration page and their tracked link.
5. **Transparent fees:** it's a paid event (currently R$ 55,00–70,00 depending on category, until 15 Nov; R$ 60,00–75,00 from 16 Nov; **confirm at checkout**), and publication costs an extra R$ 16,98 per work. Hiding the price damages trust with academic partners.
6. **Real contact:** the committee's WhatsApp and email.

### Rules for partner kits

- **Tailor the first sentence** to the partner's real work: teacher training, curriculum, school management, literature, quilombola education. The rest can stay standard.
- **A partner sharing is not an endorsement.** Don't use any institution's logo without written authorization, and don't call them "apoiador" or "parceiro oficial" unless they agreed to that exact role. The scientific committee lists people from several universities; those are personal affiliations, not institutional sponsorship.
- **Facebook Event co-host** is a management relationship. Only invite a partner as co-host if they agree and know what they can edit (see [Facebook Events & Groups](facebook-events-groups.md)).
- **Ask for one modest action**, e.g. "could you forward this to your teacher-training list?" Don't ask for staff or student contact lists.
- **Offer something useful back:** a speaker conversation, a resource, or a few complimentary registrations *if the committee approves them* (free places have a real cost).

Suggested request to a group or list administrator (adapt before sending):

```text
Olá, sou [nome], da organização do CONAFROEDU. O evento on-line de 5 e 6/12 trata de [tema relacionado ao grupo]. A chamada informa valores e regras na Even3; as submissões vão até 4/11. Vocês aceitam divulgação de congressos externos pagos? Posso enviar um texto curto para avaliação da moderação?
```

### Prompt: adapt the packet to each partner

```prompt
Adapt the approved release below for [PARTNER NAME], whose real work is [ACTIVITY, with source URL].
Rewrite only the opening (max. 2 sentences) to connect honestly with that work. Keep every date, price, link and condition exactly as written. Do not claim any partnership, endorsement or previous relationship.
Then produce: (a) a WhatsApp version under 400 characters with one link; (b) an email subject line.
Output in Brazilian Portuguese. Approved release: [PASTE]
```

## Kit 4: Ambassadors and referral links

An ambassador program is just a small, organized group of people who already like the event and agree to share it.

1. **Start small:** 5–10 people, e.g. a basic-education teacher, a licenciatura student rep, a postgraduate student, a school coordinator and a member of a partner NEABI.
2. **Agree on a light commitment**, for example: one Feed post, one WhatsApp Status and one message to a group they already belong to, over the whole campaign.
3. **Give each one a personal tracked link** (and a coupon, only if the committee approves one).
4. **Write a one-page brief:** facts, deadlines, what not to promise (certificate credit, Qualis, progression), how to reply to questions ("send them to the official WhatsApp"), and the contact for doubts.
5. **Disclose benefits and use the platform control.** If promotion is exchanged for complimentary admission, payment or another benefit, check eligibility and use Meta’s **paid partnership label**, plus caption disclosure. Collab does not replace the label. Resolve availability before agreeing to the arrangement ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/fr/news/2023/02/comment-meta-accompagne-les-createurs-de-contenus-et-les-acteurs-de-linfluence-en-ligne/)).
6. **Reward real help, not vanity.** Don't reward likes, comments, tagging friends or adding people to groups. Thank people publicly (with permission) and share results with them.

> [!CAUTION]
> Don't run prize draws or contests ("marque 5 amigos para concorrer") without checking the legal requirements first. Promotional contests can have specific legal rules in Brazil, and engagement bait is penalized by Facebook. A clear, deterministic benefit (e.g. a courtesy registration for each ambassador) is simpler and fairer.

### Prompt: ambassador brief

```prompt
Write a one-page brief in Brazilian Portuguese for volunteer ambassadors of the CONAFROEDU 2026 congress.
Facts: [PASTE FACT SHEET]. Their agreed commitment: [DESCRIBE]. Benefits they receive: [DESCRIBE or "none"].
Include: why their help matters (2 sentences), the key dates, what to share and where, 3 short caption examples in different tones, what NOT to promise (certificate recognition, Qualis, career progression, scarcity), how to handle questions (redirect to the official WhatsApp (19) 98816-7751 or conapsuporte@gmail.com), and a reminder to disclose any benefit, check branded-content eligibility and use Meta’s paid partnership label for benefit-for-post arrangements. Warm, clear, no marketing jargon.
```

## User-generated content (UGC): ask, credit, never fabricate

**UGC** is content that participants create themselves: an author's post, a teacher's takeaway, a photo of their study corner during the online congress. It's the most credible content you can share, *if* you treat it properly.

### The rules

1. **Ask before reposting.** A public post is not permission to reuse it in your Page, Reels or next year's campaign.
2. **Credit the person** the way they prefer (name, @handle, institution, or nothing).
3. **Don't edit their words.** AI can help you *summarize* for internal use, but published quotes must be exact and approved.
4. **Never fabricate.** No invented testimonials, no AI-generated "participants", no fake screenshots, no "previous edition" photos (there is no previous edition).
5. **Be careful with screenshots of online sessions:** a video-call gallery shows other people's faces and names. Crop to the speaker, or ask everyone visible.
6. **Keep a permission record:** who, what, where it may be used, until when.

### UGC across the campaign

| Phase | Dates | What to invite | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acceptances | Now – mid-Nov | "Trabalho aprovado" posts | Share (with permission) in Stories; thank authors |
| Countdown | 26 Nov – 4 Dec | "Estou inscrita/o, vou participar" posts, preparation photos | Answer, reshare a few, avoid flooding |
| Event days | 5–6 Dec | Takeaways, quotes from talks (attributed), study-corner photos | One person monitors #CONAFROEDU and mentions; ask permission before reposting |
| After | 7–15 Dec | Certificate posts (from 6 Dec), reflections, application stories | Thank contributors; request testimonials *with permission*; share the proceedings link only when actually published |

### Permission request (pt-BR)

```text
Oi, [nome]! Aqui é [pessoa], da organização do CONAFROEDU. Adoramos sua publicação sobre [assunto]. Podemos compartilhá-la no perfil do congresso (Facebook e Instagram), com crédito para você como [@perfil / nome]? Não vamos alterar seu texto. Se preferir que não, tudo bem, é só avisar.
```

## Checklist before you send any kit

- [ ] Every date, price and link matches the current Even3 page (checked today).
- [ ] Nothing unconfirmed is stated as fact (cut-off time, presentation rule, 40-hour conditions, coupons).
- [ ] Names, titles and accents copied exactly and checked by a second person.
- [ ] Photo and video permissions recorded for this use.
- [ ] No AI retouching of real people; any AI illustration is labeled and reviewed (see [representation-bias](representation-bias.md)).
- [ ] Tracked link for each person/partner, tested on a phone.
- [ ] Essential information also appears in the caption text, not only inside the image; alt text suggested.
- [ ] Captions say "adapte ao seu jeito"; sharing is optional.
- [ ] Benefits disclosed in the caption and Meta paid-partnership eligibility/control checked for exchanges.
- [ ] After 4 Nov: submission captions removed from kits and folders.
