## Two clocks, two funnels

The single most important idea for this campaign: **CONAFROEDU has two separate deadlines aimed at two different groups of people.** Mixing them up confuses everyone.

| | Funnel 1: **Authors** | Funnel 2: **Participants** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Postgraduate students, research groups, NEABIs/NEABs, ERER researchers, teachers documenting a practice | Basic-education teachers, undergraduates, school managers, interested researchers |
| Journey | discover → read the rules → register (the responsible author must be registered) → submit → review → corrections → presentation/publication | discover → see program and conditions → register/pay on Even3 → receive access → take part |
| Deadline | **4 November 2026** (submissions and presenter video) | **6 December 2026** (registrations) |
| Main call to action | "Leia as normas e submeta seu trabalho" | "Inscreva-se para participar em 5 e 6 de dezembro" |
| Link tag | `utm_campaign=conafroedu_2026_submissoes` | `utm_campaign=conafroedu_2026_inscricoes` |
| What to count | Completed submissions (not author tickets bought) | Confirmed registrations, then actual attendance |

After 4 November, **stop recruiting submissions** unless the committee formally extends the deadline. From then on, the campaign is about attendance.

This full plan needs roughly **12–17 combined person-hours/week**; reduce output using the [5–7-hour option](facebook-playbook.md) if needed. Proposed times are Brasília-time test slots (see [Facebook playbook](facebook-playbook.md)). Adjust to the team's capacity — cut posts before cutting replies.

### Key-day playbooks

**The submission deadline (4 November)**

1. **Wed 28 Oct (T−7 days):** "Uma semana para submeter" + checklist of formats and rules.
2. **Mon 2 Nov (T−48 h):** practical checklist post (file format, registration of the responsible author, same e-mail for registration and submission). Note: 2 November is Dia de Finados, a national holiday — schedule the post rather than counting on someone being online.
3. **Tue 3 Nov (T−24 h):** one final reminder in approved channels. Only say "faltam 24 horas" if the closing time is confirmed.
4. **Wed 4 Nov:** a morning post + an announced **help window** ("Das 9h às 12h, respondemos dúvidas sobre submissão no WhatsApp"). Nothing after closing time.
5. **Thu 5 Nov:** switch everything: pinned post, Event, Stories highlights, partner kits and **queued scheduled posts** that still mention submissions.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never say "improrrogável" or "última chance" unless the committee has decided there will be no extension. And never invent an extension "a pedidos" — only announce one after the committee approves it **and** Even3 is updated (template below).

**The price change (15/16 November)**

- **Tue 10 Nov:** "O valor atual vale até domingo, 15/11." Check prices in the checkout first.
- **Sat 14 Nov:** short Story/WhatsApp reminder.
- **Mon 16 Nov:** update prices everywhere they appear. Don't call the new tier "sold out" or invent scarcity.

**20 November — Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra**

It's a Friday and a national holiday. Many educators are busy with school and community actions, so **don't expect high activity**, and don't make the day about selling.

- Publish **one substantive educational contribution**: for example, a carousel "20 de novembro no calendário; ERER no cotidiano" explaining [Lei 10.639/2003](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/2003/l10.639.htm), [Lei 11.645/2008](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2007-2010/2008/lei/l11645.htm) and the holiday law, ending with one practical curriculum question; or a short video of a Black educator (credited, with consent) explaining a classroom practice.
- If you invite people to the congress, do it in a **separate, brief** post — no discounts or pressure on the commemorative post.
- No fabricated quotations, tokenistic stock images, invented partnerships or "celebrate by buying" framing. See [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully](representation-bias.md).

**Program release (by 25 November)** — publish the confirmed program on the Page, Event and WhatsApp; then make 2–3 "itinerary" posts by audience. If it's late, say so honestly.

**Final days (3–6 December)** — Thu 3: Page post with program, access route and Even3 link. Fri 4: updated FAQ and Stories. Sat–Sun: welcome, real schedule changes, support. Assign roles: one person on social replies, one on WhatsApp support, one capturing authorized moments. No hourly countdowns, no mass tagging, no DMs to Event respondents.

**After (7–15 December)** — thank speakers, authors, partners and participants by name only with permission; share certificate steps; publish the proceedings link when it exists (never a fake link or invented DOI).

## Part 1 — The reusable timeline (for future editions)

Use this for the next CONAFROEDU, or any academic congress. "T" is the first day of the event. This is a recommended rhythm based on practice, not a measured benchmark — start earlier when you need academic review and institutional approvals.

| Phase | When (relative to T) | What to communicate | Distribution & measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | T−24 to T−16 weeks | Audience, purpose, dates and format; fact sheet; permissions; registration page | Set up Page, Event, support contact and tagged links |
| 2. Announcement | T−16 to T−12 | Why the congress matters; who should come; what's confirmed | Ask real partners to share; watch relevant visits |
| 3. Call for papers | Early — according to review needs | Themes, eligibility, formats, selection, deadline, costs, accessibility | Separate submission link from registration link; count eligible submissions |
| 4. Speaker reveals | T−12 to T−6 | One useful idea per speaker and why they can speak to it | Speaker kit, Instagram Collab, relevant groups |
| 5. Early bird / price tier | A real, agreed sales period | Exact price, conditions and change date, plus program value | Reminder ~1 week before and near the change; count registrations |
| 6. Program depth | T−8 to T−3 | Tracks, examples, "itineraries" per audience | Schools, universities, subject communities |
| 7. Objection handling | T−4 to T−2 | Online access, time, payment, certificate conditions, accessibility | Pin the FAQ; track recurring questions |
| 8. Countdown | T−14 days to T−1 | Practical preparation and access | More frequent useful updates; moderation coverage |
| 9. Live event | T0 | Welcomes, real schedule changes, approved clips, participant takeaways | Dedicated capture and moderation roles |
| 10. First recap | T+1 to T+3 days | Thanks, highlights, when resources arrive | Ask permission before reusing participant content |
| 11. Resources & evaluation | T+1 to T+2 weeks | Certificates, proceedings, corrected captions, feedback | Review registrations vs. attendance, partner results, effort |
| 12. Continuing relationship | T+2 to T+6 weeks | Stories of people applying what they learned; next-edition interest | Respect the original consent; ask for new opt-in if needed |

### Work backwards from real lead times

A future edition should start the call for papers much earlier than this year's. To plan, start from the event date and subtract:

1. **Proceedings and certificates** need time after the event (this year: certificates from the last day, proceedings by T+10 days).
2. **Program publication** needs final acceptances (this year: T−10 days).
3. **Review + corrections + presenter videos** need weeks. This year, submissions closed at T−31 days and a final-day submission could get its review result *after* the video deadline. **Next time, close submissions earlier and set the video deadline after review results.**
4. **Institutional sharing** (university news portals, NEABI lists, graduate program e-mails) often needs 2–4 weeks of lead time — ask early.
5. **Facebook Live** needs an account at least 60 days old and a Page with 100 followers (see [Facebook playbook](facebook-playbook.md)). Build the Page months ahead.

## Build your content calendar (free spreadsheet)

Use Google Sheets or any spreadsheet the team already has. One row per piece of content.

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| ID | `FB-2026-11-04-01` |
| Date / time (BRT) | 04/11/2026 09:00 |
| Channel | Facebook Page (+ IG, WhatsApp Channel) |
| Funnel | submissoes / inscricoes / institucional |
| Phase | Call for papers — deadline |
| Audience | Postgraduate students |
| Objective | Help authors finish a correct submission |
| Format | Multi-photo checklist |
| Hook | "Última revisão antes de submeter" |
| CTA + tagged link | Normas e submissão → `...utm_content=checklist_final_01` |
| Source facts (version) | Ficha oficial v3, 22/10 |
| Owner / reviewer | Ana / Comissão científica |
| Permission status | Speaker image authorized ✔ |
| Status | idea → draft → needs review → approved → scheduled → published → checked |
| Published URL | — |
| Result after 48 h / 7 days | Clicks, questions, submissions |

Rules that prevent embarrassing mistakes:

- **One ID per piece**, reused through drafts, scheduling and results — avoids duplicate posts.
- A queued item is not a published one: after the scheduled time, open the post and save its URL.
- Every Monday, filter for rows that mention a deadline that has passed, and fix them.

To schedule approved rows for free, see [Scheduling tools](scheduling-tools.md) (Meta Business Suite covers Facebook and Instagram).

## If the committee extends the deadline

Only after the scientific committee decides **and** the submission system is updated. Then update pinned posts, the Event, Stories highlights, partner kits and every queued post at once. Template (pt-BR — not a real announcement):

```text
A Comissão Científica do CONAFROEDU prorrogou o prazo de submissão de [data anterior] para [nova data], às [horário] (horário de Brasília). [Modalidades e condições mantidas/alteradas, conforme decisão.] Quem já enviou não precisa reenviar [somente se confirmado]. Normas e envio: [link atualizado].
```

Announce it once, then one factual closing reminder. Repeated extensions weaken trust.

## AI prompts that build the calendar for you

Paste the fact sheet into every prompt. Ask for a table you can paste into a spreadsheet. Then a person checks every date against Even3.

> [!NOTE]
> These English prompts ask for Portuguese hooks and captions, because the team posts in pt-BR. The Portuguese version of this page has every prompt in Portuguese.

### 1. Reusable calendar for a future edition

```prompt
You are a campaign planner for an online academic congress in Brazil (education, anti-racist education, Africanidades).
Event dates: [T]. Submission deadline: [date]. Review turnaround: [days]. Presenter video deadline: [date]. Price tier changes: [dates]. Program release: [date]. Registration closes: [date].
Team: [number] volunteers, [hours] total hours per week. Main channel: Facebook Page + Event. Support: Instagram and a WhatsApp Channel. No ad budget.

1) Check the dates for problems (e.g., video deadline before review results, submissions closing too close to the event) and list them first.
2) Build a week-by-week plan from T−24 weeks to T+6 weeks using these phases: foundation, announcement, call for papers, speaker reveals, price tier, program depth, objection handling, countdown, live event, recap, resources, continuing relationship.
3) Keep two funnels separate: authors (submissions) and participants (registrations). After the submission deadline, no submission CTAs.
4) Maximum 4 Facebook posts per week, including at most 2 videos. Include time for replies and human review.
Output a table: week, dates, phase, funnel, Facebook posts, Event update, Instagram, WhatsApp, partner action, main metric. Write hooks in natural Brazilian Portuguese.
Do not invent speakers, prices, partners, testimonials or observances. Mark anything without evidence as HYPOTHESIS.
```

```prompt
- 20 Nov: one sourced educational contribution ending with an educational question. Any congress invitation goes in a separate optional post; no sales CTA or discount in the educational piece.
- Reuse C01–C03 on 26/28/30 Oct (submission how-to, seven-day checklist, classroom Reel); do not add another 28 Oct reminder. Price reminders: Stories/Channel on 10 and 14 Nov.
```

### 3. Weekly refresh (every Monday)

```prompt
Here is last week's content calendar with results (aggregated numbers only, no personal data) and next week's planned rows.
Last week: [paste rows with clicks, questions, registrations/submissions].
Next week: [paste rows]. Today's date: [date]. Approved facts: [paste].

1) Flag any row that mentions a deadline that has passed or a fact that changed (e.g., submissions after 4 Nov, old prices after 16 Nov).
2) Separate observation, hypothesis and missing information. Don't claim causality from small numbers.
3) Propose at most 2 changes for next week, with time cost and how we'll judge them.
4) Return the corrected next-week table.
```

### 4. Turn one calendar row into ready drafts

```prompt
Turn this calendar row into drafts in natural Brazilian Portuguese:
A) Facebook post (self-contained, 80–130 words, one CTA with the tagged link);
```

### 5. Audit the calendar before scheduling

```prompt
Audit this content calendar against the official fact sheet. Do not rewrite the posts; only list problems.
Check: wrong dates or weekdays; missing "horário de Brasília"; submission CTAs after 4 Nov; prices not matching the tier for that date; invented urgency or scarcity; 20 Nov posts that are sales-driven; posts that rely on unconfirmed facts (certificate rules, presentation requirements, speakers); duplicate posts on the same day across channels; weeks with more than 4 Facebook posts.
```
