This page is a recipe book. Each recipe gives you a **ready-to-paste prompt**, the **tool** and **size** to use, and the **text to add afterwards in Canva**. The images are generated without text on purpose: AI still misspells Portuguese (`Educação`, `Inscrições`, `Consciência`), so all words, dates and logos go in as editable text. The reasons are explained in [Image generation](image-generation.md#portuguese-text-in-images-generate-without-text-add-it-in-canva).

**What you'll get from this page:**

- A reusable **style block** that keeps every image in the same visual family.
- 11 recipes for the CONAFROEDU 2026 campaign, in calendar order.
- Fix-it prompts for when the result is almost right.
- A checklist to run before every post.

> [!NOTE]
> Prompts on this English page are in English. The [Portuguese page](../pt-BR/image-prompts.md) has the same prompts in Brazilian Portuguese; both work in ChatGPT and Gemini. The text you add in Canva is always **in Portuguese**, because the team posts in Portuguese. English glosses are in brackets.

## How to use this cookbook

1. **Default tool:** ChatGPT Images or the Gemini app, both free with limits. Midjourney (paid) is suggested only where a stronger art direction helps. Finish everything in **Canva Free** (or Adobe Express Free).
2. **Paste your style block** (below) at the start of the chat once. For the following images in the same chat, say "same style as before".
3. **Replace everything in [brackets]** before sending.
4. **Two attempts maximum** per image. If it still fails, use the fix-it prompts or switch to a plain background and typography.
5. **Review every image with people** using the [representation checklist](representation-bias.md#pre-publication-review-checklist) before posting.
6. **Never ask the AI for:** the CONAFROEDU logo, speakers' faces, prices, ISBN/DOI numbers, QR codes, or "a photo of the congress".

> [!WARNING]
> CONAFROEDU 2026 is the **first edition** and is **100% online**. No prompt should create a venue, an auditorium, a crowd or "photos from last year". That would be false evidence.

## The style block (paste once per chat)

Fill this in with your [brand kit](brand-kit.md). The colours below are only an example.

```prompt
STYLE FOR ALL IMAGES IN THIS CONVERSATION
Project: CONAFROEDU 2026 – National Congress of Africanidades in Education (online, Brazil), theme "Development, Participation and Anti-racist Education".
Visual style: contemporary editorial illustration in paper-cut shapes, subtle paper texture, clean composition, warm and professional.
Palette: [main colour, e.g. navy #17324D], [second colour, e.g. green #3F8F79], [background, e.g. cream #F6F2E9], [accent colour].
People (only when I ask for them): adult Black Brazilian educators and researchers with varied skin tones, ages, bodies and hair (coily, curly, braids, locs, short), in roles of authority: teaching, researching, leading. Faces must be distinct, not clones. Lighting that preserves detail in dark skin.
Always: no words, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks. Leave clean empty space for text that I will add later.
Never: religious symbols, orixás, Adinkra, Kente or generic "tribal" patterns, savannahs, wild animals, huts, masks, scenes of poverty or rescue, an in-person venue or audience.
Confirm you understood in one sentence and wait for my first request.
```

## 1. Event poster / background

**Tool:** ChatGPT Images or Gemini app (free). For a more artistic hero image, Midjourney V8.2 (paid). **Size:** generate at 4:5, then extend to 9:16 and ≈1.91:1 (Event cover).

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create the main background for the CONAFROEDU 2026 campaign. Vertical 4:5. In the lower half: a contemporary scene of knowledge exchange among adult Black Brazilian educators: one presents ideas, others listen and take notes, a laptop shows an abstract online meeting with no readable screen. Books and notebooks without readable text. The upper half must be calm and almost empty (background colour with a few soft shapes) for a title. Conceptual illustration, not a photograph of a real event. No text, numbers or logos.
```

**No-people alternative (lowest risk, very useful):**

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create an abstract background: overlapping paper shapes that suggest connection, dialogue and growth (paths that meet, open books seen from above, circles that link together). Vertical 4:5, empty upper half for a title. No people, no text, no symbols with cultural or religious meaning.
```

**Add in Canva:**

- `CONAFROEDU 2026` (largest)
- `Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação`
- `Desenvolvimento, Participação e Educação Antirracista`
- `5 e 6 de dezembro de 2026 · 100% online`
- `Inscrições na Even3` [Registration on Even3] + the real logo
- Caption: the Even3 link `https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/`

## 2. Speaker card

**Tool:** Canva with the speaker's **real, approved photo**. Use AI only for the background or frame. **Size:** 1080 × 1350.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a background for a speaker card. Vertical 4:5. Leave a large empty area on the left (about 55% of the width) where I will place a real portrait photo, and a clean band at the bottom for name and topic. On the right, only soft paper shapes in the palette colours. No people, no faces, no text, no frames that look like a photo.
```

Copy the **session date and time together** from the final approved program; the congress spans two days.

**Add in Canva:** the approved portrait (crop only; **do not** use AI to retouch the face, skin or hair) and:

- `Palestrante` [Speaker] or `Convidada/Convidado`, as the committee defines
- `[Nome completo exatamente como aprovado]` [Full name exactly as approved]
- `[Título da fala, aprovado pela pessoa]` [Talk title, approved by the speaker]
- `[Instituição, se autorizado]` [Institution, if authorized]
- `[CONFIRMAR] — data da sessão · [CONFIRMAR] — horário (horário de Brasília)`
- `CONAFROEDU 2026 · 100% online`

> [!WARNING]
> Copy names exactly from the approved list. Do not let AI "correct" an unusual name. Don't put an institution's logo on the card without that institution's permission. Listing an affiliation is not an endorsement.

## 3. "Trabalho aprovado" author card

A card authors can share when their work is accepted. Offer it to authors who want it; don't publish anyone's name without their consent. **Confirm the exact wording with the committee** ("aprovado para apresentação"? "aprovado para publicação nos anais"?). Publication has a separate fee (R$ 16,98 per work, per the event page), so don't imply it is included.

**Tool:** ChatGPT or Gemini once for the background, then one Canva template reused for every author. **Size:** 1080 × 1350 (feed) and 1080 × 1920 (Story).

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a celebratory but sober background for a card announcing an accepted academic paper. Vertical 4:5. Elements: paper shapes rising like steps or pages opening, a feeling of achievement and shared knowledge. Large clean central area for text. No people, no trophies, no medals, no text, no numbers.
```

**Add in Canva (author fills their own name, or the team with consent):**

- `Trabalho aprovado!` [Paper accepted!] (exact wording to confirm)
- `[Título do trabalho]` [Paper title], short version if very long
- `[Nome(s) da(s) autora(s)/autor(es)]` [Author name(s)]
- `[Modalidade: artigo completo / resumo expandido / resumo simples / prática exitosa]`
- `CONAFROEDU 2026 · 5 e 6 de dezembro · on-line`

> [!TIP]
> Creating a [Canva Template link](https://www.canva.com/help/share-template-link/) requires an eligible plan; it is **not included in Free**. With Free, the team duplicates the master, fills each author’s approved details and sends a finished PNG. If an eligible plan is already available, test its template link with an external account before distributing it. Never give authors edit access to the master. See [Speaker & partner promo kits](speaker-partner-kits.md).

## 4. Quote card

Use **only real quotes** from an approved text or talk transcript, approved by the person. Never ask AI to "write a quote from" a speaker or a historical figure.

**Tool:** Canva typography, optionally with an AI texture. **Size:** 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1350.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a minimal background for a quote card. Square 1:1. A soft paper texture in the background colour, with one discreet shape in the accent colour in one corner, and a large quotation-mark-like curved shape made of paper (not a typographic character). 80% of the area must remain empty and calm for a long text. No people, no letters, no numbers.
```

**Add in Canva:**

- The quote, **verbatim**, in quotation marks (or labelled `paráfrase` if it is a paraphrase)
- `— [Nome], [função/instituição aprovada]`
- `CONAFROEDU 2026 · 5 e 6/12 · on-line`

## 5. Carousel slides

A 5–7 slide carousel, for example "Como submeter seu trabalho até 4/11" [How to submit your paper by 4 Nov] or, from 25 Nov, the program. **One idea per slide.**

**Tool:** one AI background, reused on every slide with small variations; text in Canva. **Size:** 1080 × 1080 for all slides.

For predictable separate files, request **one variation per message**, approve it, then ask for the next using the approved image as reference. The batch prompt below is an optional planning shortcut, not a guarantee of three files.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a set of 3 matching square (1:1) backgrounds for a carousel, as variations of the same composition: (1) cover: shapes concentrated on the right, large empty area on the left; (2) inside page: shapes only along the bottom edge, large empty area; (3) final page: shapes framing the edges, empty centre. Same palette, same texture, same shape thickness across all three. No people, no text, no numbers, no arrows.
```

**Add in Canva (example: submission carousel, valid until 4 Nov):**

1. `Quer apresentar seu trabalho no CONAFROEDU 2026?` [Want to present your work?]
2. `Modalidades: artigo completo, resumo expandido, resumo simples e práticas exitosas` (confirm the list with the committee; the FAQ and the submission form differ)
3. `Prazo: até 4 de novembro de 2026` [Deadline: 4 November 2026]
4. `Avaliação em até 5 dias úteis após a submissão` [Review within 5 business days]
5. `Publicação nos anais com ISBN e DOI (taxa de R$ 16,98 por trabalho)` [Proceedings with ISBN and DOI; fee per work]
6. `Normas e inscrição: link na legenda` [Rules and registration: link in caption]

Number the slides (`1/6`, `2/6`…). On Facebook, preview the post, because a multi-photo post may not keep the same order and experience as an Instagram carousel.

## 6. Countdown

Two useful countdowns: **submissions** (7, 3 and 1 day before 4 Nov) and **the congress** (the last week before 5 Dec). Generate **one** background and change only the number in Canva, which keeps the series consistent and uses one AI generation.

**Tool:** ChatGPT or Gemini once; Canva for the numbers. **Size:** 1080 × 1920 (Story) and 1080 × 1350 (feed).

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a vertical 9:16 background for a countdown series. A large, completely empty circular area in the centre (where I will place a big number), surrounded by paper shapes arranged like a clock face or a path moving forward. Keep the top 15% and bottom 20% of the image simple (the app's buttons will cover these areas). No numbers, no letters, no clock hands, no people.
```

**Add in Canva:**

- Big number: `7` / `3` / `1` + `dias` [days] (or `Falta 1 dia` [1 day left])
- Submissions: `para o fim das submissões · 4/11` [until submissions close]
- Congress: `para o CONAFROEDU 2026 · 5 e 6/12 · 100% online`
- `Link na legenda` / Even3 link sticker on Stories

> [!WARNING]
> Count honestly. Don't publish "última chance" (last chance) or "vagas limitadas" (limited places) unless true; it's an online event. If the committee extends the deadline (prorrogação), update every scheduled post the same day.

## 7. Consciência Negra visual (20 November)

**Friday, 20 November** is the Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra, a national holiday. This post should **offer educational content**, not sell. Scientific submissions are closed by then, so do not ask for papers. A separate, light invitation to attend is fine.

**Best option:** a commissioned or licensed work by a Black Brazilian artist, or strong typography alone. If you use AI, keep it contemporary and focused on education. Read [Representing Black & African cultures](representation-bias.md) first.

**Tool:** typography in Canva (preferred) or ChatGPT/Gemini for a background. **Size:** 1080 × 1350.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a contemporary editorial illustration for a reflection on Black Consciousness Day in Brazilian education. Vertical 4:5. Adult Black Brazilian educators of different ages and skin tones, in a present-day school library, choosing books together and planning a class; one of them explains, others contribute. Atmosphere of pride, study and collective work. Clearly an illustration, not a photograph. Upper third empty for text. Do NOT include: chains, whips, slave ships, scenes of suffering, portraits of Zumbi or other historical figures, orixás or religious objects, raised-fist icons, flags, generic "African" patterns, savannah or animals. No text.
```

**Add in Canva (suggestion; adjust with the committee and invited educators):**

- `20 de novembro · Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra`
- `20 de novembro no calendário; educação antirracista no cotidiano` [20 Nov on the calendar; anti-racist education every day]
- Caption: substantive content, sources, credit and a genuine educational question about Leis 10.639/2003 and 11.645/2008. Keep any optional attendance invitation in a **separate post**, without sales pressure.
- Credit line for the artist, or `Ilustração gerada com IA; não retrata pessoas reais.`

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not generate a portrait of Zumbi dos Palmares, Dandara or any historical figure. AI invents faces and clothing and presents them as fact. Use verified archival images with proper rights, or typography.

## 8. Price-change reminder (15/16 November)

According to the event page, the current ticket tier ends on **15 November** and prices rise on **16 November** (from R$ 55–70 to R$ 60–75, by category). **Confirm the final values in the Even3 checkout before posting.**

**Tool:** Canva typography; optional AI background. **Size:** 1080 × 1350 and 1080 × 1920.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a clean background for an informational reminder about a date. Vertical 4:5. A simple paper calendar shape with an empty square highlighted in the accent colour (no numbers on it), and soft shapes around it. Large empty area for a short message and a small table. No people, no money symbols, no coins, no text, no numbers.
```

**Add in Canva:**

- `Valores atuais até 15 de novembro` [Current prices until 15 November]
- A small table with the categories and both prices, **copied from Even3** (e.g. `Ouvinte: R$ 55,00 → R$ 60,00 a partir de 16/11`)
- `Inscrições: link na legenda`

> [!TIP]
> Keep the tone informative: "os valores mudam em 16/11" (prices change on 16/11), not "CORRA!". Academics respond to clear information. Don't mix this post with the 20 November content.

## 9. Live-day thumbnail (5–6 December)

For the cover of live streams, session clips and "estamos ao vivo" (we're live) posts. **Confirm with the committee where sessions are streamed and the confirmed schedule (program published by 25 Nov).**

**Tool:** Canva with the speaker's **real photo** or a screenshot (with permission); AI only for the background. **Size:** 1920 × 1080 (16:9) and 1080 × 1920 (Reel/Story cover).

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a horizontal 16:9 background for a live-stream thumbnail. Left 45% empty and calm (for a real photo of the speaker). On the right, energetic paper shapes suggesting sound waves and connection, with an empty band for a large title. High contrast, readable at small size. No people, no faces, no text, no "LIVE" badge, no play buttons.
```

**Add in Canva:**

- Badge: `AO VIVO` [LIVE] or `HOJE` [TODAY]
- `[Título curto da sessão]` (max. 6 words)
- `[Nome da palestrante]` + approved photo
- `[Horário] (horário de Brasília) · CONAFROEDU 2026`

## 10. Certificate and proceedings announcement

According to the event calendar, certificates are expected **from 6 December** and the proceedings are expected **by 15 December**. **Only post the proceedings announcement once they are actually published**, with the real link, ISBN and DOI typed from the official source. **Publish a certificate-available announcement only after confirming actual release**; otherwise use a committee-approved status update. **Confirm the certificate rules** (who is eligible, attendance requirements, how to download the 40-hour certificate) with the committee.

**Tool:** ChatGPT or Gemini for a background; Canva for everything else. **Size:** 1080 × 1350.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a background for an announcement about certificates and published proceedings of an academic congress. Vertical 4:5. Paper shapes forming an open book whose pages turn into connected paths, and a blank document shape with a simple ribbon (no seal, no signature, no lettering). Upper half empty for text. No people, no text, no numbers, no barcodes, no QR codes.
```

**Add in Canva:**

- Only after confirming release: `Certificados disponíveis` [Certificates available] + `Como baixar: [passos confirmados]` [How to download]
- or `Anais publicados!` [Proceedings published!] + `ISBN [número real]` + `DOI individual por trabalho` (only as published)
- `Obrigada/o por construir o CONAFROEDU 2026 com a gente` [Thank you for building it with us]

> [!CAUTION]
> Never generate an image of a certificate, a seal or a book cover with an ISBN. An AI image of a "certificate" could be mistaken for (or used as) a real document.

## 11. Recap (7–15 December)

The recap must be **real**: screenshots of actual sessions (with permission), real quotes, real numbers from your platform. Use AI only for a cover background.

**Tool:** Canva with real material; AI optional for the cover. **Size:** 1080 × 1080 carousel.

```prompt
Using the agreed style, create a square 1:1 cover background for a thank-you and recap carousel. Paper shapes arranged like a mosaic of empty frames of different sizes (where I will place real screenshots), with a soft warm glow. Large empty band for a title. No people, no faces, no text, no numbers.
```

**Add in Canva:**

1. Cover: `CONAFROEDU 2026: obrigada/o!` [thank you!]
2. Real screenshots of sessions, cropped to people who agreed to appear; blur chat names and other participants
3. Real, approved quotes from speakers
4. Real numbers only, if the committee confirms them (e.g. number of papers presented)
5. `Certificados e anais: veja como acessar` [Certificates and proceedings: how to access]
6. `Até a próxima edição!` [See you next edition!] (only if a next edition is planned)

> [!WARNING]
> Never generate an "audience" or "participants" image for a recap, or use AI to add people to a screenshot. Recaps are documentation. Under LGPD, people in a screenshot need to have agreed to appear; when in doubt, crop or blur.

## Fix-it prompts (iteration)

Use these in the same chat, **one problem per message**. If two fixes fail, go back to the last good version or use a plain background.

**Remove text that appeared anyway**

```prompt
Recreate exactly this image but remove all letters, words, numbers and pseudo-text, including on books, screens and posters. Keep everything else the same.
```

**Too busy, no space for the title**

```prompt
Simplify: keep the same scene but reduce the number of elements by half and leave the top third completely empty with just the background colour.
```

**Change the format (outpainting)**

```prompt
Extend this image to [9:16 / 16:9 / 1.91:1]. Continue only the background, shapes and texture. Do not add people, objects, text or logos. Do not change anything that already exists.
```

**Faces look alike (homogenized)**

```prompt
The people have very similar faces. Keep the composition and roles, but give each person distinct facial features, ages, body types and hairstyles. Don't change skin tones toward lighter shades.
```

**Skin was lightened or lighting hides dark skin**

```prompt
Skin tones look lighter than requested and faces lose detail. Keep the composition and restore deeper skin tones as described before, with soft lighting that shows facial detail in dark skin. Change nothing else.
```

**Hair is wrong or uniform**

```prompt
Correct only the hair: [person on the left] has short coily hair, [person in the centre] has braids, [person on the right] has curly shoulder-length hair. Drawn with care and natural volume. Change nothing else.
```

**Wrong authority (who leads the scene)**

```prompt
Recompose so that [the woman with dark skin] is the one presenting and explaining, standing at the centre and using the laptop, while the others listen and take notes. Keep the style.
```

**A cultural or religious element appeared**

```prompt
Remove [the patterned fabric / the mask / the symbol] from the image and replace it with plain fabric in the palette colours. Do not add any other cultural, religious or decorative symbol.
```

**Looks too much like a real photo**

```prompt
Make the image clearly an illustration: flatter colours, visible paper-cut shapes and texture, less photographic detail. It must not be mistaken for a photograph of a real event.
```

**Match an approved previous image**

```prompt
Use image A (approved) as a style reference: same palette, texture, shape thickness and lighting. Create [new scene]. Do not copy the characters or the exact composition. No text.
```

## Optional: testing Portuguese lettering

If you *really* want generated lettering (for an artistic title concept, never for dates or names), test it this way, preferably in Ideogram, ChatGPT or Gemini:

```prompt
Create a vertical 4:5 poster concept with few elements and strong contrast. Use only these two texts, exactly as written, without translating: Title: "Educação que transforma" Call: "Inscrições abertas". Keep the ç, the ã and every accent. Do not invent dates, cities, prices, sponsors, testimonials or logos. Leave a free area at the bottom.
```

Check every letter against the approved text. If the same line fails twice, type it in Canva.

## Checklist before every post

- [ ] The image has no generated text, numbers or logos (or every letter was checked).
- [ ] All Portuguese text was added in Canva and proofread by a second person at phone size.
- [ ] Dates, prices and deadlines were checked against Even3 and the committee today.
- [ ] Speakers' photos are real, approved and only cropped, not AI-retouched.
- [ ] The image passed the [representation checklist](representation-bias.md#pre-publication-review-checklist).
- [ ] No venue, crowd, "previous edition", fake certificate or fake testimonial.
- [ ] AI use is labelled where someone might mistake it for reality; artists are credited.
- [ ] The prompt and final file are saved in the shared folder for reuse.

Before enabling saved instructions or installing/configuring an editor, use [Set up your tools](setup-tools.md#canva).
