When four volunteers post from four laptops with three AI assistants, the result can look like four different events. A **brand kit** is the small set of rules and files that keeps everything consistent: how CONAFROEDU *sounds*, how it *looks*, and which ready-made pieces everyone reuses.

**What you'll get from this page:**

- A **voice and tone guide** you can paste into any AI assistant.
- A simple **visual identity**: colors, fonts, logo and photo rules.
- A set of **Canva templates** with the right sizes for Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp — on the free plan.
- **Respectful visual language** for Africanidades themes.
- **Reusable prompt snippets** and a **hashtag set**.
- A one-page **brand sheet checklist**.

> [!TIP]
> Don't aim for perfection. A one-page brand sheet and three templates, finished this week, beat a beautiful manual finished after the submission deadline. You can refine it in week 3.

## Part 1 — Voice and tone

**Voice** is the personality that never changes. **Tone** is how that voice adapts to the situation — a deadline reminder sounds different from a 20 November reflection, but it's still recognizably CONAFROEDU.

### Who we're talking to

| Audience | What they care about | Frame it as | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic-education teachers | Classroom practice, curriculum, ERER (Educação para as Relações Étnico-Raciais) in daily school life | Exchange of real practices among colleagues | "Eliminate racism from your school in two days" |
| Undergraduate students | First research experience, certificate hours | Participating and presenting for the first time | Guaranteeing the certificate counts as *horas complementares* — that depends on each course |
| Postgraduate students and researchers | Presenting work, proceedings with ISBN/DOI, networks | Discussing research with peers; accurate bibliographic record | "Qualis A", guaranteed points or automatic recognition |
| School managers | School response, teacher development, protocols | Reflection and tools for the school community | Promising MEC accreditation or official policy credentials |
| Community educators and social movements | Recognition of situated knowledge | Valuing collective and territorial knowledge | Treating community knowledge as raw material for others |

### Four voice principles

1. **Academic, but warm.** Educator to educator. We respect expertise without sounding like a legal notice.
2. **Specific and honest.** Real dates, real prices, real conditions. No fake urgency, no promises we can't keep.
3. **Respectful and precise about race and culture.** We name things correctly (*pessoas negras, população negra, culturas afro-brasileiras*), don't treat "negro", "africano" and "quilombola" as synonyms, and don't use euphemisms like "moreno(a)".
4. **Clear before clever.** Short sentences. Explain every acronym the first time. One call to action per post.

### Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone | Example opening (pt-BR, as posted) |
|---|---|---|
| Call for papers | Inviting, clear, practical | "Você desenvolve práticas de educação antirracista? Sua experiência pode virar um trabalho no CONAFROEDU." |
| Deadline reminder | Calm and direct | "As submissões terminam em 4 de novembro. Confira a checklist antes de enviar." |
| Price change | Factual, no pressure | "O valor da inscrição muda em 16 de novembro. Até 15/11, vale o valor atual." |
| 20 November | Reflective, crediting others, no selling | "Neste 20 de novembro, que mudanças de currículo e de formação estamos construindo ao longo do ano?" |
| Support replies | Kind and practical | "Olá! Obrigada pela mensagem. Para baixar o certificado, siga estes passos…" |
| Event days | Energetic but precise | "Começamos às [horário], horário de Brasília. O link de acesso está no e-mail da inscrição." |
| Corrections | Transparent, brief | "Corrigindo: o horário certo é [horário]. Pedimos desculpas pela confusão." |

### Words we use and words we avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *inscrição* (registration) / *submissão* (sending a paper) | Mixing the two | They are different steps with different deadlines |
| *certificado de 40 horas, conforme os critérios divulgados* | "40h de aulas ao vivo", "vale progressão" | Not documented; depends on each institution |
| *anais com ISBN e DOI* | "publicação Qualis", "revista científica" | Proceedings aren't automatically a rated journal |
| *professoras e professores* (or another agreed inclusive form) | Only the masculine form by default | Most of our audience are women teachers |
| *100% on-line* | "presencial", any city name | The event is online; don't infer a city |
| *o valor muda em 16/11* | "últimas vagas", "corre que acaba" | No real capacity limit has been announced |
| *Educação para as Relações Étnico-Raciais (ERER)* on first mention | Unexplained acronyms | Not every teacher knows every policy acronym |

### Formatting conventions

- **Dates:** "4 de novembro de 2026" in text; "04/11" or "4/11" on cards. In English, "4 November 2026".
- **Money:** "R$ 55,00".
- **Time:** "às 19h, horário de Brasília" — Brazil has several time zones.
- **Name:** "Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação (CONAFROEDU)" once, then "CONAFROEDU".
- **Spelling:** the official announcement writes "on-line"; pick one form ("on-line" or "online") and use it everywhere.
- **Emojis:** at most two per post, and never instead of words (screen readers read them aloud).
- **Hashtags:** see the [hashtag set](#hashtag-set) below.

### Let AI draft your voice document

Paste the [CONAFROEDU context block](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block) first, then:

```prompt
Using the fact sheet above, draft a one-page "voice and tone guide" for CONAFROEDU 2026 in Brazilian Portuguese, for volunteers and AI assistants to follow.
Include: (1) who we talk to (teachers, undergrad and postgrad students, researchers, school managers, community educators); (2) four voice principles; (3) a table of tone by situation (call for papers, deadline reminder, price change, 20 November, support replies, event days, corrections) with one example sentence each; (4) 10 words/phrases to use and 10 to avoid, with the reason; (5) formatting conventions for dates, money, times and the event name.
The tone must be academic but warm, respectful about race and culture, honest (no fake urgency or promises about MEC, Qualis or career progression). Keep it under 600 words.
```

Review it as a team, adjust, and save the final version next to the fact sheet. The short version for prompts is the "VOZ DA MARCA" paragraph in [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#your-brand-voice-in-one-paragraph).

## Part 2 — Visual identity

### Start from what already exists

CONAFROEDU already has official graphics on its [Even3 page](https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/) and on the organizers' Instagram ([@congressosnacionais_online](https://www.instagram.com/congressosnacionais_online)). Before inventing anything:

1. Collect the official logo files and 3–5 existing graphics in one shared folder.
2. Ask the committee which elements are "official" (logo, colors, typefaces) and who made them.
3. Note what works (legibility, colors) and what to improve. Some existing graphics use generalized "African" decorative motifs — that's not a reason to add more invented symbols. See [Part 4](#part-4--respectful-visual-language-for-africanidades).

### Colors

Choose **two main colors, one accent and two neutrals** (a dark for text, a light for backgrounds). Write down their **hex codes** (the six-character color code, like `#17324D`) so everyone uses exactly the same shades.

- If the official graphics already have colors, reuse them — ask the designer for the codes, or use Canva's color picker on the uploaded logo.
- Check **contrast**: text must be easy to read on a phone in sunlight. Dark text on a light background (or the reverse) is safest. Avoid thin colored text over photos.
- Don't rely on color alone to convey meaning (for example, "red = closed"); add words.

> [!NOTE]
> **Example palette only** — our image research used this in a sample prompt: navy `#17324D`, green `#3F8F79`, cream `#F6F2E9`. It is **not** CONAFROEDU's official palette. Use it only if the committee has no colors and approves it.

### Typography

- Pick **two font families** from the fonts available in Canva Free: one for headlines, one for body text.
- **Test Portuguese characters** before adopting a font. Type this test line: *Educação, Participação, Conceição, ações, à, ê, õ — 5 e 6 de dezembro*. If any letter looks wrong or falls back to another font, choose another.
- Keep text **large**: headline readable at phone size without zooming; no long paragraphs inside images — put details in the caption.
- Never let an AI image generator write the event name, dates or prices. It often misspells Portuguese. Add all text in Canva.

### Logo

- Use only the **approved** logo files. Never ask AI to "redraw" or "improve" the logo.
- Leave clear space around it; don't stretch, recolor or add effects.
- **No institutional logos** (universities, NEABs, MEC) unless that institution has formally agreed to be a partner — an individual's affiliation doesn't authorize the logo.

### Photos

- **Real, consented photos of real people come first** — speakers' approved portraits, committee members who agree, documented classroom practices with permission.
- Keep a simple record for each photo: who took it, who's in it, what use was permitted, required credit.
- Don't use AI to "improve" portraits: no skin lightening, hair straightening or facial reshaping. Simple crop and exposure adjustments only.
- Free photo banks have limits: some (like Brasil com S) forbid promotional use. Check before using — details in [Representation & bias](representation-bias.md) and [Image generation](image-generation.md).

### Illustrations and commissioned art

If you use illustrations, pick **one style** and stick to it across the campaign. Better still, commission a **Black Brazilian designer or illustrator** for one key piece:

- [PretUX](https://www.pretux.com.br/quero-ajudar/) offers a route to hire Black design professionals from its catalogue.
- Ask for something small and specific: one illustration or lettering treatment plus two crops, with agreed fee, uses, credit and whether AI editing is allowed.
- Credit example (agree the wording with the artist): `Ilustração: [nome] (@perfil), encomenda para o CONAFROEDU 2026; uso autorizado.`
- Visibility is not payment. If there's no budget, use typography and simple shapes instead of asking for free work.

## Part 3 — Canva templates

### Canva Free vs Pro

| Plan | Cost (Brazil, Oct 2026) | What matters for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Canva Free** | R$ 0 | Design, templates, uploads, PNG/MP4 export; limited AI uses (the Brazilian pricing page mentions up to 20, depending on the feature). Some elements are marked as paid. |
| **Canva Pro** | R$ 35/month (one person) | More assets, one-click resize, more AI. Only worth it if layouts eat hours — try one month. |
| **Canva for Education** | Free for verified K–12 teachers | A conference committee doesn't qualify by itself, and Education content is limited to **educational, non-commercial** use — not for promoting a paid congress. |
| **Canva for Nonprofits** | Free for approved charitable nonprofits | Requires official registration documents; universities and professional associations are excluded. A volunteer committee usually won't qualify. |

Source: [Canva Brazil pricing](https://www.canva.com/pt_br/precos/). Canva also has a "Brand Kit" feature for saving colors and fonts; if your plan doesn't include it, keep a **master design** with your colors and fonts and always duplicate it.

### Sizes to set up

| Template | Size (px) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post (Facebook + Instagram) | **1080 × 1350** (4:5) | The shared master for most posts |
| Square / carousel page | **1080 × 1080** (1:1) | Use the same size for every page of a carousel |
| Story / Reel | **1080 × 1920** (9:16) | Keep text away from the top and bottom edges, where app buttons appear |
| Facebook Event cover | **1920 × 1005** | A team default (Meta doesn't publish an official size); keep key text in the center |
| Facebook Page cover | **1702 × 630** (or 851 × 315) | Shows differently on mobile and desktop; preview both |

More detail on sizes and crops is in [Image generation](image-generation.md).

### Build the template set (step by step)

1. In Canva, **Create a design → Custom size → 1080 × 1350**.
2. Add your colors, the two fonts and the approved logo. Set a margin (keep everything at least a finger's width from the edges).
3. Build the layout with **editable text boxes** for: title, date line, "100% on-line", call to action ("Inscrições na Even3").
4. Name it **"MASTER — Feed — NÃO EDITAR"**. To make a post, always **duplicate** the master.
5. Repeat for **1080 × 1920** (Story/Reel) and **1080 × 1080** (carousel). On the free plan, resize manually: create the new size and paste/rearrange elements.
6. Share the folder with the team (edit access only for Content/Design).

Templates to create during the sprint:

- [ ] Call for papers (until 4 Nov)
- [ ] Deadline reminder ("Faltam X dias")
- [ ] Speaker card (approved photo + name + institution + talk title)
- [ ] Quote card (verbatim, approved quotes only)
- [ ] Carousel page (numbered, one idea per page)
- [ ] "What it costs" card (registration + publication fee)
- [ ] "Como acessar" FAQ card
- [ ] 20 November educational carousel (designed with the invited educator)
- [ ] "Faltam 7 dias" countdown (can be animated — see [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md))
- [ ] Certificate how-to and thank-you cards

> [!WARNING]
> Canva's AI terms prohibit removing provenance/metadata tags from AI-generated outputs. Don't try to hide that an image was AI-generated — and label it in the caption when people could mistake it for a real photo of the event. See [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

## Part 4 — Respectful visual language for Africanidades

CONAFROEDU's subject is Africanidades and anti-racist education. Its visuals will be judged by the very educators and researchers it wants to attract — they'll notice stereotypes immediately. The full guide is [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully with AI](representation-bias.md); these are the brand rules:

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Show contemporary Black Brazilian educators and researchers **teaching, researching, leading and using technology** | Show Black people mainly as recipients of help, or at the edge of the image |
| Use real, consented photos of real participants | Generate a realistic "audience" or "previous edition" (this is the 1st edition) |
| Preserve skin tone, facial features and hair exactly as they are | "Beautify" portraits by lightening skin or straightening hair |
| Show variety of skin tones, hair (crespo, cacheado, tranças, locs, short), ages and roles across the campaign | Use the same face or the same hairstyle as "the" Black look |
| Use typography and simple geometric shapes when you have no specific cultural reason for a symbol | Decorate with savannahs, masks, "tribal" patterns or a mix of unrelated cultural symbols |
| Verify the name, meaning and origin of any specific symbol (e.g., an Adinkra symbol) with knowledgeable sources | Ask AI to invent a symbol and its "meaning" |
| Consult practitioners before any religious imagery (orixás, Candomblé, Umbanda) | Use sacred figures or objects as decoration or mascots |
| Label illustrations as illustrations, and disclose AI use | Present AI images as photos of the event |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Adinkra** (Akan, Ghana) and **Kente** (Ghanaian strip-weaving, UNESCO-listed in 2024) are specific cultural heritages — not generic "African patterns", and not Brazil's single ancestral reference. If no one on the team can explain why a symbol is there, remove it.

## Part 5 — Reusable prompt snippets

Save these in the shared document under the fact sheet. Combine them as needed.

**Voice snippet** — paste after the fact sheet in any writing task. The full text is in [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#your-brand-voice-in-one-paragraph).

**Visual style snippet for a text-free background** (for ChatGPT Images, Gemini or Canva AI):

```prompt
Editorial illustration for CONAFROEDU 2026, an online congress on Africanidades and anti-racist education in Brazil. Style: paper cut-out with simple shapes, subtle texture, warm and professional. Palette: [HEX 1], [HEX 2], [HEX 3]. Vertical 4:5 composition; keep the top half simple for a title added later. Do not include any words, numbers, logos or brands. No religious symbols, "tribal" patterns or generic cultural costumes. If people appear, they are adult Black Brazilian educators with different skin tones, ages and hair (crespo, cacheado, tranças), in roles of teaching, research and leadership. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photo of a real event.
```

**Caption adaptation snippet:**

```prompt
Adapt the approved Facebook caption below for: (1) Instagram — up to 125 words, up to 5 relevant hashtags at the end, "link na bio" plus the exact Even3 URL for the bio; (2) WhatsApp — plain text, no hashtags, the Even3 link on its own line, under 60 words. Keep every date, price and name exactly as written. Write in Brazilian Portuguese.
CAPTION: [paste]
```

**Alt text snippet** (alt text = a short description that screen readers read aloud to blind and low-vision users):

```prompt
Write alt text in Brazilian Portuguese (max 2 sentences) for this social media card. Describe the layout and all text on it exactly. Describe people only by what is visible (action, setting); do not guess anyone's race, religion or identity. [paste or describe the image and the text on it]
```

**Design review snippet** (a helper, never the final judge):

```prompt
Review this campaign image as support for a human reviewer: Who is at the center, who teaches, who listens, who uses technology? Are faces homogenized? Is skin tone linked to authority? Are there cultural elements without context, or anything that looks like a documentary photo of the event? Is all text legible and correctly spelled in Portuguese? List doubts and possible problems; do not infer the race or religion of real people and do not declare the image culturally correct. Approval is human.
```

The Portuguese versions of these snippets are on the [pt-BR page](../pt-BR/brand-kit.md#parte-5--trechos-de-prompt-reutilizáveis).

## Hashtag set

Hashtags help people find posts by topic; they don't guarantee reach. Meta says excessive or unrelated hashtags can reduce distribution, so keep them few and relevant.

**Core set (Facebook: use 0–3 per post):**

`#CONAFROEDU #EducaçãoAntirracista #Africanidades`

**Swap one in by topic:**

| Topic | Hashtag options |
|---|---|
| Authors / call for papers (until 4 Nov) | `#PesquisaEmEducação` `#PósGraduação` `#ArtigoCientífico` |
| Teachers and teacher education | `#Professores` `#FormaçãoDocente` |
| Ethnic-racial relations | `#RelaçõesÉtnicoRaciais` |
| The event format | `#CongressoOnline` `#CongressoAcadêmico` |
| November — **only on substantive related content** | `#ConsciênciaNegra` `#NovembroNegro` |

**By channel:**

- **Facebook:** 0–3, at the end of the post.
- **Instagram:** up to 5 relevant hashtags (Instagram has reportedly been moving to a five-hashtag limit).
- **WhatsApp:** none.

**Rules:** no unrelated trending tags; no city tags (the event is national and online); check what a hashtag is used for before adopting it; write the topic naturally in the text too.
