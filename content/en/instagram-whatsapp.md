**What you'll get from this page:** a clear job for Instagram and for each WhatsApp feature, step-by-step setup of the free **WhatsApp Business app** (profile, greeting, away message, quick replies, labels) and a **WhatsApp Channel**, how to run Instagram **Collabs** with speakers, how to cross-post without losing quality, ready-to-use Portuguese messages, and the consent rules that keep CONAFROEDU out of trouble. Everything here can be done for **R$ 0**.

Read these carefully: an installed app is not permission to message someone, and an ad-audience estimate is not a count of active users. But the direction is clear — **in Brazil, information about professional events travels through WhatsApp and Instagram**, often forwarded by a colleague or a course coordinator.

## Instagram

### Step 1 — Confirm the account and connect it

The Even3 page links to **@congressosnacionais_online**, a broader account for the organizers' congresses (not a CONAFROEDU-only profile). Its followers and who manages it were not verified.

1. **Ask the committee who manages @congressosnacionais_online** and whether CONAFROEDU content will be posted there (recommended for six weeks — starting a new account now means starting from zero).
2. Make sure it's a **professional account** (business or creator). That is what usually lets Meta Business Suite schedule posts and show Insights. Switching is free.
3. **Connect it to the Facebook Page** in Meta Business Suite (look for settings → linked accounts / Instagram). Each teammate should get their own access instead of sharing the Instagram password.
4. In the bio, write what CONAFROEDU is, the dates and "100% on-line", and put **one link**: the tagged Even3 link, or a simple link page.

Bio example (adjust):

```text
Congressos nacionais on-line 📚 CONAFROEDU 2026 — Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação · 5 e 6/12 · 100% on-line · Inscrições ⬇️
```

> [!TIP] One link or a link page?
> If each post has one action, a direct Even3 link is best (one less click). If you need several (Inscrições, Normas e submissão while open, Programação, Acesso e acessibilidade, Contato), use a free link page — Buffer's [Start Page](https://buffer.com/start-page) has a free entry, and [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/s/pricing) has a restricted free plan. Advanced analytics are often paid. After 4 November, remove the "Normas e submissão" button.

### Step 2 — Links on Instagram

- **Links typed in captions are not clickable.** Say "link na bio" or use a Story link where your account offers it.
- Use tagged links per placement so Even3 shows where registrations came from:

| Placement | `utm_source` | `utm_medium` | `utm_content` example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bio | `instagram` | `organic_social` | `bio_registration` |
| Story | `instagram` | `organic_social` | `story_deadline_01` |
| WhatsApp Channel | `whatsapp` | `messaging` | `channel_programme_01` |

Example: `https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=conafroedu_2026_inscricoes&utm_content=bio_registration`

### Step 3 — Collabs with speakers (the best free reach on Instagram)

A **Collab** is one post or Reel co-authored by two or more accounts: the invited account accepts, and the post can appear on both profiles and count for both ([Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/music-and-collabs-on-instagram/)). In 2023 Meta described up to three invited collaborators; check the current limit in the app.

1. Agree with a **confirmed** speaker on one genuinely useful topic (e.g., "Como trabalhar a Lei 10.639 em ciências e matemática?").
2. Record a 30–60-second answer (or a carousel with 4–6 slides).
3. In the Instagram composer, look for **Invite collaborator** (pt-BR: *Convidar colaborador*) and add the speaker's account before publishing.
4. The speaker **accepts** the invitation.
5. Ask the speaker to adapt the opening to their community and to answer comments on their side.
6. After publishing, open the post from both profiles to check it.

Fallback if Collab doesn't work: the speaker posts their own invitation using your approved kit and a tracked link (see [Speaker & partner kits](speaker-partner-kits.md)).

### Step 4 — Formats and hashtags

| Format | Use for | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | Speaker answers, classroom practices, "como submeter" | Same rules as Facebook: real people, a useful example first, corrected captions |
| Carousels | Checklists, step-by-step, program "itineraries" | On Facebook, the same images appear as a grid — each slide must stand alone |
| Stories | Questions, deadlines, behind the scenes, live updates | 2–4 frames; add question boxes, countdowns or links only if your account has them |
| Highlights | Permanent FAQ: "Inscrição", "Submissão", "Acesso", "Certificado" | Update after 4 November and 16 November |

**Hashtags:** Instagram announced in December 2025 that it was gradually moving to a limit of **five hashtags per post or Reel**, focused on relevance ([report](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)). Use up to five relevant ones, e.g. `#CONAFROEDU #EducaçãoAntirracista #Africanidades #FormaçãoDocente #PesquisaEmEducação`. Forget the old "30 hashtags" advice.

### Step 5 — Cross-post without losing quality

- Adapt instead of copying: a Facebook caption can be longer and self-contained; Instagram needs "link na bio".
- When sharing a Reel from Instagram to Facebook, Facebook has **its own audience setting** for Reels ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/help/204305118221546)). Check it once.
- Cross-posting doesn't guarantee the same reach, and a Collab relationship may not carry over to Facebook — check each destination.
- Always upload the clean original file, never a downloaded copy with a watermark.

## WhatsApp: pick the right tool

These products have different rules and costs. Don't confuse them.

| Tool | What it is | Cost | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Business app** | Free app with business profile, greeting/away messages, quick replies, labels, catalog ([features](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app-features/)) | **Free** | Answering individual questions on the support number |
| **Channel** | One-way broadcast; following doesn't show the follower's number to admins or other followers ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/09/whatsapp-channels-heres-everything-you-need-to-know/)) | **Free** | Opt-in public updates |
| **Community** | Groups organized together, with announcement group and events ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/05/events-in-whatsapp-communities/)) | Free | Only if you need participant/presenter groups — probably not this year |
| **Partner groups** | Existing groups of courses, NEABIs, teachers | Free | Forwarded invitations **through their admins** |
| **Status** | 24-hour updates for your contacts; optionally shareable to Facebook/Instagram Stories via Accounts Center ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/01/whatsapp-joins-accounts-center/)) | Free | Personal invitations by team and ambassadors |
| **Broadcast list** (ordinary) | One message sent individually to a list | Free, with strict rules (below) | Small lists of people who **asked** for updates |
| **Paid business broadcasts / message packs** | Newer marketing-message feature in the Business app | **Paid**; Brazilian pack prices not confirmed | Not needed for this campaign |
| **WhatsApp Business Platform (API)** | For developers and providers; templates and per-message billing | **Paid** (see below) | Not needed for this campaign |

### Set up the free WhatsApp Business app (support number)

The committee's published contact is **(19) 98816-7751**. First confirm **which app that number uses** and who answers it. If it's on the ordinary WhatsApp app, moving it to WhatsApp Business is a committee decision — back up the chats first and don't do it during deadline week.

1. Install **WhatsApp Business** (free) on the phone that holds the support number.
2. **Business profile** (pt-BR: *Perfil comercial*):
   - Name: `CONAFROEDU — Comissão Organizadora` (or the organizer's real name)
   - Category: education
   - Description: one line about the congress, dates and "100% on-line"
   - Website: the tagged Even3 link · E-mail: `conapsuporte@gmail.com`
   - Hours: the real service hours, in Brasília time
3. **Greeting message** (*Mensagem de saudação*) — for first contact. Example:

   ```text
   Olá! Esta é uma resposta automática da organização do CONAFROEDU 2026. Recebemos sua mensagem e uma pessoa da equipe responde em breve, de [DIAS], das [HORAS] (horário de Brasília).
   O congresso é 100% on-line, nos dias 5 e 6 de dezembro.
   Inscrições, normas e valores: https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/
   Por segurança, não envie CPF, dados de pagamento ou documentos por aqui.
   ```

4. **Away message** (*Mensagem de ausência*) — outside service hours, saying when a real person returns.
5. **Quick replies** (*Respostas rápidas*) — shortcuts that **a person** chooses while typing; they are not a chatbot. Create these, pasting only approved facts:

| Shortcut | Content (pt-BR, fill with confirmed facts) |
|---|---|
| `/inscricao` | "As inscrições são feitas na Even3: [link]. Valores por categoria até 15/11; a partir de 16/11 entra o novo lote. Qualquer dúvida no pagamento, me conte aqui." |
| `/submissao` | "As submissões vão até 4/11, às [horário], horário de Brasília. O autor responsável precisa estar inscrito; use o mesmo e-mail na inscrição e na submissão. Normas: [link]." (Replace after 4/11.) |
| `/programacao` | "A programação oficial sai até 25/11 na Even3: [link]." |
| `/acesso` | "[Como as pessoas inscritas recebem o acesso: CONFIRMAR]" |
| `/certificado` | "O congresso oferece certificado de 40 horas, conforme os critérios divulgados pela organização: [CONFIRMAR]. Os certificados ficam disponíveis a partir de 6/12. O aproveitamento para horas complementares ou progressão depende das regras da sua instituição." |
| `/anais` | "Os trabalhos aprovados vão para os Anais com ISBN e DOI. Há taxa de publicação de R$ 16,98 por trabalho, além da inscrição. Previsão de publicação: até 15/12." |
| `/acessibilidade` | "Conte para nós de que apoio você precisa para participar. [Recursos confirmados: CONFIRMAR]" |
| `/humano` | "Vou passar sua dúvida para [nome/comissão], que responde até [prazo]." |

6. **Labels** (*Etiquetas*): "Dúvida sobre inscrição", "Dúvida sobre submissão", "Instituição parceira", "Precisa de resposta humana", "**Não enviar novidades**". Labels organize chats — they are not permission to message a whole category.

> [!WARNING]
> Refunds, payment problems, accessibility needs, certificate disputes and complaints go to a **person**, not a template. And never answer "a apresentação é obrigatória?" until the committee resolves the contradiction on the Even3 page.

For automation beyond this (Meta's AI replies, ManyChat, FAQ bots), see [Automating replies](replies-inbox.md).

### Create a WhatsApp Channel

A Channel is the cleanest way to send updates to many people: they choose to follow, they can leave anytime, and followers' phone numbers aren't shown to admins or other followers.

1. In WhatsApp (Business), open the **Updates** tab (pt-BR: *Atualizações*) and look for the option to **create a channel** (*Criar canal*). Menus vary by version.
2. Name: `CONAFROEDU 2026`. Description: what it is, dates, "100% on-line", and what followers will receive ("programação, prazos e avisos de acesso — no máximo 2 mensagens por semana até a véspera").
3. Icon: the logo.
4. Copy the Channel link and put it in the Facebook Page, Event, Instagram link page, Even3 page and e-mail signatures.
5. Invite partners to **share the link** (not to add people).

**Rhythm:** 1–2 useful updates per week until the last fortnight; real deadlines and program in the final fortnight; a short checklist the day before; a few service updates per event day; afterwards, certificates, proceedings and a clear statement about future updates.

Example Channel update (pt-BR):

```text
📚 A programação oficial do CONAFROEDU já está na Even3. Para quem trabalha na educação básica, destacamos a conversa sobre [tema], com [palestrante confirmado]. 5 e 6/12, 100% on-line. Confira horários e inscrições: [link rastreável]
```

### Partner groups and forwarded messages

WhatsApp groups of graduate programs, licenciaturas, NEABIs and teacher networks are powerful — and private. Work **through people who belong there**.

1. Ask a coordinator, professor or admin you know whether the group accepts external event announcements.
2. Send them a short, forwardable text and one image; let them decide.
3. Never ask to be added to a group to promote, never add people to groups, and never collect numbers from a group.
4. Remember that a group invite link can expose members' phone numbers to each other — if you ever create a group, explain this before people join.

Forwardable text (pt-BR — personalize the first sentence for each group):

```text
CONAFROEDU 2026 — Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação. Dias 5 e 6 de dezembro, 100% on-line. Um espaço de pesquisa e troca de experiências em educação antirracista, com certificado de 40 horas e publicação nos Anais (ISBN e DOI). Submissões até 4/11; inscrições até 6/12. Modalidades, valores e condições na Even3: [link rastreável do parceiro]
```

After 4 November, remove "Submissões até 4/11" (unless officially extended).

### Status: personal invitations

Status works because it comes from a person people know. Ask each organizer and a few ambassadors (a teacher, a student, a partner) to post **one personal invitation per week**, in their own words, with the image from the kit and a tracked link. Sharing Status to Facebook/Instagram Stories through Accounts Center is optional and depends on each person's settings.

### Broadcast lists: the rules people don't know

WhatsApp's help center says:

- A broadcast-list message is delivered **only to people who saved your number in their own contacts** — you saving theirs isn't enough.
- Up to **256 contacts per list**; lists aren't available on Windows, Mac or Web; country-specific monthly limits are being rolled out ([WhatsApp help](https://faq.whatsapp.com/861663048350950/?cms_platform=iphone&locale=en_US)).
- Saving your number is a delivery condition, **not marketing consent**.

So: use broadcast lists only for people who explicitly asked for CONAFROEDU updates on WhatsApp — and prefer the Channel.

### The paid stuff you don't need

- **WhatsApp Business Platform (API):** from **1 October 2026**, each business number gets **1,000 free service messages per month**; above that, and for templates, Meta charges per message. Brazil base rates: **marketing template R$ 0,3217**, **utility template R$ 0,0350** per message ([Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/)). 100 marketing messages would cost R$ 32,17 in Meta fees alone, plus provider costs. **Not needed for a zero-budget campaign.**
- **Meta Business Agent / Meta One:** Meta announced in Brazil an AI agent that answers WhatsApp/Messenger messages, with a free starting offer ([Meta Brasil, June 2026](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2026/06/conversations-2026-apresentamos-o-meta-business-agent/)), and paid Meta One business bundles (from R$ 59/month). Whether it's active on your account, and how good its Portuguese answers are, must be tested. Details in [Automating replies](replies-inbox.md).
- **Unofficial bulk senders, purchased number lists, extensions promising "unlimited" sending:** never. They break WhatsApp's [Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) and can get the support number banned in the middle of the campaign.

## Consent and LGPD in practice

WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy (updated 23 September 2026) requires that people **gave you their number and agreed to receive messages**, and that you **honor requests to stop** ([policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)). A public phone number, a group membership or a "Going" on the Facebook Event is **not** permission. Brazil's LGPD adds: a clear purpose, only necessary data, and easy withdrawal. For details, see [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

Practical rules for CONAFROEDU:

1. **Separate service from marketing.** Messages about someone's registration are service. Invitations to future editions are marketing and need their own permission.
2. **Optional opt-in** (unchecked) in the Even3 form or a link-page form. Suggested wording for the committee to review:

   ```text
   [ ] Quero receber pelo WhatsApp informações e lembretes sobre o CONAFROEDU 2026 até 6 de dezembro de 2026, no máximo duas mensagens por semana. Posso cancelar a qualquer momento. Aviso de privacidade: [link].
   ```

3. **Record** the wording, date and source of each opt-in.
4. **"SAIR" — or any clear request to stop — means stop.** Label the chat "Não enviar novidades".
5. **Never paste chats, phone numbers or registration exports into AI tools.** Anonymize questions before asking AI for help.
6. For people **already registered**, Even3 can send event e-mails to participants and authors ([Even3 help](https://ajuda.even3.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/115003452963-Notificar-pessoas-pela-plataforma)); scheduled sending must be enabled by Even3 support ([help](https://ajuda.even3.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/14288648505499-Agendar-o-envio-de-notifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o-no-evento)). That's often better than WhatsApp for access instructions.

## AI prompts for Instagram and WhatsApp

> [!NOTE]
> These prompts ask for Portuguese output, because the team posts in pt-BR. The Portuguese version of this page has every prompt in Portuguese.

### Adapt one approved announcement to three channels

```prompt
Adapt the approved text below into three versions in natural Brazilian Portuguese:
A) Facebook: self-contained explanation with a registration CTA and the link;
B) Instagram: caption for a carousel that points to the link actually available in the bio or Story; don't promise a clickable link in the caption; suggest up to five relevant hashtags, separated for review;
C) WhatsApp Channel: a direct summary of up to 500 characters with the link, no hashtags.
Keep dates, times, prices, names and conditions exactly. No literal translation, no advertising exaggeration.
Approved text: [paste]. Available links and surfaces: [list].
Also deliver a table comparing the facts in each version.
```

### Write the quick replies from the fact sheet

```prompt
Using ONLY the fact sheet below, write WhatsApp Business quick replies in natural Brazilian Portuguese for these shortcuts: /inscricao, /submissao, /programacao, /acesso, /certificado, /anais, /acessibilidade, /humano.
Each reply: up to 60 words, friendly and clear, one link at most. Where the fact sheet doesn't answer, write [CONFIRMAR] — never guess (especially about certificate criteria, whether presenting is mandatory, and access).
Add a version of /submissao for after the deadline (4/11) that redirects people to registration as participants.
Fact sheet: [paste].
```

### A Collab Reel script with a speaker

```prompt
Write a 45-second vertical video script in natural Brazilian Portuguese for an Instagram Collab between CONAFROEDU and a confirmed speaker.
Speaker: [name, as approved]. Approved topic: [topic]. Audience: [e.g., history teachers].
Structure: a concrete classroom question in the first 3 seconds → one useful answer or example (most of the time) → a one-line, factual invitation to CONAFROEDU (5–6 December, 100% online, link in bio).
Don't invent credentials, quotes or results. Mark lines the speaker must approve. Add on-screen caption text and a short alt-text-style description of the video.
```

### Status messages for ambassadors

```prompt
Write 3 short WhatsApp Status texts (max 140 characters each) in natural Brazilian Portuguese that a teacher could post to invite colleagues to CONAFROEDU — personal, warm, not salesy. Each must mention 5–6 December and 100% online, and end with "link: [link rastreável]". Give one version for teachers, one for undergraduates and one for researchers. No invented benefits; the 40-hour certificate may be mentioned as "certificado de 40 horas" only.
```
