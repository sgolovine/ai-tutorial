**What you'll get from this page:** a simple, free publishing system for a small volunteer team. It has one shared content sheet, one scheduler (Meta Business Suite), and a weekly "batch session" where AI drafts and humans approve. You will also get a list of the CONAFROEDU dates to queue now, a comparison of the free tiers of other schedulers, and a routine for checking the queue so an out-of-date post (for example, "submissions close today" after 4 November) never goes out.

> [!NOTE]
> Tool features and prices change often. Everything here was checked on **21 October 2026**. Before you rely on a limit, check the tool's current plan page (linked below) and your own account.

## Why scheduling matters for a six-week campaign

CONAFROEDU 2026 has very little runway. **Paper submissions close on 4 November**, the **ticket price tier changes on 16 November**, **20 November** is the Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra, and the congress runs **5–6 December**, 100% online. Volunteers have teaching jobs, classes and families. If someone has to post "live" every day, posts get skipped.

Scheduling lets you:

- write and review posts in **one sitting a week** instead of every day;
- have a second person **check facts before** anything goes public;
- keep posting during busy weeks (end of the school term, exam periods);
- see the whole campaign in a calendar and spot gaps or repeats.

Scheduling does **not** replace the daily work of answering questions. See [Automating Replies & Community Management](replies-inbox.md).

## The system: one sheet, one scheduler, one owner

### Step 1 — Create the shared content sheet

Use a free Google Sheet (or Excel Online) that everyone on the team can open. One row = one piece of content. Suggested columns:

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| ID | `CF-014` |
| Phase / objective | Submissions · Attendance · Novembro Negro · Access |
| Audience | Teachers in basic education, postgraduate students… |
| Channel(s) | Facebook Page, Instagram, WhatsApp Channel |
| Final approved text | (pasted caption) |
| Media file | link to the final image/video in the shared folder |
| Fact-sheet version | `fatos-v3 (21/10)` |
| Date and time **(horário de Brasília)** | `28/11/2026 19:00` |
| Approver | Name |
| Status | `idea → draft → needs review → approved → scheduled → published → checked` |
| Published URL | link to the live post |
| Result after 7 days | link clicks, shares, questions |

Use the same ID through every step. This prevents the most common small-team mistake: the same post scheduled twice by two different people.

> [!TIP]
> Keep a separate tab called **Ficha oficial** (the official fact sheet). It holds the event name, dates, Even3 link, prices, deadlines, certificate wording and contact details, each with the date it was confirmed. Every AI prompt and every post should be checked against this tab. See [Build Your Brand Kit with AI](brand-kit.md).

### Step 2 — Choose one scheduler

For a Facebook-first campaign with no budget, start with **Meta Business Suite**. It is Meta's own free tool for Facebook Pages and connected Instagram accounts. You don't need an ad budget to use it for organic posts. ([Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/meta-business-suite))

Only add a second tool if it solves a specific problem (see the comparison below). Three schedulers that overlap mean three places where an old post can hide.

### Step 3 — Name one queue owner

One person "owns" the queue. They don't do all the work, but they are responsible for checking it twice a week and after every fact change. Write their name at the top of the sheet.

## What scheduling can and cannot reach

This is the part that surprises most teams. A "Facebook" scheduler usually means **Facebook Page**, not every place on Facebook.

| Surface | Can you schedule it? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook Page posts (text, photo, link, video/Reel) | **Yes**, in Meta Business Suite | The main use. Check the preview before you confirm. |
| Facebook/Instagram Stories | **Yes**, Meta's tutorial shows Story scheduling in Business Suite | The available options depend on your account. |
| Instagram feed posts/Reels (connected account) | **Yes**, in Business Suite, if Instagram is connected to the Page | Adapt the caption. Links in Instagram captions are not clickable. |
| Facebook **Groups you manage** | Facebook's own Group tools document scheduled posts for admins ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/124331564818285/)) | This happens inside the Group, not through external tools. |
| Other people's Facebook Groups | **No**. Post manually, and only if the group's rules allow it | Meta removed the Groups publishing API in 2024 ([Meta developer blog](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/01/23/introducing-facebook-graph-and-marketing-api-v19/)). Buffer's current help says it only **reminds** you to post in Groups ([Buffer help](https://support.buffer.com/articles/connecting-and-publishing-to-your-facebook-group-with-buffer-VevqG0mHZG)). |
| Facebook Event discussion | Assume **no**. Post manually | Separate surface with separate permissions. |
| Personal profiles | **No** (don't try) | Volunteers share from their own profiles by choice. |
| WhatsApp Channel / Status / groups | **No** external scheduling here. Post manually | See [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md). |
| Even3 emails to registrants | **Maybe**. Even3 documents scheduled notifications, but **support must enable the feature** ([Even3 help](https://ajuda.even3.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/14288648505499-Agendar-o-envio-de-notifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o-no-evento)) | Use only for service messages to people who registered. |

> [!WARNING]
> Never use a tool, extension or "agent" that promises to post automatically into many Facebook Groups. Meta removed that API on purpose. Tools that still do it work by imitating a person, which breaks Meta's terms and can get the Page or a volunteer's account restricted. See [Computer-Use & Browser Agents](computer-use-agents.md#what-not-to-automate).

## Meta Business Suite, step by step

Meta's official walkthrough shows Facebook and Instagram scheduling, including Stories ([Meta video tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQjvbXyMhkM)). That video is from 2022. The buttons in your Brazilian account in 2026 may have different names or be in different places, so read the steps as a guide and follow what your screen shows. We did not log into a live Page to test them.

### First-time setup (once, about 20 minutes)

1. **Give each teammate their own access** to the Page. In the Page settings, add people with the access they need. Never share the owner's password. If someone leaves the team, you remove only their access.
2. Open **Meta Business Suite** (on a computer at business.facebook.com, or with the free mobile app).
3. Top left: **check that the correct Page is selected**. Many volunteers also manage a school or personal project Page.
4. Use an Instagram **professional account (Business or Creator)** connected to the correct Facebook Page, and confirm both destinations appear. The public event links to @congressosnacionais_online; confirm ownership with the committee. If unavailable, publish manually.
5. Find the **time zone** setting in the scheduling screen or Page settings. Use **horário de Brasília** (in tools that list cities, choose `America/Sao_Paulo`). Brazil has more than one time zone, so make sure the tool uses the event's time zone, not each volunteer's.

### Scheduling one post (5–10 minutes per post)

1. Go to **Planner** ("Planejador") or **Content** ("Conteúdo") and click **Create post** ("Criar publicação"). For video, choose the Reel option if it appears.
2. Choose the destinations: Facebook Page and, if wanted, Instagram.
3. Paste the **approved caption** from the content sheet. Don't retype it.
4. Attach the **final exported media** (not a draft). Add alt text if the option appears.
5. If there is a link, paste the Even3 link (`https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/`) or your tagged version of it. Open it once to make sure it works.
6. **Check each preview.** A caption or crop that works on Facebook may need a shorter or different version for Instagram.
7. Instead of **Publish now**, choose **Schedule** ("Agendar"/"Programar"; the label varies). Set the date and time **in horário de Brasília**.
8. Confirm. Then open the Planner and **check that the post appears** on the right day.
9. Update the sheet: status → `scheduled`.
10. **After the scheduled time**, open the Page, find the live post, copy its link into the sheet, and set status → `published`. A scheduled post is not proof of publication. Uploads sometimes fail or logins expire.

> [!NOTE]
> You may see different maximum scheduling ranges quoted online (for example "29 days"). We could not confirm the current limit for 2026. If your account won't let you schedule far enough ahead, schedule in two-week blocks.

## The weekly batch session (90 minutes, once a week)

Batching means doing one kind of task many times in a row. It is much faster than switching between tasks every day. Suggested slot: **Monday evening**, with two people.

| Minutes | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Queue owner | Open the sheet. Check the fact sheet for anything that changed (deadline, prices, programme). |
| 10–30 | Writer + AI assistant | Draft captions for next week's 3–4 posts using the prompt below. |
| 30–50 | Reviewer | Check every fact against the fact sheet, plus the tone and accents in pt-BR. Mark `approved` or send back. |
| 50–80 | Queue owner | Schedule approved posts in Business Suite. Check the previews. |
| 80–90 | Both | **Queue audit** (below), then write down any open questions for the committee. |

### Prompt: draft next week's posts from the calendar

```prompt
You are helping the volunteer committee of CONAFROEDU 2026 (Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação), a 100% online academic congress on 5–6 December 2026. Registration: https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/

Use ONLY the official fact sheet below. Do not invent prices, speakers, deadlines, certificate rules or partnerships. If something is missing, write [CONFIRMAR].

Write the captions in natural Brazilian Portuguese (academic but warm, "você", no translated-from-English tone).

Next week's calendar rows:
[paste the rows: ID, date/time, objective, audience, format, key message]

For each row, give me:
1. A Facebook caption (60–120 words), one clear call to action, the Even3 link written out in full.
2. A shorter Instagram version (no clickable link; say "link na bio" only if we confirm that the bio link exists).
3. Alt text only from the FINAL IMAGE attached or a factual description plus all visible text. If neither is supplied, write AGUARDAR IMAGEM; never invent people, objects or text.
4. A list of every fact you used, so the reviewer can check them.

FINAL IMAGES (attach, or factual descriptions + visible text): [supply per row]
FACT SHEET (version and date): [paste]
```

> [!TIP]
> The team posts in Portuguese, so ask for Portuguese output even if you write the prompt in English. The [pt-BR version of this page](../pt-BR/scheduling-tools.md) has the same prompt fully in Portuguese.

## The queue audit: stop stale posts before they go out

A scheduled post is a promise you made in the past. When a fact changes, that promise can become wrong. Run this audit **every week** and **immediately** after any of these events:

- **4 November (submissions close):** remove or edit every queued post that says "submit your paper". After this date, posts should invite people to *attend*, unless the committee formally announces an extension. **Confirm with the committee** whether there will be a *prorrogação* (deadline extension). Don't plan one in advance.
- **16 November (new price tier):** remove any post that quotes the old prices (R$ 55,00 / R$ 60,00 / R$ 70,00). From 16 November the displayed prices are R$ 60,00 / R$ 65,00 / R$ 75,00. Check the final amounts at checkout before quoting.
- **25 November (programme published):** replace "programação em breve" posts.
- Any change to speakers, times, certificate rules or the Even3 link.

### Prompt: audit the queue

```prompt
Below is our list of scheduled posts (date, time, text) and our current fact sheet. Today is [DATE].
Flag every post that:
- invites people to submit papers after 4 November 2026 (unless the fact sheet shows an approved extension);
- mentions prices that will be outdated on its publication date (prices change on 16 November 2026);
- says "last chance", "últimas vagas" or similar without a fact in the sheet that supports it;
- contradicts the fact sheet in any date, price, name, link or certificate condition;
- duplicates another post's message within 48 hours.
Return a table: post date | problem | suggested fix. Do not rewrite posts that have no problem.

SCHEDULED POSTS: [paste]
FACT SHEET: [paste]
```

## CONAFROEDU dates to put in the calendar now

These are suggestions for posts tied to **real, verified dates**. Pick the ones your team can actually produce. For the full week-by-week plan, see [CONAFROEDU 2026: The 6-Week Sprint Plan](conafroedu-sprint.md).

| Publish on (BRT) | Post idea | Schedule now? |
|---|---|---|
| By 25 Oct | Pinned call for papers: modalities, rules link, deadline 4 Nov, full cost (registration + R$ 16,98 publication fee per work) | Yes |
| 28 Oct | "How to submit on Even3" checklist (format, same email for registration and submission) | Yes |
| 2 Nov (T−48h) | Submission reminder. **Confirm the exact closing time** before writing "48 hours" | Draft now, finish when the time is confirmed |
| 4 Nov | Final submission reminder (morning) | Draft now, and delete it if an extension is announced |
| 5 Nov | "Submissions closed, now join as a participant" | Yes, and edit it if there is an extension |
| 12–14 Nov | Honest price reminder: current tier ends 15 Nov | Yes, after checking checkout prices |
| 20 Nov | Substantive Consciência Negra educational post (credit real educators; attendance CTA kept light) | Draft now, review carefully |
| 25–26 Nov | Official programme | No. Wait for the real programme |
| 28 Nov | "Faltam 7 dias" countdown card | Yes |
| 3 Dec | How to access, programme, support contact | Partly (access details must be confirmed) |
| 5–6 Dec | Live-day service updates | **No.** Post these by hand on the day |

> [!WARNING]
> Don't schedule event-day posts in advance. On 5–6 December, things change in real time (links, delays, speakers). A post that goes out automatically with the wrong information is worse than no post.

## Third-party schedulers: free tiers compared

Add one of these only if Business Suite doesn't solve your problem. For example, you might want one calendar for several networks, or a better analytics view.

| Tool | Free option (checked 21 Oct 2026) | Paid entry point | Fit for CONAFROEDU |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta Business Suite** | Free. Native scheduling for Facebook Pages and connected Instagram | No subscription needed for organic posts | **Start here.** |
| **Buffer** | **3 channels, up to 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time** (a queue limit, not a monthly limit) ([pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing)) | Essentials about **US$ 6/channel/month**, or US$ 5 with annual billing | Easy, clean queue. Facebook **Groups get reminders only**. Check which formats publish automatically. |
| **Metricool** | **1 brand, 20 scheduled posts per month**, 30 days of analytics. The free plan excludes LinkedIn and X ([pricing](https://metricool.com/pricing/)) | Starter from about **US$ 20/month** (annual view; monthly billing can cost more) | Good calendar + analytics. 20 posts/month is tight for a 3-network campaign. |
| **Later** | Later's help (updated August 2026) still describes a limited free plan with a two-week calendar window ([help](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059362253-Choose-a-Later-Social-Plan)), but the pricing page now promotes paid trials | Current price for new accounts not confirmed | Check at sign-up. Don't count on a free plan. |
| **Hootsuite** | No permanent free plan found, only trial/paid ([plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans/standard)) | Regional price not confirmed | Not a good fit without budget. |
| **mLabs** (Brazilian) | Free trial. No permanent free plan found ([site](https://www.mlabs.com.br/)) | Advertised as **R$ 29,90/month per brand**, but as an **annual plan paid upfront by Pix** (R$ 358,80 for 12 months at that rate) | Brazilian support and Portuguese interface, but it is a yearly commitment. |
| **Postiz** | Open-source code you can host yourself. The hosted version offers trials, not a free plan ([pricing](https://postiz.com/pricing)) | Hosted from **US$ 29/month** | Only with a technical volunteer. Self-hosting needs maintenance. |

**Before signing up for any of them, check:** Page versus Group support; which formats (image, video, Reel, Story) publish directly and which only send a reminder; queue limit versus monthly limit; how many people can log in; whether you have to reconnect the Facebook account often; and the currency of the invoice.

> [!TIP]
> A free tier's limits fit a lean campaign: about 3 Page posts and 1 short video per week, plus Stories. At that pace, Business Suite alone covers you. If you outgrow it, the cheapest fix is usually *fewer, better posts*, not a paid tool.

## Can an AI assistant schedule posts for me?

Some assistants can now run "scheduled tasks" (for example, ChatGPT can run a recurring task, but local scheduled tasks need the computer and app to stay on ([ChatGPT docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations))). Browser agents can click through Business Suite for you. These are useful for **reminders and drafts**. They are not a reliable publishing system for an event:

- they can pick the wrong Page or time zone;
- logins expire and uploads fail silently;
- they cost paid-plan usage.

The safest pattern is: **AI drafts → human approves → human (or Business Suite) schedules → human checks the live post.** For agent-assisted scheduling with guardrails, see [Computer-Use & Browser Agents](computer-use-agents.md).

## Time zones and dates: small details, big mistakes

- In all public copy, write **"horário de Brasília"** after every time: *"Submissões até 4 de novembro de 2026, às [HORA] (horário de Brasília)"*. The exact closing time on 4 November has not been published, so **confirm it with the committee**.
- Write dates in full in captions (*4 de novembro de 2026*) and as `04/11/2026` in compact graphics. In English, write *4 November 2026*. Never use US month/day order.
- Participants live in several Brazilian time zones. For the programme, add one line: *"Horários de Brasília. Confira a diferença na sua região."*
- Double-check the date on countdown graphics: "Faltam 7 dias" belongs on **28 November**, not before.

## Checklist

- [ ] Shared content sheet created with ID and status columns
- [ ] "Ficha oficial" tab filled in and dated
- [ ] Each teammate has their own Page access (no shared password)
- [ ] Business Suite shows the correct Page and Instagram account
- [ ] Time zone confirmed as horário de Brasília
- [ ] One named queue owner
- [ ] Weekly 90-minute batch session on the calendar
- [ ] Queue audit scheduled for 4 Nov (evening) and 16 Nov (morning)
- [ ] Event-day posts (5–6 Dec) planned as manual, not scheduled
- [ ] Every published post's link copied into the sheet
