There are dozens of AI tools and every week someone claims a new one is "the best". For a volunteer team promoting CONAFROEDU 2026 with no budget, the real question is simpler: **which free tools will save us the most time this month, without putting wrong information in front of teachers?**

**What you'll get from this page:**

- A jargon-free explanation of the difference between an *assistant* and a *model*.
- A map of the main assistants in October 2026 and what each is good at.
- A "best for" table matched to real CONAFROEDU tasks.
- Free vs paid prices in USD and BRL, with honest notes on what we could and couldn't confirm.
- A **recommended zero-budget starter stack** and a 20-minute test to choose your Portuguese writer.
- Short pointers to image and video tools (covered in depth elsewhere).

> [!NOTE]
> **Last verified: 21 October 2026.** Prices and features come from each company's official pricing or help pages on that date. USD and BRL prices are separate listings published by the vendors, **not currency conversions**. Your account may show different limits, taxes or offers — always check the plan page before paying.

## Assistants vs models: the one concept you need

- An **assistant** is the app or website you chat with: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, Copilot, Grok, Perplexity. It includes the chat window, file uploads, web search, saved projects, and a price plan.
- A **model** is the "engine" inside the assistant — for example, Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Gemini 4 Argon. One assistant may offer several models; better models are often reserved for paid plans.
- **Image and video generation** often use separate models from the text ones, with their own limits.

Why it matters: you'll read headlines like "Model X tops the leaderboard". That tells you very little about whether its **free app** writes good Brazilian Portuguese captions for a teacher audience. Choose the **assistant** by the job you need done, then test it.

## The main assistants in October 2026

### Claude (Anthropic)

- **Current models:** Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5 and Fable 5.1. Free includes **Sonnet and Haiku**; Opus and Fable require eligible paid access. Fable has additional plan/credit conditions, so it is not an unlimited upgrade.
- **Good at:** careful, natural writing and rewriting; long documents; following detailed instructions (like our "don't invent" rules); creating simple animations and graphics with code (see [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md)).
- **Useful features:** **Projects** (saved instructions and files), **Artifacts** (editable documents and designs beside the chat), web search, file reading. On Free, the official matrix lists search, code/files, Artifacts, Design, Slides, Docs and Skills. Optional reusable Skills use **Customize → Skills**, with code execution enabled; see [setup](setup-tools.md#claude-skills).
- **Watch out:** usage limits reset over time and are not unlimited even on paid plans. Paid browser and computer-use features exist but are not needed for this campaign.
- [Claude plans](https://claude.com/pricing)

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)

- **Current models:** ordinary ChatGPT received the **GPT-6 Sol/Luna October update**, according to OpenAI’s [7 October system card](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/gpt-6-october.pdf). Work/Codex use separately released versions; the developer catalog also lists names such as GPT-6.1 Sol and Astra. Do not equate that catalog with your consumer model selector; plan and account access still matter.
- **Good at:** general planning, working with files and spreadsheets, and **image creation and editing** (ChatGPT Images).
- **Useful features:** file uploads, web browsing, scheduled tasks and image creation, with feature-specific limits. **Do not build a new custom GPT for this campaign:** personal accounts can no longer create/publish them. Existing GPTs are scheduled to retire **11 December 2026**; follow the [account-specific migration notice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001519-custom-gpt-retirement-and-migration-faq).
- **Watch out:** free limits depend on the model and task; exact message counts are not published as a fixed number.
- [ChatGPT pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing)

### Gemini (Google)

- **Current models:** Gemini 4 Argon began rolling out to paid Pro/Ultra users on 13 October 2026; the Brazil plan page lists **Flash-Lite for Free**, **Flash for AI Plus** and **Argon for Pro/Ultra**. Auto routing and other available choices depend on the account; use the default for routine captions.
- **Good at:** research, summarizing documents, anything that touches Google Docs/Drive, and image editing.
- **Useful features:** **Gems** — saved assistants with your instructions (Google says they are being replaced by **skills**, rolling out since 30 September 2026); Canvas for drafting; **Gemini Notebook** (see below).
- **Watch out:** usage across tiers uses a "compute budget" that resets every five hours, up to a weekly limit — not a fixed number of messages.
- [Gemini plans in Brazil](https://gemini.google/br/subscriptions/?hl=pt-BR) · [Usage limits](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)

### Gemini Notebook (formerly known as NotebookLM)

- A Google tool that answers **only from the documents you upload**, with clickable citations. Many people still call it NotebookLM, and some Portuguese help pages still use that name.
- **Good at:** turning the official rules, fact sheet and approved speaker bios into FAQs, bios and summaries you can trace back to a source.
- **Free:** the standard plan lists 100 notebooks per user and 50 sources per notebook.
- **Watch out:** use the **standalone Gemini Notebook interface** for source-only work. Ordinary Gemini can add web/tool context. Select only approved documents; exclude synced chats and previous AI answers as official sources. Open and check every citation. Google [documents the difference](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/17003757?hl=en); follow [setup](setup-tools.md).
- [Notebook plans](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16213268?hl=en)

### Meta AI

- Built into **WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Messenger** in Brazil, plus a standalone app. Meta’s current model family includes **Muse** (older articles say Llama); the precise model in each Brazilian app is not established here. A research/API model name does not prove it is available inside WhatsApp.
- **Good at:** quick brainstorming and rewording right where the team already talks.
- **Privacy:** do not forward, paste or @mention Meta AI into participant enquiries. Draft from an anonymized summary with the fact sheet.
- **Watch out:** Meta AI is a personal assistant. It is **not** the same as the business tools that answer messages on your Page or WhatsApp Business, and it doesn't give anyone permission to post as the Page.
- Meta confirms the core Meta AI experience remains free. Paid **Meta One** bundles exist (see the cost table).

### Microsoft Copilot

- A free consumer assistant, plus Copilot features inside paid **Microsoft 365** plans (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).
- **Good at:** if someone on the team already pays for Microsoft 365, drafting inside Word or explaining a spreadsheet.
- **Watch out:** Microsoft doesn't publish a single model version behind every Copilot feature; the Family/Premium AI benefits are for the subscription owner.
- [Copilot plans (Brazil)](https://www.microsoft.com/pt-br/copilot/pricing/individuals)

### Grok (xAI)

- Flagship model **Grok 4.7**. A new "X Subscription" (announced 12 October 2026) bundles Grok with X and other products.
- **Good at:** searching what's being discussed on X, creative variations.
- **Watch out:** X is not a priority channel for this campaign; there's little reason to pay for it here.
- [The X Subscription](https://x.ai/news/x-subscription)

### Perplexity

- A **search-first** assistant: every answer comes with links to sources. Paid plans let you choose models from several companies.
- **Good at:** finding things with sources — for example, NEAB/NEABI pages at universities, education graduate programs, or teacher associations for [outreach](outreach.md).
- **Watch out:** always open the links; a citation doesn't guarantee the source says what the summary claims. The free plan doesn't let you choose the advanced model.
- [Perplexity pricing](https://www.perplexity.ai/pt/hub/pricing)

### Open models (Llama/Muse, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral)

"Open" models can be downloaded and run on your own computer or server. Examples in 2026: Meta's **Muse Spark Open** (released 15 October 2026), **Qwen3.6-27B**, **DeepSeek V4.1-Flash** (via a low-cost API) and **Mistral Small 4**; Mistral's chat app is now called **Vibe** (formerly Le Chat).

- **Good for:** a technically skilled volunteer with a specific privacy or automation need.
- **Not needed for this team.** "Open" doesn't mean "free to run": you need hardware or a paid host, and setup takes time. A weak model on an old laptop costs more volunteer hours than a free hosted assistant.

### When you see a model selector

| App / choice | Access documented here | First task to try |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Sonnet / Haiku | Free; usage limits apply | Sonnet for a caption with several constraints; Haiku for a short rewrite, then review |
| Claude Opus / Fable | Eligible paid plans; Fable has plan/credit conditions | Optional complex planning only after the free model has failed a real task |
| Gemini Flash-Lite / Flash / Argon | Free / Plus / Pro–Ultra plan listings respectively | Default/light model for captions; optional Argon for a difficult multi-document comparison |
| ChatGPT Chat vs Work/Codex | Different product catalogs; this review does not certify every consumer picker/account entitlement | Use the default available chat model and verify output; do not buy a plan solely to match an API name |

## Best-for table: CONAFROEDU tasks

This is a **suggested order to try**, based on documented features — not a scientific benchmark. This research did not establish a reliable current comparison of Brazilian Portuguese writing for educators.

| Task | Try first | Also good | How to judge the result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook/Instagram captions and speaker intros in natural pt-BR | Claude | ChatGPT, Gemini | Sounds Brazilian, facts unchanged, one clear call to action |
| FAQ and bios from official documents, with sources | Gemini Notebook | Claude Projects | Every claim points to an approved document |
| Finding NEABs, graduate programs, associations with links | Perplexity | Gemini, Claude with search | Official site? Current? Actually about Brazil? |
| Rewording a message quickly inside WhatsApp | Meta AI | — | Tone right, no new "facts" added |

### When (and whether) to pay

The basic organic campaign can run on free plans; some optional generated-media exports require paid commercial rights. Before paying, eligible Brazilian university students aged **18+** can check Google’s [one-year AI Plus student offer](https://gemini.google/br/students/?hl=pt-BR), redeemable through **31 December 2026**. Verification and a payment method are required; it renews at the standard price unless cancelled (currently R$ 24,99/month). This is an individual offer, not a shared committee subscription. Consider paying **only** if free limits interrupt real work several times a week. Then:

1. Pick **one** plan for **one month** — the cheapest that solves the actual bottleneck (for example, Google AI Plus at R$ 24,99 if you're hitting Gemini limits).
2. Use it for a real campaign week.
3. Cancel or keep based on time saved — not on hype.

A more expensive "frontier" model is rarely necessary to rewrite a caption.

## Leaderboards: interesting, not decisive

The [Text Arena](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/text) compares preference votes; [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models) combines different evaluations and specifies model settings. Results change, and a top “max with fallback” API configuration is not necessarily what a free app runs. Neither establishes the best writer for CONAFROEDU. Test **correct, warm Brazilian Portuguese without invented facts** on your own tasks.

## Test them yourself: a 20-minute pt-BR comparison

1. Open two or three free assistants in separate tabs.
2. In each, paste the [CONAFROEDU context block](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block) and the same prompt:

```prompt
Using ONLY the fact sheet above, write in natural Brazilian Portuguese:
1. A 70-word Facebook invitation for CONAFROEDU 2026.
2. A 20-word hook for a Reel.
3. A reply to the WhatsApp question "Tem certificado?" — don't invent rules; use [CONFIRMAR] where needed.
4. A 120-word e-mail inviting a university education graduate program to share the call for papers.
Mark any information you could not find in the fact sheet.
```

3. Copy the answers into one document **without the tool names** (label them A, B, C).
4. Ask two Brazilian team members to score each from 1 to 5 on: **facts correct**, **sounds natural**, **clear**, **respectful and inclusive**, and **how much editing it needs**.
5. Pick the winner as your main "writer". Repeat in a month if things change.

> [!TIP]
> The Portuguese version of this test prompt is on the [pt-BR page](../pt-BR/choosing-ai-tools.md#teste-você-mesmo-comparação-de-20-minutos-em-pt-br).

## Recommended zero-budget starter stack

**Minimum:** one writer (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini), Canva Free, Meta Business Suite and the approved support account. Follow [Set up your tools](setup-tools.md), then [Learn with AI](learning-with-ai.md). In the table, the researcher, second opinion, Meta AI and Perplexity are **optional additions**; do not create all of them during onboarding.

| Job | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Main writer: captions, e-mails, WhatsApp texts, calendar drafts, simple animations | **One free writer** (Claude includes up to five Projects; ChatGPT/Gemini also work with a pasted fact sheet) | R$ 0 |
| Researcher: answers from official documents with citations | **Gemini Free + Gemini Notebook** | R$ 0 |
| Second opinion + images | **ChatGPT Free** | R$ 0 |
| Quick rewording inside WhatsApp | **Meta AI** | R$ 0 |
| Finding partners with sources | **Perplexity Free** | R$ 0 |
| Design: cards, carousels, simple video | **Canva Free** (see [Brand kit](brand-kit.md)) | R$ 0 |
| Scheduling Facebook/Instagram posts | **Meta Business Suite** (see [Scheduling tools](scheduling-tools.md)) | R$ 0 |
| Answering questions | **WhatsApp Business app** + Page inbox (see [Replies & inbox](replies-inbox.md)) | R$ 0 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Keep personal data out of public AI tools.** Don't paste registrant lists, CPFs, payment details, private messages or unpublished papers into any assistant. Use anonymized summaries ("three people asked about the certificate"). Details in [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

### Setup checklist (about 1 hour)

- [ ] Each volunteer creates only the free accounts they need (don’t share passwords), then checks [privacy/training settings](setup-tools.md#privacy).
- [ ] Coordination creates the shared "FICHA OFICIAL" document from [Prompting basics](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block).
- [ ] Content/Design creates a Claude Project (or a pinned document) with the fact sheet and brand voice.
- [ ] **Only if needed:** someone creates a standalone Gemini Notebook "CONAFROEDU 2026 — fontes aprovadas" with the official announcement, the Even3 rules and approved bios.
- [ ] Choose the writer already familiar to the team; run the comparison only if its output causes repeated editing.
- [ ] Agree on the rule: **nothing AI-written is published without a second person checking facts.**

## Image and video tools: quick pointers

Images and video have their own guides; here's the short version so you know what exists.

**Images** — [full guide](image-generation.md):

- For finished cards and posters: **Canva** with real, consented photos and editable text. Don't let an AI generate the event name, dates or prices inside an image — it often misspells Portuguese.
- For illustrations and edits: **ChatGPT Images** or **Gemini** (Google's "Nano Banana" image models), both with free allowances.
- Before generating any image of people or cultural symbols, read [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully](representation-bias.md). Real photos of real participants (with consent) and work by Black Brazilian designers come first.

**Video** — [full guide](video-generation.md):

- Most of your Reels should come from **real footage**: a speaker recording a 20-second phone invitation, or clips from talks. See [Reels, clips & captions](video-repurposing.md).
- Animated text and countdowns can be built with Claude — see [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md).
- Fully AI-generated video (Google's Flow/Gemini Omni and Veo, Kling, Runway and others) uses limited credits and needs careful review. Older guides recommend OpenAI's Sora; it is discontinued (web/app: 26 April; API: 24 September 2026) and is not an option for this campaign, so check [Video generation](video-generation.md) before planning around any tool.
