Today is **Wednesday, 21 October 2026**. CONAFROEDU opens on **Saturday, 5 December** — 45 days away. But the most urgent date is much closer: **paper submissions close on Wednesday, 4 November**, just 14 days from now.

That shapes everything. This campaign is really **two campaigns in a row**:

1. **21 Oct → 4 Nov: recruit authors.** Help teachers, students and researchers turn their work into a submission.
2. **5 Nov → 6 Dec: fill the (virtual) room.** Registrations for listeners, the honest price change on 16 November, Consciência Negra on 20 November, the program, and helping people actually attend.

**What you'll get from this page:**

- The **key dates** at a glance, with what to do on each one.
- **Four team roles** and who owns what.
- A **week-by-week checklist** from today until after the event.
- The **decisions the committee must confirm** — and by when.
- A **minimum plan** if the team falls behind.

> [!NOTE]
> This page is the **action plan** (who does what, when). The detailed post-by-post content calendar is in [Campaign timeline & content calendar](campaign-timeline.md). All dates were checked on the official [Even3 page](https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/) on 21 October 2026; if the committee changes anything, update this plan and the [fact sheet](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block) together.

| Date | What happens | What the team does |

| **Thu 5 Nov** | Last day for 2- and 3-submission packages | **Remove all "submit your paper" calls**; switch to attendance. The packages don't reopen submissions |
| **Sun 15 Nov** | Last day of the current registration price | Honest reminder (after checking checkout) |
| **Mon 16 Nov** | New prices (R$ 60–75) | Update the fact sheet and every queued post |
| **Fri 20 Nov** | **Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra** (national holiday) | A substantive educational contribution — not a sales post |
| **Wed 25 Nov** | Official program due | Publish it (or explain any delay honestly) |
| **Sat 28 Nov** | 7 days to the congress | "Faltam 7 dias" card |
| **Thu 3 – Fri 4 Dec** | Final days | One main final invitation; access and support FAQ |
| **Sat 5 – Sun 6 Dec** | **CONAFROEDU 2026**, 100% online | Support, moderation, consented highlights |
| **From Sun 6 Dec** | Certificates available | "How to download your certificate" post and quick reply |
| **By Tue 15 Dec** | Proceedings (Anais) due | Share the real link when published, or an honest status update |

> [!WARNING]
> **Two dates that are easy to confuse:** the 2- and 3-submission packages are listed until **5 November**, but **submissions close on 4 November**. Never write "you can still submit on the 5th" unless the committee officially extends the deadline. Likewise, **registration stays open until 6 December** — so "starts in 48 hours" is honest in December, but "registration closes in 48 hours" is not.

## Team roles

A small volunteer team works best when every task has **one name** next to it. One person can hold two roles; just don't leave a role empty.

| Role | Owns | Typical weekly time |
|---|---|---|
| **Coordination** (*Coordenação*) | The [fact sheet](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block); talking to the scientific/organizing committee; checking Even3; approving posts; the weekly review | 2–3 h |
| **Content/Design** (*Conteúdo/Design*) | Canva templates, captions drafted with AI, Reels, alt text, the [brand kit](brand-kit.md) | 3–5 h |
| **Social/Community** (*Redes/Comunidade*) | Scheduling in Meta Business Suite, Facebook Page and Event, Stories, the inbox and WhatsApp Business, posting in groups with admin permission | 3–4 h + 15 min daily |
| **Partnerships** (*Parcerias*) | NEABs/NEABIs, graduate programs, associations, speakers' share kits, follow-ups | 2–3 h |

The **committee** (Comissão Organizadora/Científica) isn't a marketing role, but it makes the decisions listed [below](#decisions-to-confirm-with-the-committee). Marketing never announces a decision the committee hasn't made.

### The two rules everyone follows

1. **No fact without the fact sheet.** Dates, prices, rules and names come from the current version of the fact sheet — never from an AI's memory.
2. **Four eyes before publishing.** Anything written or designed with AI is checked by a second person using the [60-second fact check](prompting-basics.md#the-60-second-fact-check).

## Daily and weekly rhythm

- **Every day (15–20 min), Social/Community:** check Page inbox, comments, Event discussion and WhatsApp Business at the agreed hours. Answer with approved quick replies; pass anything about payments, certificates, accessibility or personal cases to Coordination. See [Replies & inbox](replies-inbox.md).
- **Every Monday (30 min), everyone:** look at the numbers — confirmed registrations by category, submissions, link clicks, top questions — and agree on next week's posts. See [Measuring results](measuring-results.md).
- **Posting rhythm:** about **3 substantive Facebook posts a week**, adapted Stories, and extra service reminders only around real deadlines. Adapt the best pieces for Instagram and WhatsApp ([Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md)). More is not better if the team can't review it.

## Week 0 — Wed 21 to Sun 25 Oct: set up and start the author push

**Goal:** a clear, correct call for papers is live everywhere by Friday, and the committee has the decision list.

**Coordination**

- [ ] Run a 45-minute kick-off: assign the four roles, agree on the "four eyes" rule and on who can speak for the committee.
- [ ] Send the committee the [decision list](#decisions-to-confirm-with-the-committee) — especially the **4 November cut-off time**, the **presentation rule** and the **presenter video** question.
- [ ] Create the shared "FICHA OFICIAL" document from the [context block](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block).
- [ ] Open the Even3 page on a phone: do the submission rules, prices and contact match the fact sheet? List any conflicts for the committee.
- [ ] Find out whether an official **CONAFROEDU Facebook Page** exists and who has admin access. We found no verified Page or Event in our research.

**Content/Design**

- [ ] Build the minimum [brand kit](brand-kit.md): one 1080×1350 feed template and one 1080×1920 Story template in Canva Free.
- [ ] Create three cards: **call for papers** (deadline 4/11), **submission formats** (as confirmed), and **what it costs** (registration + the separate R$ 16,98 publication fee per work).
- [ ] Draft the captions with AI using the fact sheet ([Copywriting](copywriting.md)); hand them to Coordination for checking.

**Social/Community**

- [ ] Set up or tidy the Facebook Page ([Facebook playbook](facebook-playbook.md)) and create **one** canonical Facebook Event: "CONAFROEDU 2026 — Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação | Online" ([Events & Groups](facebook-events-groups.md)).
- [ ] Publish and **pin** the call for papers. Schedule the next 3 posts in Meta Business Suite ([Scheduling tools](scheduling-tools.md)).
- [ ] Install WhatsApp Business on the organizer phone and save quick replies: `/submissao`, `/valores`, `/certificado`, `/acesso`, `/humano` — approved facts only.

**Partnerships**

- [ ] Build a shortlist of **10** relevant NEABs/NEABIs, education graduate programs (PPGEs) and associations, starting from the ABPN's [CONNEABS directory](https://abpn.org.br/conneabs-2/) and checking each institution's own site ([Outreach](outreach.md)).
- [ ] Draft 5 personalized invitations with AI; a person reviews and sends each one through an institutional channel.
- [ ] Ask 1–2 confirmed speakers (or committee members) to record a 20-second phone invitation for authors.

## Week 1 — Mon 26 Oct to Sun 1 Nov: the author recruitment push

**Goal:** everyone who could submit knows the deadline, the formats and the full cost — and gets help if stuck.

**Coordination**

- [ ] Chase the committee's answers (target: by **Tue 27 Oct**). Update the fact sheet and every saved AI project the same day.
- [ ] Agree a **prorrogação protocol** now, even if you never use it: the committee decides, Even3 is updated first, then one clear announcement (see [below](#if-the-committee-extends-the-deadline)).
- [ ] Check submission numbers on Even3 on Monday and Thursday.

**Content/Design**

- [ ] Mon: "How to submit in 5 steps" carousel (register → read the rules → prepare the PDF → submit → wait for the review result, within 5 business days).
- [ ] Wed **28 Oct**: "7 days left" checklist for authors.
- [ ] Fri: a short Reel — a teacher explaining a real classroom practice that could become a *práticas exitosas* report (with written consent; no invented example presented as real).
- [ ] Sun **1 Nov**: "3 days left" reminder with the help contact.

**Social/Community**

- [ ] Daily inbox and WhatsApp check; log repeated questions for the FAQ.
- [ ] One Event discussion post: "Dúvidas sobre submissão? Pergunte aqui."
- [ ] Ask admins of 3–5 relevant teacher or graduate-student groups whether the call may be shared; post only where allowed, adapted to each group's rules ([Events & Groups](facebook-events-groups.md)).

**Partnerships**

- [ ] Send the remaining invitations from the shortlist.
- [ ] Send speakers a simple share kit: one card, a ready caption, the Even3 link ([Speaker & partner kits](speaker-partner-kits.md)).
- [ ] One polite follow-up, about a week after the first message, only where it makes sense — never repeated chasing.

## Week 2 — Mon 2 to Sun 8 Nov: close submissions, switch to attendance

**Goal:** a clean finish on 4 November, then no more "submit" messages anywhere.

**Key days**

- [ ] **Mon 2 Nov — Content/Design + Social:** "Before you submit" checklist post (PDF format, A4, same e-mail for registration and submission, registered submitting author).
- [ ] **Tue 3 Nov — Social:** one final reminder in approved channels, with the confirmed cut-off time.
- [ ] **Wed 4 Nov — Social + Coordination:** final-day help window (announce the hours); one last reminder in the morning. No hourly countdowns.
- [ ] **Thu 5 Nov — Social:** remove or edit every submission call: pinned post, Event description, Story highlights, queued posts, WhatsApp quick replies, partner kits. Replace with "Submissões encerradas em 4/11 — inscrições para ouvintes abertas" (unless an extension was officially approved).
- [ ] **Thu 5 Nov — Coordination:** update the fact sheet.

**Rest of the week**

- [ ] **Content/Design:** "You don't need to present to take part" post (listener registration), and "how the online congress works" (only confirmed information).
- [ ] **Partnerships:** invite a Black educator to shape the **20 November** content — agree on the topic, credit, approval and, if possible, compensation. Do this now; it can't be improvised the week before.
- [ ] **Coordination:** ask the committee about the 40-hour certificate rules and the publication workflow (needed for week 3).

## Week 3 — Mon 9 to Sun 15 Nov: why attend, and an honest price reminder

**Goal:** show the value of participating, and tell people the true date the price changes.

- [ ] **Coordination:** check the real checkout prices on Even3 before any price post. Prices displayed on 21 Oct: R$ 55,00 (listener), R$ 55,00 / R$ 60,00 / R$ 70,00 (undergraduate / postgraduate / teacher + 1 submission) until 15 Nov.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** an educational post that helps teachers in their classrooms (for example, a question about curriculum and ERER — *Educação para as Relações Étnico-Raciais*), with a light link to the program.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** confirmed speaker introductions (approved bios and photos only).
- [ ] **Content/Design:** the certificate explainer — **only after the committee confirms the conditions**. Use the careful wording from [Copywriting](copywriting.md).
- [ ] **Social:** price reminder about one week before (Mon 9 or Tue 10 Nov) and one near the change (Fri 13 or Sat 14 Nov): "O valor muda em 16/11." No "últimas vagas".
- [ ] **Partnerships:** send partner-specific share kits for attendance (a teacher-focused version and a student-focused version).

## Week 4 — Mon 16 to Sun 22 Nov: Novembro Negro and 20 November

**Goal:** contribute something genuinely useful on Consciência Negra, and keep attendance messages honest.

- [ ] **Mon 16 Nov — Coordination + Social:** update the fact sheet, saved AI projects and every queued post with the new prices.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** prepare the 20 November piece with the invited educator — for example, a carousel "20 de novembro no calendário; ERER no cotidiano" explaining Lei 10.639/2003, Lei 11.645/2008 and Lei 14.759/2023 (which made 20 November a national holiday), with links to the official texts.
- [ ] **Fri 20 Nov — Social:** publish the educational contribution. If you also invite people to the congress, do it in a **separate, short** post. No submission call (they're closed), no "sale" framing, no stock images of generic "African" symbols.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** one speaker clip or short conversation.
- [ ] **Everyone:** read [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully](representation-bias.md) before designing anything for this week.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 20 November is a holiday, and many educators will be at school or community events. Treat it as a moment to **contribute**, not to sell. Credit the people whose knowledge you share, and follow through after the date.

## Week 5 — Mon 23 to Sun 29 Nov: program and "how to take part"

**Goal:** people who registered know exactly when and how to join.

- [ ] **Wed 25 Nov — Coordination + Social:** publish the official program (the advertised target date). If it's late, say so and give a new date.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** audience guides — "3 sessões para quem é professora da educação básica", "para quem está na pós-graduação" — from the confirmed program only.
- [ ] **Coordination:** confirm with the committee: transmission platform, how registered people receive access, recordings, accessibility arrangements, support hours during the event, and the certificate setup in Even3.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** "Como acessar" FAQ card and Event update.
- [ ] **Sat 28 Nov — Social:** the "Faltam 7 dias" card (you can animate it — see [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md)).
- [ ] **Partnerships:** one final, voluntary share request to partners and speakers.

## Week 6 — Mon 30 Nov to Sun 6 Dec: final week and the congress

**Goal:** a smooth event with fast, kind support.

- [ ] **Mon 30 Nov — Social:** participation checklist (time zone, access link route, certificate conditions).
- [ ] **Wed 2 Dec — Social:** "Como acessar" reminder.
- [ ] **Thu 3 Dec — Social:** the main final invitation (program, access, Even3 link). **Fri 4 Dec:** updated FAQ / Story.
- [ ] **Coordination:** test access end-to-end; build a support rota for both days (the Even3 page shows an overall range of 08:00–22:00, Brasília time — confirm the real session times); define who handles urgent problems.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** prepare 3–4 "happening now" templates and get **written permission** before filming or screenshotting anyone.
- [ ] **Sat 5 – Sun 6 Dec — Social/Community:** session reminders, support answers, moderation, consented highlights. Never post participant-only meeting links publicly.

## After the event — Mon 7 to Tue 15 Dec: deliver the promises

- [ ] **Social:** "How to download your certificate" post and WhatsApp quick reply (certificates available from 6 December; confirm the steps).
- [ ] **Everyone:** thank speakers, partners and participants by name where they agreed to be named.
- [ ] **Content/Design:** an honest recap with authorized photos and clips — no invented attendance numbers. Ask permission before using any testimonial.
- [ ] **By Tue 15 Dec — Coordination:** share the real proceedings link and identifiers when published, or a transparent status update.
- [ ] **Coordination:** a 45-minute retrospective. Write a short **learning log**: what brought registrations, which partners responded, which questions kept coming, what took too long. For the next edition, start the call for papers 8–12 weeks before the event (see [Campaign timeline](campaign-timeline.md)).

## Decisions to confirm with the committee

Marketing can't answer these alone. Until each one is decided, keep it marked `[CONFIRMAR]` in the fact sheet and **don't let any AI or volunteer fill the gap**.

| # | Decision | Why it matters | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cut-off time and time zone** for 4 November | "Até 4/11" without a time causes last-minute confusion | Tue 27 Oct |
| 2 | **Presentation: mandatory or optional?** | The rules and the FAQ on Even3 currently disagree | Tue 27 Oct |
| 3 | **Presenter video** for works submitted near the deadline whose review result arrives after 4 Nov | The review can take up to 5 business days | Tue 27 Oct |
| 4 | Is **resumo simples** accepted? | The rules list it; the FAQ and announcement don't | Tue 27 Oct |
| 5 | **Prorrogação** (deadline extension): yes or no? | Must be decided and set up in Even3 before any announcement | Mon 2 Nov at the latest |
| 6 | **2/3-submission packages** on sale until 5 Nov while submissions close on 4 Nov | Buyers may expect to submit on the 5th | Fri 30 Oct |
| 7 | **40-hour certificate**: who qualifies, what attendance is required, how it's issued | Most common question; can't be invented | Mon 9 Nov |
| 8 | **Publication**: is the R$ 16,98 fee per work or per author? Publisher and DOI process? | Authors need the full cost and process | Mon 9 Nov |
| 9 | **Official Facebook Page/Event** and who are the admins | No verified Page found | Now |
| 10 | **Transmission platform, access, recordings, accessibility** (Libras, captions) | Needed for "how to take part" posts | Mon 23 Nov |
| 11 | **Speakers**: final list, approved bios, photo permissions | No speaker post without approval | Rolling, by 25 Nov |
| 12 | **Partner coupons** (e.g., a code for partner NEABs) | A discount reduces revenue; terms must be approved and tested | Only if wanted, by 9 Nov |
| 13 | **Support hours** to publish in auto-replies | People need to know when a human answers | Fri 23 Oct |
| 14 | **Who approves posts** and who speaks publicly for the committee | Avoids contradictory answers | Wed 21 Oct |

### If the committee extends the deadline

1. The committee decides and records the new date **and time** (horário de Brasília).
2. Coordination updates Even3 **first**, then the fact sheet.
3. Social publishes **one** clear announcement: old date, new date and time, which formats, whether people who already submitted need to do anything.
4. Update everything the same day: pinned post, Event, Story highlights, queued posts, WhatsApp quick replies, partner kits.
5. Never say "a pedidos" (by popular demand) or "última chance" unless it's true.

Template (in Portuguese, as it will be posted) — fill in only what the committee actually decided:

```text
A Comissão Científica prorrogou o prazo de submissão de [data anterior] para [nova data], às [horário] (horário de Brasília). [Modalidades e condições mantidas/alteradas, conforme decisão.] Quem já enviou trabalho [não precisa reenviar — só se isso estiver confirmado]. Normas e envio: https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/
```

## If you fall behind: the minimum plan

Life happens — volunteers get sick, classes pile up. If the team can only do the essentials, do **these**, in this order:

1. **Keep the facts right.** Fact sheet updated; no wrong date or price anywhere.
2. **Answer people.** Daily inbox and WhatsApp check. An unanswered question loses a registration faster than a missing post.
3. **One post per key date:** 28 Oct, 3 Nov, 4 Nov, 5 Nov (switch), 14 Nov, 20 Nov, 25 Nov, 28 Nov, 3 Dec, 6 Dec (certificates).
4. **Ten personal partner invitations** — they usually do more than ten generic posts.
5. **Skip** animations, generated video and new channels. A clear Canva card with a real photo is enough.

## Copy-paste: plan your week with AI

Use this every Monday in your main assistant, after pasting the [fact sheet](prompting-basics.md#the-conafroedu-context-block). The team posts in Portuguese, so the prompt asks for Portuguese output; the full Portuguese version is on the [pt-BR page](../pt-BR/conafroedu-sprint.md#copie-e-cole-planeje-a-semana-com-ia).

```prompt
You are helping a small volunteer team promote CONAFROEDU 2026. Use ONLY the fact sheet above.
Today is [DATE]. This week is [WEEK, e.g. "Week 3, 9–15 Nov"]. Our capacity: about [X] hours in total.
Roles: Coordination, Content/Design, Social/Community, Partnerships.

Create a plan for this week in Brazilian Portuguese with:
1. 3 Facebook posts (day, goal, audience, format, one-line hook, call to action).
2. Which of them to adapt for Instagram and WhatsApp.
3. The task list per role, with an estimate in minutes.
4. Any date in the fact sheet that falls this week and what it requires.
Rules: don't ask for paper submissions after 4/11 unless the fact sheet says the deadline was extended; no fake urgency; anything not in the fact sheet must be marked [CONFIRMAR].
```
