**What you'll get from this page:** a plan to treat the online congress as a **content goldmine**. You'll learn what to record before, during and after CONAFROEDU 2026; how to run a Live with the tools that actually work for a new Page; a step-by-step **"1 talk → 6 Reels"** workflow with copy-paste prompts for picking clips and writing captions; how to make accurate Portuguese captions (an accessibility must); when AI dubbing and avatars are acceptable; and which music you can legally use. Free tools come first.

> [!NOTE] Why this matters more than AI video
> Meta's 2026 guidance favors **original content made by the Page itself** — your own speakers, your own organizers — over copies and minimally edited clips ([Meta, March 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/)). An online congress produces hours of exactly that. You don't need to generate video; you need to **capture, cut and caption** it. (For generated video, see [Video generation](video-generation.md).)

## The short version

1. **Get permission in writing first** — for recording, clipping, publishing and (separately) any translation, dubbing or avatar.
2. **Before (now → 4 Dec):** phone invitations from confirmed speakers, short author testimonials, one "warm-up" Live.
3. **During (5–6 Dec):** record everything you're allowed to; one person notes great moments with timestamps.
4. **After (6 Dec →):** cut each authorized talk into 3–6 Reels, caption them in Portuguese, publish over the following weeks.
5. **Free stack:** phone camera + CapCut (or Adobe Express) + a free AI assistant for clip selection and captions. Paid tools (OpusClip, Descript) are optional speed-ups.
6. **Download Facebook Live recordings quickly** — Facebook now deletes Live replays after 30 days.

## Step 0: permissions (do this before anything else)

A talk being public does **not** give you permission to cut it into Reels. Ask each speaker and author, in writing (a WhatsApp message or e-mail is fine as a record), and list each use separately:

| Use | Ask separately? |
|---|---|
| Record the talk / interview | ✅ |
| Publish short clips on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube | ✅ |
| Use their name, photo and institution in posts | ✅ |
| Translate captions into another language | ✅ |
| **AI dubbing** (their voice speaking another language) | ✅ — separate, explicit |
| **Voice cloning or a digital avatar** of them | ✅ — separate, explicit; never assume |
| Edit or retouch their image | ✅ |

Also confirm with the committee: **where the congress will be streamed, whether sessions are recorded, and who owns the recordings.** These weren't public at the time of writing.

```prompt
Write a short, friendly message in Brazilian Portuguese from the organizing committee of CONAFROEDU 2026 (Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação, 5–6 December 2026, online) to a confirmed speaker. Ask for permission for each of these uses separately, so they can answer yes/no to each: (1) recording their talk, (2) publishing short clips (up to 90 seconds) on the congress's Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, (3) using their name, photo and institution in posts, (4) translated captions. State that we will NOT use AI dubbing, voice cloning or avatars without a separate request. Say they can review clips before publication if they wish. Warm, respectful academic tone. Max 150 words.
```

> [!WARNING] LGPD and sensitive material
> Faces and voices are personal data under the LGPD. Keep raw recordings in a restricted folder, don't upload unpublished papers or participant lists to AI tools, and avoid filming minors. Note: CapCut's international terms (April 2026) give the service a broad license over what you upload and treat uploads as non-confidential — think twice before uploading an unreleased recording. More in [Ethics, policies & LGPD](ethics-legal.md).

| **Before — registrations** | 5 Nov → 4 Dec | Speaker phone invitations; warm-up Live; short Q&A answers | Speaker invitation Reels, Live, quote cards |

## Before the congress

### Speaker invitation videos (recorded on a phone)

This is the highest-value, lowest-cost video you can make. Send confirmed speakers this simple guide:

1. **Hold the phone vertically** (9:16), at eye level, about an arm's length away.
2. **Face a window** for soft light; avoid a bright window behind you.
3. **Quiet room**; earphones with a mic improve sound a lot.
4. **15–30 seconds**, in this order: who you are → one idea or question from your talk → invitation.
5. Record 2–3 takes and send the best one **as a file** (WhatsApp "document" mode keeps quality better than sending as video).

```prompt
Write a 20–25 second video script in Brazilian Portuguese for a confirmed speaker to record on their phone, inviting people to CONAFROEDU 2026 (Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação, 5 and 6 December 2026, 100% online, registration on Even3). Speaker: [NAME], [ROLE/INSTITUTION — as approved]. Talk topic: [APPROVED TOPIC]. Structure: (1) one-line introduction, (2) one concrete question or idea from the talk that matters to teachers, (3) invitation with "inscrições pelo Even3, link na publicação". Natural spoken language, short sentences, no exaggerated claims, no promises about certificates beyond what is written here. Give two versions: one more formal, one warmer.
```

> [!TIP]
> Offer the script as a suggestion only — speakers sound best in their own words. Never write a script that makes a speaker claim something they didn't approve.

### Author testimonials

Authors submitting papers (and the presenter video due by **4 November**, for those presenting) are your best ambassadors. Ask a few — with permission — for a 20-second clip: "Estou submetendo meu trabalho ao CONAFROEDU porque…". Presenter videos sent for evaluation must **not** be reused publicly unless the author explicitly agrees.

### A warm-up Live

One rehearsed 15–25-minute conversation before the congress is enough. Suggested run-of-show: 2 min introductions → 10 min on one real practice or research question → 5 min answering questions collected in advance → 2 min on how to register (or submit, while open). Title it clearly, e.g. **"Conversa de aquecimento — CONAFROEDU"**, so nobody confuses it with the congress itself.

## During: Lives and streaming

### Which route to use

| Route | Cost | Requirements and caveats |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Live / Live Producer** (native) | Free | Meta's eligibility rules apply. StreamYard's documentation describes them as: a Facebook account **at least 60 days old**, a Page/professional profile with **at least 100 followers**, and permission to create content on the Page ([StreamYard help](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043291412-Connect-a-Facebook-Page)). Rehearse before announcing. |
| **StreamYard → Facebook + YouTube at once** | **Paid plans** (a 7-day trial is not a free plan) | Multistreaming is a paid feature ([StreamYard](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045622851-How-to-Multi-stream)). Check monthly price before committing. |
| **StreamYard → existing Facebook Event (RTMP)** | Plan-dependent | Facebook comments and viewer counts are **not** imported into the studio — assign a moderator inside Facebook ([StreamYard](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059249772-Connect-an-existing-Facebook-Event)). Keep the stream key private. |
| **YouTube Live, linked from Facebook** | Free | Requires channel verification and enabling live streaming in advance ([YouTube help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2474026)). Good fallback if the Facebook Page isn't eligible. |

> [!WARNING] A brand-new Page can't go live in time
> A Facebook account created today can't reach 60 days before 5 December. Stream through an established, authorized organizer's account and grow genuine Page followers — or use the YouTube fallback and share the link. Don't buy followers or borrow accounts.

**Also:** the old Facebook **Watch Party** button was retired in 2021. If you want a "watch together" moment in a Group, simply agree a time with the admins, share the public Live or YouTube link, and have someone lead the discussion in the comments.

### Roles for a Live (even a small one)

- **Host:** introduces, keeps time.
- **Producer:** controls the stream tool, slides, and a backup recording.
- **Moderator:** answers comments, removes abuse, collects questions. For anti-racist education, agree in advance how to handle racist comments and privacy.

### Save the recordings

Facebook automatically removes Live recordings after **30 days** ([Meta, Feb 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/updating-our-facebook-live-video-storage-policy/)). **Download the master the same day** and keep it in your own storage. Meta also lets you clip Live moments into Reels to keep them beyond that window. If the congress runs on 5–6 December, the replays would disappear in early January.

**During the congress, assign one "clip spotter"** per session: they write down timestamps of strong moments ("00:14:20 — great classroom example about Lei 10.639"). This saves hours later.

## Workflow: 1 talk → 6 Reels (step by step)

**Inputs:** written permission, the original recording (not a re-downloaded copy), the speaker's approved name/title/institution, and about 2–3 hours of volunteer time for the first talk (it gets faster).

### Step 1 — Get a transcript with timestamps

Options, cheapest first:

1. **CapCut:** import the talk → Captions → **Auto captions** → choose Portuguese (if available on your account). Export or copy the caption text with times.
2. **Descript free plan:** upload and transcribe (check your media-hour limit).
3. **OpusClip free:** upload and it transcribes while suggesting clips (export within 3 days).

### Step 2 — Correct the transcript

Before asking AI anything, fix names, institutions, acronyms (ERER, NEABI, ABPN), law numbers (10.639/2003, 11.645/2008) and specialist terms. AI picks clips based on the text — errors here spread everywhere.

### Step 3 — Ask AI to propose clips (not invent them)

```prompt
You are a content editor for an education congress in Brazil (CONAFROEDU 2026, on Africanidades and anti-racist education). Use ONLY the transcript below. Select up to six excerpts that could become 20–60-second vertical videos for teachers and researchers. Aim for variety: a practical classroom tip, a surprising insight, a short story, a research finding, an answer to an audience question, and an inspiring closing thought. For each excerpt give: start/end timestamp, the exact words, the core idea, any context the viewer needs, a short faithful title (max 8 words, in Brazilian Portuguese), and why it is useful for this audience. Do not invent quotes, data or timestamps. Do not cut caveats that change the meaning. If timestamps look unreliable, say so. Do not give "virality scores".

[PASTE CORRECTED, AUTHORIZED TRANSCRIPT WITH TIMESTAMPS]
```

### Step 4 — Watch around every suggestion

Watch 30 seconds before and after each proposed clip. Reject any that depend on a slide the viewer can't see, cut a "but…" that changes the meaning, or could embarrass the speaker out of context. Keep the best **six**; start by editing **three** (a tip, a story, an opinion with its evidence).

### Step 5 — Edit in CapCut (per clip)

1. New project → import the talk → set the canvas to **9:16**.
2. Trim to the chosen timestamps; keep a natural lead-in.
3. **Reframe:** keep the speaker's face centered. If a slide matters, use a split layout (slide on top, face below) rather than cropping it out.
4. Add **auto captions** → proofread every line (next section).
5. Add a **lower-third** with the speaker's exact approved name and institution.
6. Add a 2–3-second **end card**: "CONAFROEDU 2026" + the relevant action (before the event: "Inscrições pelo Even3"; after: "Assista/acompanhe os anais" — confirm with the committee what's available).
7. Export **1080 × 1920, 30 FPS, MP4**.

### Step 6 — Write the post copy

```prompt
Write 3 alternative Facebook/Instagram captions in Brazilian Portuguese for a vertical video clip from CONAFROEDU 2026 (Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação). Speaker: [APPROVED NAME, ROLE, INSTITUTION]. Clip summary: [CORE IDEA]. Exact quote to feature (do not change it): "[QUOTE]". Phase: [before the congress — registrations open on Even3 / after the congress — thank you + proceedings]. Each caption: 60–110 words, opens with the idea from the clip (not with the event name), credits the speaker, has one clear call to action, and ends with at most 3 relevant hashtags from this list: #CONAFROEDU #EducaçãoAntirracista #Africanidades #CongressoOnline #PesquisaEmEducação. Natural, warm academic tone. No exaggerated claims, no "últimas vagas", no engagement bait. Mark any quote as literal.
```

Then ask for an on-screen cover title and alt text:

```prompt
For the same clip, suggest: (1) five on-screen cover titles in Brazilian Portuguese, max 6 words each, faithful to what the speaker said; (2) a short accessible description of the video for the caption (what is seen and who speaks), without guessing anyone's race, religion, disability or emotions.
```

### Step 7 — Review, approve, schedule

- A second person checks names, accents, the quote and the link.
- Send the clip to the speaker if you promised a review.
- Schedule over 2–3 weeks — not all six on one day — using [Meta Business Suite](scheduling-tools.md).
- Put the Even3 link in the Facebook caption: https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/ (on Instagram, links in captions aren't clickable — point to the bio/Story link).

> [!TIP] What 6 Reels can look like
> 1) Practical tip · 2) Surprising insight · 3) Short story · 4) Research finding · 5) Answer to an audience question · 6) Closing thought + thank you. Each one should make sense on its own, without having seen the talk.

## Auto-captions in Portuguese (accessibility)

Many people watch with the sound off, and captions are essential for Deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers. Automatic captions are a **draft**, not a finished product ([W3C captions guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/)).

**Caption style rules:**

- Short lines (2 lines max), high contrast, readable at phone size.
- Place them away from the mouth, key slide text and the app's buttons (bottom and right edges).
- Include relevant **non-speech sounds** ("[aplausos]", "[risos]") and identify speakers when there's more than one.
- Avoid decorative fonts and excessive emojis.

**Proofreading checklist for pt-BR auto-captions:**

- [ ] Accents and cedillas: educação, inscrições, participação, Consciência Negra, afro-brasileira.
- [ ] Proper names exactly as approved (don't let AI "correct" an unusual name).
- [ ] Acronyms and laws: ERER, NEABI, Lei 10.639/2003, Lei 11.645/2008.
- [ ] Numbers and dates ("5 e 6 de dezembro").
- [ ] Words the speaker didn't say (auto-captions sometimes invent words in noisy parts).
- [ ] Timing: each line appears while it's being said.

Also put essential information (dates, link, how to register) in the **post text**, not only in the video. Mention Libras interpretation only if the committee confirms it.

## AI dubbing and translation: use with care

**Default:** if a guest speaks English or Spanish, keep their **original voice** and add accurate **Portuguese captions**. It's cheaper, more honest and avoids making them appear to speak Portuguese.

Consider AI dubbing only if it clearly improves access **and** the speaker approves both the translated script and the synthetic voice.

| Tool | What it does | Free? | Rights caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HeyGen** | Video translation with lip-sync; distinguishes **Portuguese (Brazil)** from Portugal ([languages](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/11391941-video-translation-languages-we-support)) | 1–3 watermarked videos/month (varies by region) | Digital twins require a consent recording from the actual person |
| **ElevenLabs Dubbing** | Translated voice track | Yes, watermarked | **Free plan has no commercial license** — don't use Free for the campaign |
| **Descript** | Translation and dubbing on some plans | Limited | Check plan and Portuguese support first |

**A careful localization process:**

1. Correct the original transcript (names, terms).
2. Translate meaning into natural Brazilian Portuguese — not word-for-word.
3. A native reviewer checks terms, dates and tone; keep a pronunciation list for names.
4. **The speaker approves the translated script** before any synthetic voice is made.
5. Review the rendered audio: stress, pauses, names, lip-sync, and any invented words in silent parts.
6. Keep the original-audio version and the subtitle file. Label the dubbed version clearly.

Suggested label: **"Dublagem em português criada com IA e aprovada pelo palestrante."** Also turn on Meta's AI label — realistic synthetic audio requires disclosure ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)).

> [!CAUTION]
> Never use regenerated or cloned speech to put new words in a speaker's mouth — not even "small fixes".

## Avatars: rarely the right choice here

AI avatar tools (HeyGen, Synthesia, Captions) can turn a script into a talking presenter. For CONAFROEDU, a real organizer on a phone is almost always better. If you do use one:

- **A real person's digital twin** needs their explicit, separate consent for that specific use. HeyGen requires a consent recording from the person — that's the tool's check, not a replacement for your own written agreement.
- **Stock avatars** have their own terms. Synthesia, for example, requires express permission for stock avatars in paid/promoted ads; organic permission doesn't automatically cover a later boost.
- **Free plans** usually watermark exports; HeyGen Free is 1–3 videos/month up to 1 minute; Synthesia lists a free Basic plan (current minute allowance unclear).
- Label it: **"Apresentador virtual. Texto revisado pela organização."** and use Meta's AI label.
- **Never** present an avatar as a real speaker, author or participant.

## Music: what you can legally use

| Option | Use it? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **No music / speech only** | ✅ Best default | A clear voice is what matters in an academic clip. |
| **Meta Sound Collection** | ✅ For Facebook/Instagram | Meta describes it as cleared for commercial uses, unlike the regular music library ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/402084904469945)). Check the track; don't assume the same file is cleared for WhatsApp or YouTube. |
| **Trending Instagram/Facebook songs** | ⚠️ Avoid for promotion | The regular library is for personal/non-commercial use. |
| **CapCut "commercial" sounds** | ⚠️ Not for Meta | CapCut's current terms limit Commercial Sounds to CapCut/TikTok/TikTok for Business unless separately cleared. |
| **Suno (AI music)** | ⚠️ Paid plans only | Free has no downloads and no commercial rights. Paid plans grant commercial use for songs downloaded while subscribed. |
| **Udio** | ❌ | Downloads were disabled in 2026. |
| **Original/commissioned music** | ✅ With a written agreement | A great way to involve Black Brazilian musicians — pay or credit fairly and record the permissions. |

## Final checklist

- [ ] Written permission for each use, per speaker/author (dubbing/avatars separately).
- [ ] Committee confirmed streaming platform, recording policy and recording ownership.
- [ ] Facebook Live eligibility tested in Live Producer (or YouTube fallback ready).
- [ ] Recordings downloaded the same day (Facebook deletes Live replays after 30 days).
- [ ] Transcript corrected before asking AI to pick clips.
- [ ] Each clip watched in context; no meaning changed; quotes literal.
- [ ] Portuguese captions proofread (accents, names, laws, numbers).
- [ ] Lower-third with approved name and institution; end card with the right CTA for the phase.
- [ ] Music cleared for the platform (or none).
- [ ] AI dubbing/avatars approved, labeled in the caption and with Meta's AI label.
- [ ] Clips scheduled over several weeks; expired CTAs (e.g. submissions) removed.
- [ ] Masters, caption files and permission records saved outside Facebook.
