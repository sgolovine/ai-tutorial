## CRITICAL

### C01 — An incoming WhatsApp enquiry is treated as permission for later campaign messages

- **Files / location:** `content/en/copywriting.md:262`, “WhatsApp messages”; `content/pt-BR/copywriting.md:262`, “Mensagens de WhatsApp”.
- **Problem:** The rule permits messaging people who requested updates **or who wrote first**. In a section about campaign updates, the second condition permits adding someone who merely asked about a certificate or payment to later promotional reminders. The section title and the sample opt-in message do not remove this explicit exception.
- **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/), updated 23 September 2026, §1 requires both provision of the contact identifier and opt-in for subsequent messages/calls. §2 separately permits replies in the Platform's customer-service window; it does not turn an enquiry into marketing permission. The policy covers the Business app as well as the Platform.
- **Fix:** Replace the bullet with: **“Envie lembretes e novidades apenas a quem autorizou esse tipo de mensagem. Se alguém escrever primeiro, responda à dúvida; antes de enviar divulgação posterior, peça e registre a autorização. Número salvo, participação em grupo e clique em ‘Tenho interesse’ não são autorização.”** Translate the same distinction into EN. Keep service replies and recurring campaign updates separate.

### C02 — Reusable speaker cards assign every talk to 5 December

- **Files / location:** `content/en/image-prompts.md:102`, recipe 2; `content/pt-BR/image-prompts.md:99`; `content/en/motion-graphics-claude.md:161`, animated speaker prompt; `content/pt-BR/motion-graphics-claude.md:159`.
- **Problem:** The copy-paste fields make the time a placeholder but hard-code **5 December**. Reusing either template for a session on 6 December produces a wrong public session date. In the animation prompt, the instruction to preserve the supplied text makes the error especially likely to survive generation.
- **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** [Official Even3 event](https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/) explicitly describes a two-day congress on 5–6 December. The reusable templates are not restricted to confirmed sessions on the first day. This is also reproducible by mentally filling a 6 December session into the supplied prompts: the output is expressly told to retain “5 de dezembro”.
- **Fix:** Use **`[DATA DA SESSÃO, CONFORME A PROGRAMAÇÃO] · [HORÁRIO CONFIRMADO] (horário de Brasília)`** in all four places. Require the session date and time to be copied together from the approved program.

### C03 — The “ready for Reels” downloads and rendering commands produce 24 FPS instead of the documented 30 FPS minimum

- **Files / location:** `content/en/motion-graphics-claude.md:33`, `:350–386`; `content/pt-BR/motion-graphics-claude.md:33`, `:348–384`; `public/examples/render-frames.mjs:26`; `public/examples/countdown.mp4`; `public/examples/quote.mp4`. Cross-reference: `content/{en,pt-BR}/video-generation.md:192`.
- **Problem:** The technical route defaults to 24 FPS, its stop-motion example explicitly outputs 24 FPS, and the supplied MP4s have that frame rate. This contradicts the guide's shared Facebook/Instagram target and its claim that the downloads are ready for Reels. A working local MP4 is not evidence that it meets the stated upload specification.
- **Evidence, retrieved/tested 2026-10-21:** [Instagram Reel size and aspect ratios](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1038071743007909), read successfully in the collaborative browser after the web extractor was blocked, specifies a minimum of 30 FPS. Direct MP4 timing-table inspection found **288 frames / 12 seconds = 24 FPS** for countdown and **336 / 14 = 24 FPS** for quote. The script's default and FFmpeg input frame rate explain the result. This finding does not claim that every Instagram uploader will reject 24 FPS.
- **Fix:** Set the renderer's default to 30 and regenerate both downloads. Show explicit commands such as **`node render-frames.mjs countdown.html 12 countdown 30`** and **`node render-frames.mjs quote.html 14 quote 30`**. Change stop-motion output to `fps=30`, retaining 8 input poses/second. Make the WebM conversion explicitly output 30 FPS too, and verify metadata after export. Update the descriptive text and download sizes if they change.

## MAJOR

### M01 — The free author-card workflow ends with a paid Canva sharing feature

- **Files / location:** `content/en/image-prompts.md:24` and `:126–127`; `content/pt-BR/image-prompts.md:21` and `:123–124`.
- **Problem:** The cookbook defaults to Canva Free, then tells the team to create a Template link so authors can fill their own cards. It does not identify the plan requirement or give a free alternative. Volunteers can build the design and only discover the blocker at distribution time.
- **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** [Canva: Share your design as a template link](https://www.canva.com/help/share-template-link/) limits creation/sharing of Template links to specified paid or institutional plans. Receiving a template is a different permission. The same page also describes a paid-access condition for recipients sharing the creator's work-email domain.
- **Fix:** **“Criar ‘Link de modelo’ exige um plano elegível; não está incluído no Canva gratuito. No caminho gratuito, a equipe duplica o modelo, preenche os dados autorizados e envia o PNG pronto a cada autora ou autor. Se já houver um plano elegível, teste o link com uma conta externa antes de distribuir.”** Mirror in EN. Do not replace this with an editable link to the single master, which invites accidental overwriting.

### M02 — Gemini's API reference-image limit is presented as an app capability

- **Files / location:** `content/en/image-generation.md:62`; `content/pt-BR/image-generation.md:62`, model comparison.
- **Problem:** The **Gemini app** row advertises up to 14 reference images for Nano Banana 2.1. That number comes from the developer model API. The documented app upload limit is lower, so a novice attempting the advertised workflow may be unable to submit it.
- **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** [Gemini API image-generation guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation) documents the model's 14-reference capability. [Gemini Apps upload help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14903178?hl=en), “Upload files & code” and “File types & upload limits”, documents up to **10 files per prompt**, subject to availability. A model limit does not establish an identical consumer UI limit.
- **Fix:** App row: **“Edição conversando e combinação de imagens de referência; o app documenta até 10 arquivos por prompt, sujeito à disponibilidade.”** Put the 14-image API capability in a clearly marked optional developer note, if it is useful at all for this team.

A ajuda lista três vídeos no total, de até três minutos; confira o contador da sua conta antes de planejar.”** Keep the current monthly creation allowance in the avatar section only. Link the feature-specific source next to the translation row.

### M05 — The Live warning confuses the age of the Page with the age of the account

- **Problem:** The heading says a brand-new **Page** cannot go live in time. The supporting paragraph correctly discusses a new **account**. This can make the committee abandon Live on a newly established event Page even if an existing organizer account can manage it and it reaches the follower threshold.

Confira no Live Producer antes de anunciar.”** Replace the StreamYard-only citation with this primary Meta source.

### M06 — The quote animation's small source text fails the normal-text contrast target on a phone

- **Files / location:** `public/examples/quote.html:22–26`, `:70`; linked example and quote prompt in `content/en/motion-graphics-claude.md:30–38`, `:175–186`; corresponding pt-BR `:30–38`, `:173–184`.
- **Problem:** Cream `#f5e9d7` on terracotta `#b4532a` has approximately **4.16:1** contrast. That can work for large text, but the source line is smaller. At the tested 375-pixel-wide viewport its computed size was **14.25 CSS px**, weight 600, so the large-text exception does not apply. The example teaches an accessibility review while supplying small source text below the normal-text target.
- **Evidence, retrieved/tested 2026-10-21:** Local CSS inspection and browser measurement; luminance calculated from the declared colors using the [W3C contrast definition and thresholds](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): normal text needs 4.5:1, large text 3:1. The final MP4 was also inspected at a late frame. This is a small-text finding, not a claim that every use of this palette fails.
- **Fix:** Give the source line an opaque cream panel with dark-brown text, or use a sufficiently darker terracotta background. Check at the **displayed phone size**, then regenerate the example MP4/GIF. Add the same small-text contrast requirement to the prompt so newly generated variants do not reproduce the problem.

### M07 — The resizing workflow does not say to extract the background before sending the master to AI

- **Files / location:** `content/en/image-generation.md:206–217`; `content/pt-BR/image-generation.md:206–217`, “one design, every format”.
- **Problem:** The workflow starts from the approved Canva master, which may contain a real portrait, logo and all event text, then tells the reader to ask AI to extend “this image”. It omits exporting/selecting **only the text-free background**. A volunteer can reasonably upload the finished master and rely on the prompt's “unchanged” instruction to preserve everything. That risks redrawn names/faces and sends a portrait to an AI service without the separate upload permission discussed later on the page.
- **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** This follows directly from Workflow 1's portrait/text master and Workflow 3's missing input-preparation step. The page itself warns at `:234–237` that preservation instructions are not guarantees and publishing permission does not automatically cover AI upload. [OpenAI's image editing help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11084440-images-in-chatgpt) describes editing an uploaded image; it does not promise that a natural-language preservation instruction protects every other pixel.
- **Fix:** Before the extension prompt: **“Exporte somente a ilustração de fundo, sem texto, logo nem retratos. Envie apenas esse arquivo ao gerador. Depois recoloque o fundo ampliado no Canva e preserve as camadas originais de texto, logo e foto. Se não houver fundo separado, complete as bordas com cor sólida no Canva.”** Mirror in EN. Mental test: a finished speaker card is an unsafe input; its isolated abstract background is the intended input.

### M08 — The Portuguese research table turns skin-tone homogeneity into face homogeneity

- **Files / location:** `content/pt-BR/representation-bias.md:41`, Wilson/Ghosh/Caliskan row; compare `content/en/representation-bias.md:41`.
- **Problem:** The pt-BR row says **“rostos uniformizados”**. The cited study measures skin tones and their variability, not facial morphology or cloned facial features. The page cites separate research for face homogenization, but that does not justify assigning this result to Wilson et al. The translation changes what the study demonstrated.
- **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** [Wilson, Ghosh & Caliskan, Bias Amplification in Stable Diffusion's Representation of Stigma Through Skin Tones and Their Homogeneity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17465) explicitly describes skin-tone variability and homogenization of **skin tones**. Its AIES 2024 attribution is valid despite the later arXiv upload; that date is not an error.
- **Fix:** **“O Stable Diffusion associou identidades estigmatizadas a determinados tons de pele; o SDXL produziu tons mais escuros e menor variação de tons de pele nas comparações do estudo.”** Make the EN row equally specific. Retain the Scientific Reports study as the support for facial homogenization.

### M09 — Suno's free-download statement is false under the September 2026 rules

- **Files / location:** `content/en/video-repurposing.md:252`; `content/pt-BR/video-repurposing.md:252`, music table.
- **Problem:** “Free has no downloads” / “O gratuito não permite download” is categorical and outdated. - **Evidence, retrieved 2026-10-21:** [Suno: How do downloads work?](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13876865), updated 3 September 2026, allows trial downloads for some free users and purchase of downloads; it explicitly withholds commercial rights from **trial** downloads. Para divulgar o congresso, use uma licença comercial válida; confirme as condições do download e da faixa. Sem orçamento, prefira voz sem música ou uma faixa da Coleção de Sons da Meta liberada para o uso pretendido.”** Add the current download-policy link. Do not imply that a downloadable file is licensed for promotion.
