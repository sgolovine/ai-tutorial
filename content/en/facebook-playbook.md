**What you'll get from this page:** a practical, zero-budget plan for using a Facebook Page to fill an online academic congress. You will learn how Facebook ranks content today (in plain language), how to create or fix the Page step by step, which post formats to try, a realistic weekly rhythm for a small volunteer team, Brazil-specific timing, and copy-paste AI prompts. Everything uses **CONAFROEDU 2026** (5–6 December, 100% online) as the example.

> [!IMPORTANT] Where CONAFROEDU stands today (21 October 2026)
> The official public presence we could verify is the [Even3 event page](https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/) and the Instagram account it links to, **@congressosnacionais_online** (a broader account for the organizers' congresses, not a CONAFROEDU-only profile). **We could not find an official CONAFROEDU Facebook Page or Facebook Event.** So this guide includes setting both up. If the committee already has a Page, use the "optimize" checklist instead of creating a new one.

## The short version

1. **One Page, one Event, one registration link.** The Page is your public home; the Facebook Event is the stable listing; [Even3](https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/) is where people actually register and submit papers.
2. **Post useful education content with real people**, not a poster every day. Meta's 2026 guidance favors original content made by the Page itself ([Meta, March 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/)).
3. **Three main posts weekly, optional fourth**, is our full-plan starting point (about **12–17 combined person-hours/week**, estimated). Answering comments matters more than posting more.
4. **Respect the two clocks:** paper submissions close **4 November**; registrations run until **6 December**. Your posts must say which one they are about.
5. **Measure registrations, not likes.** Use tagged links and Even3's own reports (see [Measuring results](measuring-results.md)).

Cost of everything on this page: **R$ 0**. Facebook Pages, Events and Meta Business Suite are free to use (the Meta Business Suite app is listed as free in the [Brazilian App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/meta-business-suite/id514643583)). Meta now sells an optional subscription called Meta One with extra features, but Meta says the core experience stays free ([Meta One announcement, September 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai/)). You don't need it.

## How Facebook decides what people see (2026, in plain language)

There is **no public "algorithm formula"**, no guaranteed reach percentage and no proven "first-hour" rule. Meta describes several systems (Feed, Reels, Stories, Groups) that, simplified, work like this:

1. **Collect candidates** — posts from Pages and people you follow, plus content Facebook might recommend.
2. **Look at signals** — your past interactions with that Page, reactions, comments, shares, how long people watch.
3. **Predict** — how likely is *this* person to find *this* post worthwhile?
4. **Rank and filter** — apply quality and integrity rules, then learn from feedback such as "Not interested".

Recent changes that matter for an education Page:

| When | What Meta announced | What you should do |
|---|---|---|
| April 2025 | Crackdown on spam: excessive hashtags, captions unrelated to the content and fake engagement can lose reach ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/cracking-down-spammy-content-facebook/)) | Write captions about the actual content; use few hashtags; never use engagement schemes |
| June 2025 | New Facebook videos are published as Reels, without the old length/format split ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/)) | Ignore old "90-second maximum" advice; check what your uploader allows |
| October 2025 | Reels recommendations learn interests faster; saves and "Not interested" feed back in. Videos over one minute made up over half of Facebook watch time in Meta's described context ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/finding-sharing-reels-facebook-just-got-easier-more-fun/)) | Shorter isn't always better; get to the point fast and give real substance |
| December 2025 | Multi-photo posts show in a standard grid ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/12/making-it-easier-to-create-discover-and-share-content-on-facebook/)) | Each image in a set must make sense on its own; the caption carries the argument |
| March 2026 | Original content made by the Page/creator is favored over copies and minimal edits ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/)) | Record your own speakers and organizers; don't repost viral clips |
| 13 October 2026 | "Your algorithm": users can pick topics and give natural-language feedback ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/introducing-your-algorithm-facebook/)) | Make your subject obvious (anti-racist education, ERER). Availability in Brazil was not confirmed at the time of writing |

**What "original" means for CONAFROEDU:** a teacher showing a classroom activity on Afro-Brazilian history; a confirmed speaker answering a real question from educators; an organizer explaining how to take part online; participants sharing their own takeaways (with permission). Not original: downloaded viral videos, lightly edited memes, generic stock footage, AI-generated "crowds".

> [!NOTE]
> Rules about what Facebook *recommends* to non-followers are stricter than rules about what may stay online ([Meta's Recommendation Guidelines](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/08/recommendation-guidelines/)). Being allowed doesn't guarantee reach.

## Step 1 — Decide which Page will speak for CONAFROEDU

Before creating anything, the committee should answer one question: **Do the organizers already run a Facebook Page** (for example, one matching the broader Instagram account @congressosnacionais_online, or for related congresses)?

| Option | Pros | Cons | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use an existing organizer Page** and create the CONAFROEDU Event under it | Existing followers and history; more likely to meet Live requirements (see below) | Name is less specific | A real, active organizer Page exists |
| **Create a new "CONAFROEDU" Page** | Clear, searchable name; dedicated to the theme | Starts at zero followers with 6 weeks left | No suitable Page exists, or the committee wants a lasting CONAFROEDU brand for future editions |

> [!WARNING]
> Never create fake profiles or a "personal profile" named after the congress to run things. Pages are managed by real people's accounts. Fake accounts break Meta's rules and can get everything removed.

## Step 2 — Create the Page (if you need one)

Facebook's menus change and differ between phone and computer, so treat button names as "look for something like this". On a computer it is usually easier.

1. **Log in with a real organizer's personal Facebook account** — ideally an account that has existed for a long time (this matters for Live, below).
2. Open the menu and look for **Pages → Create new Page** (pt-BR: *Páginas → Criar nova Página*).
3. **Name:** `CONAFROEDU — Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação`. Use the full name so people find it by searching either term.
4. **Category:** choose the closest education-related category the menu offers (for example, something like "Educational organization" or "Education").
5. **Bio / short description** — paste and adjust (in Portuguese, because that's your audience):

   ```text
   Congresso Nacional de Africanidades na Educação. Desenvolvimento, Participação e Educação Antirracista. 1ª edição: 5 e 6 de dezembro de 2026, 100% on-line. Inscrições na Even3.
   ```

6. **Profile picture:** the logo or a simple wordmark that is readable when tiny (it appears as a small circle).
7. **Cover image:** name, dates and "100% on-line". Keep it simple and **repeat every important fact in text** — many people never read text inside images, and screen readers can't.
8. **Contact details:**
   - Website: the Even3 link, tagged so you can count clicks from the Page (see [UTM links](#links-protect-the-path-to-even3) below).
   - E-mail: `conapsuporte@gmail.com`.
   - WhatsApp/phone: `(19) 98816-7751` (the committee's published contact — confirm who answers it).
   - Location: **leave the address empty**. This is an online national congress; a city could make people think there is a venue.
9. **Action button:** if offered, choose the button that sends people to registration (labels vary — something like "Sign up" or "Learn more" pointing to Even3) or one that opens WhatsApp. Pick **one** main action.
10. **Username (@):** short and consistent with other channels, e.g. `@conafroedu` if available.
11. **Publish**, then go to the Page settings and look for **Page access** (pt-BR: *Acesso à Página*).

### Give every teammate their own access

- Add **at least two trusted organizers with full control**, so the Page never depends on one phone.
- Give others **task access** (for example, content or messages only). Meta describes these access levels in its [Page access help](https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410/).
- **Never share one password.** Each person uses their own account, with two-factor authentication turned on.

## Step 3 — Optimize the Page (new or existing)

Use this checklist even if the Page already exists.

- [ ] Name, bio and category say what CONAFROEDU is, in plain Portuguese.
- [ ] Dates, "100% on-line" and Brasília time appear in text, not only in images.
- [ ] Website points to the Even3 page (tagged link).
- [ ] Contact e-mail and WhatsApp are correct, with service hours ("Respondemos de segunda a sexta, das 9h às 18h, horário de Brasília" — the committee sets the real hours).
- [ ] No physical address.
- [ ] One action button, tested on a phone.
- [ ] A **pinned (featured) post** with the essentials: what, when, for whom, how to register, how to submit (while open), contact. Update it when the phase changes (e.g., after 4 November).
- [ ] The Facebook Event is created and shared on the Page (see [Facebook Events & Groups](facebook-events-groups.md)).
- [ ] Instagram is connected and Meta Business Suite shows both accounts (see [Instagram & WhatsApp](instagram-whatsapp.md)).
- [ ] At least two admins; nobody shares passwords.
- [ ] You opened the Page **as a visitor on a phone** and every link works.

### Get the first followers honestly

- Each organizer invites friends and colleagues who genuinely care about education to follow the Page (Facebook usually offers an "invite friends" option on Pages you manage).
- Ask confirmed speakers and partner NEABIs/NEABs to follow and share the pinned post.
- Put the Page link in e-mail signatures, the Even3 page and WhatsApp messages.
- **Never buy followers, likes or comments.** Purchased engagement is exactly what Meta's anti-spam work targets, and it ruins your statistics.

## What to post: formats that work for an academic congress

Format performance differences on Facebook are smaller than people think. In Buffer's 2026 analysis of posts published through its tool, median engagement rates were **5.20% for images, 4.84% for video, 4.76% for text and 4.43% for link posts** ([Buffer, 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)). That's a reason to **mix formats and test**, not to bet everything on one.

| Format | First experiment to try | CONAFROEDU example | What to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original Reel | 20–45 seconds, one question + one answer | "Como trabalhar africanidades para além do mês de novembro?" answered by a confirmed speaker | Watch time, shares, clicks to Even3 |
| Longer video | 2–5 minutes when a topic needs it | A speaker walks through a real teaching decision related to Lei 10.639/2003 | Average watch time, questions asked |
| Real photo + caption | One person, one story, one useful point | A teacher says why the congress theme matters in their school | Meaningful comments, registrations |
| Multi-photo post | 4–6 readable images | "4 perguntas para revisar seu resumo expandido antes de submeter" | Shares, saves, clicks |
| Plain text | One focused question | "Que dificuldade aparece quando você planeja uma prática de ERER?" | Substantive replies |
| Story | 2–4 frames | Question → speaker answer → "Inscreva-se" | Replies, link taps |
| Live | 15–25 rehearsed minutes | Two speakers answer questions collected in advance | Live viewers, replay views, registrations |
| Direct link post | Clear benefit + real deadline | "O valor da inscrição muda em 16 de novembro" | Completed registrations |

The lengths above are **starting experiments**, not Meta rules.

### Reels checklist

- [ ] Essential visual information (screen steps, slide text, animation) is narrated or has an equivalent description; test without looking at the screen.
- [ ] Real speaker or teacher, recorded **with written permission**; keep the clean original file.
- [ ] Start with the educator's problem, not a logo animation.
- [ ] Give one useful example **before** the invitation.
- [ ] Clear audio, good light, **corrected Portuguese captions** (automatic captions get names and terms like "ERER" wrong).
- [ ] Keep text away from the edges where app buttons cover it; preview on a phone.
- [ ] Check names, titles, dates and the link.
- [ ] Use music you have the right to use — or none.
- [ ] Upload the original file; don't re-upload a watermarked download.

For turning talks into clips, see [Reels, clips, captions & dubbing](video-repurposing.md). For animated countdowns, see [Motion graphics with Claude](motion-graphics-claude.md).

### Facebook Live: check eligibility now, not on 4 December

StreamYard's help center documents Meta's requirements for going live on a Page through its tool: the person's **Facebook account must be at least 60 days old**, the **Page must have at least 100 followers**, and the person needs **appropriate Page access** ([StreamYard: Connect a Facebook Page](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043291412-Connect-a-Facebook-Page)). Your specific Page's eligibility must be tested in Facebook itself.

What that means in practice:

- A personal account created today **cannot** be 60 days old by 5 December. Use an established organizer's account.
- A brand-new Page needs 100 real followers before you can count on Live.
- **Fallback:** stream on YouTube (which has its own channel verification steps — [YouTube help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2474026)) and share the watch link on Facebook. Then publish short, authorized clips natively.
- Since February 2025, **new Facebook Live recordings are kept for 30 days** and then removed automatically ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/updating-our-facebook-live-video-storage-policy/)). Download every recording immediately; Facebook is not an archive.
- Multistreaming to Facebook and YouTube at once with StreamYard requires a **paid plan** (a short trial is not a free plan) — [StreamYard help](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045622851-How-to-Multi-stream).

> [!TIP]
> One rehearsed 20-minute "Conversa de aquecimento" with two confirmed speakers in mid-November can feed a week of content: the Live itself, 2–3 clips, a quote card and a WhatsApp summary.

## Links: protect the path to Even3

Nobody has proven that Facebook penalizes every external link, or that "link in the first comment" works better in Brazil. In late 2025 Meta tested limiting link posts for some Pages without a paid subscription; whether this affects Brazilian Pages was not confirmed at the time of writing. Practical approach:

1. Keep the Even3 link in the **Page details, action button, pinned post and Event**.
2. Put a direct link in posts whose job is registration or submission.
3. Make educational posts useful **without** requiring a click.
4. If Facebook shows your Page a link-restriction notice, write down the exact message and adapt — don't use spam accounts or disguised links.
5. Use **tagged links** (UTM parameters) to distinguish visit sources; attribute registrations only after the event panel validates that connection:

```text
https://www.even3.com.br/iconafroedu-764690/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=conafroedu_2026_inscricoes&utm_content=reel_professora_01
```

Use `conafroedu_2026_submissoes` for author posts and `conafroedu_2026_inscricoes` for attendee posts. Test each link on a phone before publishing. Details in [Measuring results](measuring-results.md).

## Hashtags: few and relevant

On Facebook, hashtags are clickable topic labels ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2013/06/public-conversations-on-facebook/)); there is no proven reach boost and no verified "best number". Excessive hashtags are flagged as spammy behavior. Our team convention is **zero to three** per post (not a platform maximum):

- General: `#CONAFROEDU #EducaçãoAntirracista #Africanidades`
- Author posts: swap one for `#PesquisaEmEducação` or `#PósGraduação`
- November content that is genuinely about the date: `#ConsciênciaNegra`

Don't paste all 12 hashtags from the announcement into every post.

## How often and when to post (Brasília time)

### A realistic weekly rhythm

For the full plan, budget about **12–17 combined person-hours/week**, including review and partnerships (estimates, not benchmarks):

- **3–4 Facebook posts**, including 1–2 original Reels and one useful image, multi-photo or text post.
- Adapt the best 2–3 pieces for Instagram.
- Stories on 3–5 days when there's something real to show.
- **One** useful update inside the Facebook Event.
- One or two WhatsApp Channel updates.
- **Two daily 15-minute passes** for comments and messages, plus a 30-minute weekly review.

Nothing proves daily posting is necessary. Cut any format that eats time without producing conversations or registrations.

**If only 5–7 combined hours/week are available:** protect 3.5 hours for two daily 15-minute inbox passes, 30 minutes for weekly review/FAQ, about 1–2 hours for two simple approved Facebook pieces (at most one easy phone video), and 30–60 minutes for a few partner contacts and adapting one piece. Reuse that piece for the Event/Channel; skip new Group/channel setup, elaborate video and agents. Deadline/event days need extra staffed cover or a published reduced service window. Two people in a 90-minute session use three person-hours.

### When to post: test, don't guess

Global studies disagree: Sprout Social sees broad strength on Tuesday/Wednesday from noon to 8 p.m. ([Sprout, 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-facebook/)); Buffer singles out Thursday 9 a.m. and Wednesdays ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-facebook/)); Hootsuite suggests 1–2 posts a day as a starting point ([Hootsuite, 2026](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-posting-schedule/)). None of them studied Brazilian teachers. Use them as hypotheses:

| Test slot (BRT) | Why try it |
|---|---|
| Tue/Thu, 07h–09h | Before classes; morning strength in global data |
| Tue/Wed, 12h–14h | Lunch break; overlaps Sprout's window |
| Wed/Thu, 18h30–20h30 | After school shifts (our hypothesis for educators) |

Rotate similar posts across slots, check results after 48 hours and 7 days, and don't declare a winner after two posts.

> [!WARNING] Time zones
> Brazil has more than one time zone. In every deadline post, write the date, time **and** "horário de Brasília", e.g. "até 4 de novembro, às [horário confirmado pela comissão], horário de Brasília". Never write just "meia-noite". In scheduling tools, choose `America/Sao_Paulo` ([Observatório Nacional on time zones](https://www.gov.br/observatorio/pt-br/assuntos/areas-de-atuacao/tempo-e-frequencia/fusos-horarios)).

## Engagement: do and don't

Meta has demoted "engagement bait" for years, including in Portuguese ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/)). Real questions are fine; manipulation is not.

| Don't | Do instead |
|---|---|
| "Comente SIM se você valoriza a educação!" | Ask a real question: "Que material você usa para trabalhar a Lei 10.639 em sala?" |
| "Marque 5 professores para concorrer" | Offer something useful people *want* to share (a checklist, a resource) |
| Buy likes, join engagement pods, fake comments | Ask real speakers and participants for voluntary, honest posts |
| Paste the same link in dozens of groups | Moderator-approved, relevant participation (see [Groups](facebook-events-groups.md)) |
| "Últimas vagas!" for an online event with no cap | State real dates: "O valor muda em 16 de novembro" |
| AI-written testimonials or credentials | Approved bios and real, authorized quotes |
| AI images of a "packed previous edition" | This is the **1st edition** — use real photos of real people, or clearly labeled illustrations |
| An auto-reply pretending to be a person | Say it's automatic and offer human follow-up |

**Reply to every genuine comment**, ideally within a day. Hide or delete spam and racist abuse; decide in advance who moderates and how. Do **not** block words like "negro", "racismo" or "antirracista" in moderation filters — legitimate discussion uses them.

> [!CAUTION] AI images and representation
> If you use AI-generated images of people, follow [Representing Black & African cultures respectfully](representation-bias.md), and disclose AI use. Meta labels some AI-generated media and asks for disclosure of realistic synthetic content ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)). Prefer real photos of real participants, with consent.

## Using AI in your Facebook workflow

AI is great for **drafting, adapting and checking** — people must **verify and approve**. The single best habit: keep a **fact sheet** (ficha oficial) and paste it into every prompt.

Use the master [FICHA OFICIAL](prompting-basics.md), maintained by Coordination with version, verifier, source and next update. This guide does not define a second fact-sheet master.

### Prompt: three Facebook captions from approved facts

```prompt
You are a copywriter for an online academic congress in Brazil. Write in natural Brazilian Portuguese.
Audience: [e.g., basic-education teachers]. Goal of this post: [one goal].
Tone: clear, warm and professional, educator to educator.

Use ONLY the APPROVED FACTS below. Do not invent benefits, statistics, testimonials, certificates, institutional support or scarcity. If something essential is missing, write [CONFIRMAR] outside the publishable text.

APPROVED FACTS:
[paste the fact sheet]

Write 3 captions in pt-BR with different openings:
1) a concrete classroom problem;
2) a genuine question;
3) a documented benefit of the session.
Each 80–130 words, with one single call to action. Use at most 3 relevant hashtags.
Do not ask for likes, tags or shares as a condition.
Then list which facts you used and what needs human review.
```

> [!NOTE]
> The team posts in Portuguese, so this English prompt asks for pt-BR output. The Portuguese version of this page has the same prompt fully in Portuguese.

### Prompt: final check before scheduling

```prompt
Compare the post below with the official fact sheet. Do not publish anything; only review.
Check names, titles, dates, time zone, prices, deadlines, certificate wording, link, image permission and natural pt-BR.
Flag: claims without a source, invented urgency, engagement bait, personal data, and essential information that only appears inside the image.
Output: "APPROVE AS DRAFT" or "FIX", with objective reasons.
Fact sheet: [paste]. Post: [paste].
```

More prompts: [Copywriting with AI](copywriting.md) and the [Prompt library](prompt-library.md). To schedule approved posts for free, see [Scheduling tools](scheduling-tools.md).

## Measure what matters

In Meta Business Suite, Insights shows a 28-day overview with **Views, Interactions and Follows** ([Meta help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/4185855074991336)). Remember: **views are not unique people** — replays count. Each week, compare:

- tagged clicks and **confirmed registrations in Even3** (the real outcome);
- which topics produced real questions;
- team hours spent.

Then choose just two changes for next week. Full method in [Measuring results](measuring-results.md).
